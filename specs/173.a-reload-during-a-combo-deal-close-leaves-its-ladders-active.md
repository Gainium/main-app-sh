# 173 — A reload during a combo deal close leaves the deal's ladders active

When a combo deal closes, `afterDealClose` closes each of the deal's minigrids
(grid ladders). It looks them up in the in-memory minigrid maps. A bot reload
(`reloadBot` → `start` → `clearClassProperties`) resets those maps
(`minigrids`, `minigridDealMap`), and `loadMinigrids` refills them only for
the deals still held. A close that is already in flight when a reload starts
carries on with its own reference to the deal and reaches `afterDealClose`
while the maps are empty. Or it reaches it after they have been refilled
without the deal. Either way the lookup returns nothing. Nothing is closed and
nothing is logged, and the ladders stay `active`/`range` in the database. They
are never loaded again, because every later load reads minigrids only for
open deals.

## §1 Definition

### §1.1 Expected

- **§1.1.1** When `afterDealClose` finds none of the deal's minigrids in
  memory, every minigrid of that deal that is not `closed` in the database is
  set to `closed` there. The write is filtered by `botId` and `dealId`.
- **§1.1.2** When the deal's minigrids are in memory, they are closed as
  today (`processCloseMinigrid` + `deleteMinigrid`), and no extra database
  sweep runs.
- **§1.1.3** Nothing is written when the bot may not proceed
  (`shouldProceed()`). This is the same gate `saveMinigrid` applies.

### §1.2 Actual

- **§1.2.1** `afterDealClose` iterates `getMinigridByDealId({ dealId })` only.
  After a reload's `clearClassProperties` that is empty, so the deal's ladders
  keep their open status.

## §2 Evidence

On one Bybit-linear SHORT combo bot that reloads every few minutes (its
settings are updated over the API), 220 minigrids were not `closed`. They
belonged to 56 deals, and 46 of those deals were already closed or cancelled.
None of those ladders had been updated after its deal's `closeTime`, and the
log has no `Close minigrid <id>` line for any of them. 39 of these closes fell
inside the 17 h window of `Reload bot` lines we have. Every one of them landed
0.1–5 s after a `Reload bot` line. Over the same window, 62 other closes near
a reload and 305 closes away from one left no ladder open. One traced case:
`Reload bot` at T, `Сlose deal X` at T+18 ms (`loadingComplete` is still true
until `clearClassProperties` runs, after `start` awaits the bot lock write),
the close completes at T+150 ms, and `Start finding minigrids` only comes at
T+7 s. Fleet-wide over 3 d: 47 closed deals with open ladders, 46 of them on
that bot.

No resting order of those deals remained `NEW`/`PARTIALLY_FILLED` in the
database. The reload's own order cancel/reconcile covers the book, so the
leftover is database state. The ladders do not place orders after the close.

## §3 Assumption

The empty lookup comes from the reload's map reset, not from the close path
itself. A close with no reload nearby always closed its ladders. The fix
therefore belongs where the lookup fails, which is `afterDealClose`. The
race in `reloadBot` is not changed. Reworking reload/close serialisation would
change how deals close, and that is out of scope.
