# 283 — Orders Binance refuses on its price band are re-sent on every reload, never when the price comes near, and the user is never told

Bug spec (Definition / Evidence / Assumption). Code: `src/bot/main.ts`
(`noteOutOfBandRefusal`, `holdOutOfBand`, the `sendOrderToExchange` write-off),
`src/bot/outOfBandRetry.ts`, `src/bot/dcaHelper.ts`
(`noteTpCoverageAfterPlacement`, `replaceVenueCanceled`). Builds on specs 250
(hold out-of-band orders), 251 (Binance re-price of passed levels) and 264
(known band held locally across reloads).

## 1. Definition

§1.1 A DCA/Combo safety order, a combo grid level or a DCA/Combo deal take
profit that Binance refuses with `Filter failure: PERCENT_PRICE_BY_SIDE`
(or `PERCENT_PRICE`) while it is still AHEAD of the price (a sell above it, a
buy below it) is kept by level and placed once a price tick brings it within
the band. Its price is never changed. Orders the price already ran through
keep the spec 251/254 re-price.

§1.2 The band: Binance's refusal names no limit. It is taken from the pair's
price filter when the connector's exchange info carries one
(`priceMultiplier`: `up` caps a sell, `down` a buy), else a default of 20 %
with a warning in the bot log; in both cases capped by the distance that was
just refused. It is per side, kept in Redis like spec 264's band, so a reload
holds the order without asking the venue.

§1.3 The user is told once: one `Order waiting for price` warning per held
safety/grid level; for a take profit one `Position left open` warning per
deal saying the deal has no take profit on the exchange, why, and that it will
be placed when the price is within range. Never a bot error, never per retry
or per reload, nothing for a bot that is not open (spec 250 places nothing for
it either).

§1.4 A held take profit is placed by re-planning the deal's take profit
(`getTPOrder` + `placeOrders`, as the missing-TP re-send does), only while the
deal is open, not closing and has no take profit resting. While it is held the
missing-TP re-send ladder does not run for that deal.

§1.5 A missing take profit whose five re-sends are used up stays given up for
a day across worker restarts; a take profit that rests, or a held one the
price comes near, clears it.

## 2. Evidence

- Binance spot hedge DCA, BEBUSDT, price ~151: TP SELL 232.93 and safety
  orders 180–211 (sell side) / 26–108 (buy side) refused
  `Filter failure: PERCENT_PRICE_BY_SIDE` 146 times in 48 h, in bursts at each
  worker restart (1, 2, 5, 15 min apart, then quiet); no bot message. The
  GraphQL order list shows the LONG deal with one resting order (a BUY at
  131.15) and no take profit. Binance's BEBUSDT filter:
  `askMultiplierUp 1.2, bidMultiplierDown 0.8`.
- Binance spot DCA SHORT METUSDT: TP BUY 0.0858 with the price ~0.227
  (`bidMultiplierDown 0.5`), re-sent in the same bursts since it went missing.
- Code: the write-off calls `noteOutOfBandRefusal` only for subType
  `Order price`; Binance's wording classifies `Order params`. Even when called,
  `bandFractionFromReason` returns null for it, and `replacesVenueCancel`
  excludes take profits. `handleErrors` returns early on `PERCENT_PRICE`, so
  nothing reaches the user. `missingTpRearm` lives in memory only.
- Harness (`outOfBandRetry.harness.spec.ts`, spec 283 block) before the fix:
  the refused order is not held (`outOfBandOrders` undefined), the venue is
  asked again on the reload pass, no notice.

## 3. Assumption (root cause)

Binance band refusals never enter the spec 250/264 hold: they are matched by
subType (`Order price`) and by a band named in the text, neither of which
Binance's refusal has, and take profits are not opted in. Hence every reload
re-sends, nothing places them when the price comes near, and the only report
is dropped in `handleErrors`.

## Invariants

- `deals.binance-band-refused-tp-not-held` (main-app

  Binance refused on its price band in the last 12 h has neither a resting take
  profit nor a held one in `heldOrders`.
