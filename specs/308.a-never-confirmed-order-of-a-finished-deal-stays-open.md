# 308 — A never-confirmed order of a finished deal stays open

An order row whose placement answer was never recorded keeps the `'-1'`
placeholder for its exchange id. Specs 109, 177, 184 and 306 settle such a row
by asking the venue once and retiring it on a definitive not-found. Every one
of those paths reaches a row only through its deal:

- `checkOrders` groups the loaded orders by `dealId` and looks the deal up in
  memory. The take-profit / stop-loss lookup runs only when the deal is held,
  and the regular-order lookup only when the deal is held and not closed or
  canceled.
- A bot load reads every open row of the bot from Mongo, so the row IS loaded.
  But a closed or canceled deal is not held, so the row is skipped on every
  load.
- The reconnect pass (spec 306) and the fill failsafe work from the same
  per-deal view.

A row that outlived its deal (the deal closed or was canceled while the
placement answer was lost, or the deal record is gone) is therefore never asked
about again. It stays `NEW` / `PARTIALLY_FILLED` in the database for good.
Either the venue never had it, so the row is wrong state forever, or the venue
does hold it, so a live order or a fill exists that nothing records. Nothing
raises an error.

## §1 Definition

### §1.1 Expected

- **§1.1.1** On every DCA and Combo bot load, the bot reads (bounded, oldest
  first) its rows with `orderId: '-1'`, status `NEW` / `PARTIALLY_FILLED` and a
  `dealId`, past the never-placed age floor (spec 177 §1.2,
  `isRetirableNeverPlacedOrder`).
- **§1.1.2** Of those, it acts only on rows whose deal is finished (`closed`
  or `canceled`) or has no record, and is not held by this process. A held or
  open deal is left to the existing paths.
- **§1.1.3** Each such row is asked about once through the normal reconcile
  lookup (`getOrderForReconcile`: client id, placement-time `since`, pacing,
  bounded retry).
- **§1.1.4** A definitive not-found retires it through `retireNeverPlacedOrder`
  (`CANCELED` row, out of the order map).
- **§1.1.5** An answer that holds the order records it: the row takes the
  venue's exchange id, status and figures, and a warning names it. It is NOT
  booked into the finished deal (no `processFilledOrder`).
- **§1.1.6** Anything else (deferred, timeout, unknown error, the bot's own
  "no exchange order id" answer) changes nothing. The next load asks again.
- **§1.1.7** Nothing is ever placed or re-placed from here, for any deal.
- **§1.1.8** The sweep runs in the background and never delays the bot's
  start. A failed deal read judges nothing.

### §1.2 Actual

§1.1.1–§1.1.6 did not hold: a `'-1'` row of a finished or missing deal was
loaded and skipped on every bot load, and stayed open indefinitely.

## §2 Evidence

- Prod (read-only, 2026-11-28): three resting LIMIT `'-1'` rows older than a
  day on finished or missing deals, on three venues (a DCA take-profit on a
  closed deal, a combo grid order on a closed deal, a DCA base order on a
  canceled deal).
- None of their client ids appears in any retained log line, across many
  restarts of their bots: no path had asked the venue about them.
- In code: `checkOrders`' `deal &&` gates (dcaHelper), with `loadOrders`'
  query `{ status: { $nin: [CANCELED, EXPIRED, FILLED] } }` loading the rows.

## §3 Assumption

A `'-1'` row of a finished deal has no reason to stay open. One lookup per bot
load (at most `BOT_FINISHED_DEAL_UNPLACED_SWEEP_LIMIT`, default 20) settles it
for good on a definitive answer. The query is on the indexed `botId`, at the
cost of the existing load read. A found order is recorded rather than booked,
because the deal is already settled and re-opening its numbers is out of
scope. Coinbase lookups only see open orders, so an order that filled there is
retired `CANCELED`; that is spec 184's existing reading of a Coinbase
not-found.

## §4 Tests

`src/bot/finishedDealUnplacedOrderSweep.spec.ts`:

- §4.1 closed / canceled / missing deal: exactly one lookup by client id with
  `since`, retired `CANCELED`, nothing placed or booked (§1.1.1–§1.1.4,
  §1.1.7). The same when the row is already in the map.
- §4.2 open or held deal, a row under the age floor, a row with an exchange id:
  no lookup, no write (§1.1.2).
- §4.3 the venue holds the order: recorded with its exchange id and status, not
  booked, nothing placed (§1.1.5).
- §4.4 an ambiguous answer or a failed deal read writes nothing (§1.1.6,
  §1.1.8).

## Invariants

- `184.unconfirmed-order-on-finished-deal` (main-app pack 184, observe): no
  resting LIMIT order holds `orderId: '-1'` with status `NEW` /
  `PARTIALLY_FILLED` more than 24h after creation while its deal is closed,
  canceled or missing. After this fix, such a row clears on its bot's next
  load. One that persists means the sweep is not reached.
