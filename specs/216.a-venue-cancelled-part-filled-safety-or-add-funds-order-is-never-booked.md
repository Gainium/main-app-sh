# 216 — A safety or add-funds order the venue cancels part filled is never booked

Bug spec (Definition / Evidence / Assumption). Tests:
`src/bot/dca/canceledRegularFill.spec.ts` (predicate),
`src/bot/dca/canceledRegularFill.harness.spec.ts` (real `dcaHelper`).

## §1 Definition

A DCA deal buys through `dealRegular` orders: its safety orders, and the
orders "add funds" places (`addFundsId` set, recorded in
`deal.pendingAddFunds` until settled). Both are resting LIMIT orders, so the
venue — or the account owner on the venue — can cancel one after part of it
traded. The part that traded is a position the account holds.

**Expected.**

- §1.1 When a `dealRegular` order of an OPEN deal arrives CANCELED with
  `0 < executed < ordered`, a usable `updateTime`, and the bot did not ask for
  the cancel, its executed part is booked into the deal exactly once, through
  the same `updateDeal` branch a fill of that order would take:
  - §1.1.1 an add-funds order: into `deal.funds` at its price and quantity, its
    `pendingAddFunds` entry removed (so a reload does not re-send it, spec 110);
  - §1.1.2 a safety order: into the deal's balances like a filled safety order.
- §1.2 The deal's average price counts that executed part, so the average, the
  size derived from it (`updateUsage`) and the take profit sized from them cover
  every coin the deal bought.
- §1.3 Unchanged — nothing is booked by this path for:
  - §1.3.1 a cancel with nothing executed, or with everything executed (the
    FILLED path's);
  - §1.3.2 a quantity without a usable `updateTime` (`<= 0`): REST-written
    cancel rows carry a bogus `executedQty` with `updateTime: -1` — the same
    trust rule the take-profit branch applies;
  - §1.3.3 a cancel the bot itself asked for (`isOwnCancel`): the engine's
    cancel path owns those (it promotes a part-filled cancel to FILLED, spec
    156 §3);
  - §1.3.4 an order already processed as a fill (`processedFilled`);
  - §1.3.5 a deal that is not open — `updateDeal` would otherwise sell the
    quantity at market (`sellRemainder`);
  - §1.3.6 take-profit and base-order cancels, which keep their own handling.

**Actual.** `processCanceledOrder` returns for every `typeOrder` other than
`dealStart` and `dealTP`. A part-filled `dealRegular` cancel is dropped: the
deal never books the coins, an add-funds entry stays pending for good, and
`getAvgPrice` (FILLED rows plus part-filled `dealStart` only) leaves the row
out of the average even where the balance ledger rebuild already counts it.

## §2 Evidence (prod, read-only, 2026-11-01)

- §2.1 Hyperliquid spot VIRT-USDC long deal, 4 FILLED entries totalling 251.1
  base for 188.974042 USDC. An add-funds order (limit 0.5836, 190 USDC →
  `origQty 325.6`).
- §2.2 Bot log: `Processing msg VIRT-USDC <add-funds id>, CANCELED, 0.5836,
base: 317.1, quote: 185.05955999999998 BUY, <updateTime>`.
  No `Send cancel request` for that id — the cancel was not the bot's.
- §2.3 The order row: `CANCELED, executedQty 317.1, cummulativeQuoteQty
185.05956`, a real `updateTime`. The deal still reads `funds: []`,
  `pendingAddFunds: [the 190 USDC entry]`, `currentBalances.base 251.1`,
  `size 251.1`, `avgPrice 0.75258` — the 317.1 bought are in no deal. The
  position reconciler reports books 251.1 vs fills 568.2.
- §2.4 `dealRegular` CANCELED rows with a venue id and `executedQty > 0` are
  overwhelmingly REST-written rows with `updateTime: -1` (excluded by §1.3.2);
  of the dated part-filled ones on DCA deals, most belong to deals already
  closed (§1.3.5).

## §3 Assumption (root cause — hypothesis)

The early `return` for `typeOrder !== dealTP` in `dcaHelper.processCanceledOrder`
is the only reason the cancel goes unbooked: the stream handler keeps the
CANCELED row with its fills in the order map (`main.ts` `processOrderQueue`),
and `updateDeal` books from `executedQty`, so routing the event there books it.
`getAvgPrice` must admit the row as well or the average stays on the old fills.

A harness driving the real `processCanceledOrder` with the §2.1 state must see
`funds` stay empty and the add-funds entry stay pending — that is the red.

## §4 Out of scope

- §4.1 Repairing deals already left in this state (a separate data fix).
- §4.2 Re-placing the unfilled remainder of a cancelled safety level.
- §4.3 Combo deals (`comboHelper` overrides this callback; spec 047 there).

## Invariants

The reconciler's books-vs-fills check already reports the left-over state
(§2.3). For the add-funds shape an `observe` invariant is added to main-app's
`specs/invariants/deals.invariants.json`: an open deal still listing an
add-funds entry whose order ended CANCELED with a dated partial fill. A
part-filled safety-order cancel leaves no pending marker to join on, so it is
left to the reconciler.
