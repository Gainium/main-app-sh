# 305 — spot LONG and SHORT bots on one account and pair share the coins, unwarned

Bug spec. Spot counterpart of spec `262` (one-way futures opposite-direction
deals), which deliberately leaves spot untouched.

## 1. Definition

### 1.1 Expected

When a user starts or saves a spot DCA or combo bot while another of their
bots on the same exchange account trades the same pair in the opposite
direction (it is running, or it still holds an open deal there), the user is
told, once per pair, in a bot warning: the two bots buy and sell the same coins
from one wallet balance, so either may be unable to close its deal in full;
run opposite directions on separate accounts or sub-accounts to avoid it.

The bot starts and trades exactly as before. Nothing is refused, held,
reserved or resized.

### 1.2 Actual

Nothing is said. A spot exchange account has one balance of the base coin and
the bots keep no reservation of it. The SHORT bot sells coins the LONG deal
counts as its own, and the LONG buys refill what the SHORT owes. The first
sign is a deal whose close the venue refuses for insufficient balance, hours
or days later, with nothing that names the other bot.

## 2. Evidence

Production, one user, one Coinbase account: a running spot LONG DCA bot and a
spot SHORT DCA bot, both on XRP-USDC. The LONG's take-profit sold the coins the
SHORT deal had bought back, and the SHORT's close was then refused every hour
with an insufficient-funds error. Neither bot's form nor its messages
mentioned the other bot.

`refuseNewDealAgainstOpposingPosition` and the start-time opposing-position
checks return at once for `!this.futures` (spec 262: "spot is untouched"), so
no code path ever compares a spot bot with the user's other bots.

Harness `src/bot/opposingSpotBot.spec.ts` drives the real
`warnOpposingSpotBots` of the DCA and the Combo helper. Before the fix every
helper case fails (`warnOpposingSpotBots is not a function`) and the
subType is not per-pair.

## 3. Assumption

A user start and a settings save are the only moments the user is looking at
the bot and can act on the warning (move one bot to another account). A worker
or service restart is not one of them; nobody started anything then, and
warning on every restart would repeat the row for no new information.

A settings save reaches `start()` via `reloadBot(id, …, rebuildIndicators =
true)` from `changeDCABot` / `changeComboBot`, with `restart = true`. That flag
is the save signal.

## 4. Required behaviour

1. A spot DCA/combo bot warns for each of its pairs on which another bot of
   the same user, the same `exchangeUUID`, the same paper/real context, the
   opposite `settings.strategy` and not deleted, either has a running status
   (open, range, error, monitoring) or holds an open deal of the opposite
   strategy.
2. The message names the other direction, the other bot(s), whether each
   holds an open deal, the risk, and that this bot keeps running.
3. A stopped opposite bot with no open deal, a same-direction bot, and a bot
   on another account are not reported.
4. Futures bots are never checked here (spec 262 covers one-way futures).
5. The warning is a per-pair bot message (`DEFAULT_PER_SYMBOL_SUBTYPES`),
   written with `setError = false`: the bot status is not changed.
6. It runs on a user start and on a settings save, not on a service restart
   or an internal reload.
7. Every read is best-effort: a failure is logged as a warning and nothing is
   shown, i.e. today's behaviour.
