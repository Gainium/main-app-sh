# 226 — The next deal's entry self-trades the closed deal's remainder sweep

Bug spec (Definition / Evidence / Assumption).

## 1. Definition

### 1.1 Expected

When a spot LONG DCA deal closes on a take-profit that sold only part of the
position, `closeDeal` market-sells the leftover with a `D-SR` sweep (spec
`157`). Then:

1. **§1.1.1** The bot does not place the next deal's orders while that sweep is
   still working on the venue. `processDealClose` waits until the sweep is
   terminal (FILLED, CANCELED or EXPIRED, as the user stream reports it) before
   it opens the next ASAP deal. The wait is bounded (§3.1). When the bound runs
   out, the next deal opens anyway, as it does today.
2. **§1.1.2** The closed deal counts what the sweep actually sold. Fills the
   stream reports after the close are booked onto the closed deal:
   `currentBalances`, `profit` (`total`, `totalUsd`, `pureBase`, `pureQuote`),
   `commission`, and the bot's own profit. Each fill is booked once, keyed on
   the order's cumulative `executedQty` / `cummulativeQuoteQty`, so repeated or
   out-of-order reports of the same cumulative amount book nothing more.
3. **§1.1.3** If the venue ends the sweep CANCELED or EXPIRED with quantity
   still unsold, and the bot did not ask for that cancel, the bot re-sends ONE
   market sweep for the unsold quantity. It does this before the next deal's
   orders are placed, and only if that quantity clears the venue minimums the
   first sweep was held to. The re-sent sweep is booked and awaited exactly
   like the first, inside the same bound.

### 1.2 Actual

1. **§1.2.1** `processDealCloseHoldingLock` calls `openNewDeal` right after the
   close teardown, with the sweep still OPEN on the venue. The new deal's
   resting LIMIT buy sits at the top of the bid, the sweep walks down into it,
   and the venue cancels the sweep (`cancel_reason: Self trade prevention`).
2. **§1.2.2** `sellRemainder` books only the immediate answer of
   `sendGridToExchange`. Coinbase answers a market order OPEN with nothing
   filled. Every later report of the order then reaches
   `updatePartiallyFilledTP`, which books only on an `open` deal, so it is
   dropped. The closed deal keeps counting the unsold base, and its P&L values
   the coins the sweep did sell as if they were still held.
3. **§1.2.3** Nothing re-sends the cancelled remainder. The coins stay in the
   account and no deal tracks them.

## 3. Assumption (root-cause hypothesis)

The close path assumes the sweep's immediate answer is final. On a venue that
answers a market order asynchronously, it is not. Two independent consequences
follow:

- the next deal is opened while the sweep is still working, so it sits in the
  sweep's way (§1.2.1);
- the sweep's real fills arrive after the deal has left memory and are dropped
  (§1.2.2).

### 3.1 Bound

`remainderSweepWaitMs` is 5 s from the moment `processDealClose` starts
waiting, polled every 100 ms. In the evidence the sweep sat OPEN for about
2.7 s before the new entry was placed. The brief limits the delay: "on a close
with a leftover, the next deal starts at most a few seconds later."

### 3.2 Scope limits

- Only sweeps sent by the close path (`sellNotByOrder`, the take-profit
  remainder) on **spot LONG, quote-profit** deals are tracked. The other
  `sellRemainder` callers (late entry fills, cancelled deals) and futures or
  short deals keep their current booking. Their booking formula is not the
  same one, and the evidence does not cover them.
- Booking is in memory, keyed by client order id. A worker restart between the
  sweep and its fills books nothing for that sweep, which is today's
  behaviour, never a double count.
- `levels.complete` is not changed. The close-path booking adds 0 levels for
  this sweep (`sellNotByOrder`), and a late report must agree with an
  immediate one.

## 4. Not changing

The take-profit itself, the deal-start settings, and every close without a
live sweep, which waits 0 ms.

## Invariants

The existing `forum.closed-spot-long-deal-holds-base` (main-app
`specs/invariants/forum.invariants.json`) already names this state. No new
invariant is added. Its post-deploy hit rate on Coinbase is the measure of
this fix.
