# 307 — A base entry the venue cancelled unfilled is ignored when the bot is in `error`

## 1. Definition

**Expected.** A `start` deal whose base order the exchange cancelled with nothing
filled (not cancelled by the bot) is cancelled about `canceledBaseEntryDelayMs`
after the report, whenever the bot is running: `open`, `error`, `range` or
`monitoring`. A `closed` or `archive` bot does not act on the deal.

**Actual.** The check arms ("Checking the deal in 15s") and then does nothing
when the bot's status is anything but `open`. A refused entry often leaves the
bot showing an error such as "Not enough balance" (`status: error`), so the
deal sits in `start` with nothing on the venue and no message to the user.

## 2. Evidence

- Production DCA-bot log: the deal's entry report is logged with
  "Checking the deal in 15s" and nothing after it; the bot is `status: error`.
- `cancelDealOfCanceledBaseEntry` returns early on
  `this.data?.status !== BotStatusEnum.open`.
- `closeDealById` has no bot-status gate of its own, so that check is the
  only thing blocking the cancel.

## 3. Assumption (hypothesis)

The `open`-only gate is narrower than "the bot is running". Every other running
check in the helper (`processFilledOrder` load queue, opposing-bot lookup)
treats `open | error | range | monitoring` as running.

## 4. Tests

- §4.5 `src/bot/reconnectCanceledBaseEntry.harness.spec.ts`: for the DCA and
  the Combo helpers, `error`/`range`/`monitoring` lead to a cancelled deal, and
  `closed`/`archive` lead to no cancel.
