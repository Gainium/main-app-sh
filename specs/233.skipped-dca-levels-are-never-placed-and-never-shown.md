# 233 — Skipped DCA levels are never placed and never shown

Bug spec (Definition / Evidence / Assumption). Owner: DCA engine
(`src/bot/dcaHelper.ts`, `src/bot/dca/missedLevels.ts`), order stamp in
`src/bot/main.ts`.

## 1. Definition

### 1.1 Expected

A DCA ladder level that was not resting while the market ran past it is a
**skipped level**. This happens when the exchange refused it (balance, margin)
or when it was a smart order that was not placed yet. The gap path then moves
one level to the market and that level fills there. The deal's last fill is
then past the levels after it.

1. The engine records the deal's skipped levels on the deal
   (`deal.missedLevels`: level number, price, size), and tells the user once in
   the deal's bot events. Nothing is recorded when nothing was skipped.
2. The bot never places a skipped level by itself, not even after a later fill
   moves the last fill price back past it. A skipped level stays skipped until
   it fills.
3. The user can execute a skipped level by hand, at the market, from the deal
   (`executeNextDca` with `missedLevel`). The engine checks that the level is
   still skipped at that moment, and refuses if it is not.
4. "Execute next DCA" refuses when the level its count names has already
   filled. A count of fills skips nothing, so on a deal with skipped levels it
   points at a filled level.
5. Shipping this places nothing that was not placed before. Recording only
   removes orders from the slice, and on the first rebuild the levels it
   removes are the same ones the last-fill filter already left out.

### 1.2 Actual

`createCurrentDealOrders` keeps only the levels on the far side of the deal's
last fill (`o.price < price` for a long, `> price` for a short). A skipped level
lies on the near side, so it is dropped on every rebuild. It leaves no order
row, no refusal and no message. The deal shows fewer filled levels than its
ladder has, with nothing resting and nothing saying why. Because the slice is
taken by count (`ordersCount − filled`), the next level it places can be a
deeper and, with a volume scale, larger one.

The engine also tells a fill's level by its price alone (`isLevelFilled`:
`origPrice === level.price`). A level that filled away from its own price
matches no level.

### 1.3 Not changing

- The gap path (spec 164 §1.4): a level the market passed before it rested is
  still moved to the market price once.
- Which levels are placed. This spec adds a record, an exclusion of recorded
  levels, a manual action, and a stamp on new orders.
- Combo deals and indicator-triggered ladders are out of scope.

## 2. Evidence

Recorded live deals (fixtures in `src/bot/dca/missedLevels.fixture.json`,
replayed through the real engine in `missedLevels.harness.spec.ts`):

- A futures long, 18 levels, volume scale 1.2. Level 13 was refused for margin
  for a day, then moved to the market and filled there. The slice then placed
  level 18 (the largest) and dropped 14–17 for good.
- A futures long, 17 levels. Same pattern, with levels 14–15 dropped and 16–17
  filled.
- A spot short, 32 levels. One level filled about 4× past the ladder's deepest
  level, after the market had run through 21 levels while the account held
  none of the base coin. Nothing has been placed since.
- A spot long, 2 levels. Level 1 never rested and level 2 filled. The pair's
  price precision has changed since then, so the rebuilt level 2 no longer
  equals the price its order was sent at.

## 3. Assumption (hypothesis)

The near-side filter is right to leave skipped levels out. Buying them now
would be a market order the user did not plan. What is missing is a record of
them and a way for the user to act on one. Identity has to come from ladder
position, not price, so that a skipped level can be told from a filled one.

### 3.1 Which level a fill filled

In order (`resolveLadderLevels`):
1. the `ladderLevel` the order was stamped with when it was sent (new);
2. an exact price match against the current ladder (stored breakpoints
   applied);
3. the nearest free level within 0.1 %, also through both ends of any stored
   breakpoint the row belongs to;
4. the shallowest free level (the count rule the engine has always used).

Rule 4 can only hide a skipped level, never invent one.

## 4. Invariants

The state this feature must never produce:

- **No unrecorded skip.** An open percentage-ladder deal never has more levels
  at or before its last fill than it has ladder fills plus recorded skipped
  levels. If one does, the user has a level that was neither bought nor offered
  to them.
- **No auto-buy of a skip.** A recorded skipped level never has a resting order
  or a fill that the user did not execute.

Pack: `deals.ladder-level-abandoned` in the main-app deals invariant pack,
starting in `observe` mode, with its own pack test. A user who is out of
funds does not trip it. A refused level the market passes moves no last fill,
and once it fills at the market the levels it passed are recorded.

## 5. Tests

- `src/bot/dca/missedLevels.harness.spec.ts` runs the real engine on the
