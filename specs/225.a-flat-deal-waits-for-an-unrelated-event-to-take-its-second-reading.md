# 225 — A flat deal waits for an unrelated event to take its second "no position" reading

Spec 005 closes a deal whose exchange position is gone, but only after two
answered readings, at least one probe window (5 min) apart, agree. Spec 185
made the first reading last a day. What neither spec does is *take* the second
reading. A probe only runs when something else asks for one: `checkTPLevel`
on a price tick that reaches the take-profit level, the combo grid-refusal
path (spec 175) when the bot re-places its ladders, or the under-minimum
close refusal in `getTPOrder`. If the price moves away from the take profit
after the first reading and the ladders are not re-placed, nothing asks
again. The deal stays open, holding the pair's deal slot, until a worker
reload or a reconnect re-places the ladders. That can take hours.

## §1 Definition

### §1.1 Expected

- **§1.1.1** When a probe records the FIRST answered "position gone" reading
  for a deal, the bot schedules a re-probe of that same deal for itself. It
  fires once both the probe window (`dpp`) and the reading window (`dpg`)
  have passed. The second reading then does not depend on any price, refusal
  or reload event. The re-probe runs the same `reconcileDealPosition` with the
  same settle as the probe that scheduled it. So a combo deal still closes by
  its base trigger and a DCA deal still closes through `closeDeal`.
- **§1.1.2** Unchanged: two agreeing ANSWERED readings, at least one probe
  window apart, before anything is settled. The re-probe is just one more
  probe. It goes through the same throttle and the same readings count, and it
  cannot settle on a single empty answer.
- **§1.1.3** One re-probe at a time per deal: a later first reading replaces
  the pending timer and does not add a second one.
- **§1.1.4** The re-probe is dropped, without asking the venue, when the bot
  has stopped or the deal is no longer open or is already closing. It is also
  cancelled when an answered probe sees the position again, and when the deal
  is settled.
- **§1.1.5** If the re-probe itself goes unanswered, it is rescheduled after
  the next probe window, at most 3 times in a row. After that it stops, and
  the existing event-driven probes are what is left (the behaviour before
  this spec).

### §1.2 Actual

- **§1.2.1** `reconcileDealPosition` logs "waiting for a second reading before
  closing the deal" and returns. Nothing is scheduled. The second reading
  waits for the next caller.

## §2 Evidence

Production, a combo bitgetUsdm deal (read-only `logs` search of
`Gainium-Combo-bot-out`):

- `2026-11-05T13:18:02Z`, worker PID 2875435: "TP level close refused for
  grid order … (no position to close) and venue holds no BTCUSDT position on
  bitgetUsdm — waiting for a second reading before closing the deal".
- No line for that deal after that until `2026-11-06T03:22:42Z`, worker PID
  4060521 (a new process, i.e. after a reload): "(2 readings) — closing the
  deal instead of re-arming against a position that is gone", then "Close
  deal …: no venue position left for its grid orders".
- That is 14 h between two readings the rule allows 5 min apart. During that
  time the sentinel's `combo.minigrid-ladder-empty-on-open-deal` reported the
  deal (an open deal with an empty minigrid ladder).

## §3 Assumption

The delay is how long the bot went without an event that calls
`reconcileDealPosition`, not anything in the readings rule. A self-scheduled
re-probe after the first reading closes the deal one window later, with no
other event.

## §4 Tests

`src/bot/positionGoneReprobe.spec.ts`, using sinon fake timers over the real
dcaHelper + comboHelper:

- §1.1.1 the first reading via a grid refusal. No further refusal or price event
  follows. After the window the deal closes by the base trigger.
- §1.1.1 the same through the DCA `checkTPLevel` path: the price moves away,
  and the deal closes through `closeDeal`.
- §1.1.2 no re-probe fires before the window. The re-probe finding the
  position resets the count and closes nothing.
- §1.1.3 two first readings arm one timer.
- §1.1.4 a deal that closed in the meantime is not probed.
- §1.1.5 an unanswered re-probe is rescheduled, and gives up after 3 misses.
