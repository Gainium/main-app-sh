# 122 — An add-funds fill in the same tick as a safety-order fill drops out of the deal's cost

Bug spec, landing in existing code (`dcaHelper.updateDeal`, add-funds branch,
and `updateDealBalances`).

## §1 Definition

### §1.1 Expected

- **§1.1.1** When an add-funds order (any `ROA` row, including the rested
  remainder of a part-filled LIMIT base order, spec `111`) fills, the deal's
  ledger counts both legs of it: the base it bought in `currentBalances.base`
  and the quote it paid in `initialBalances.quote - currentBalances.quote`.
  `usage.current`, `cost`, `size` and the unrealized P&L built on them match
  the deal's filled orders. This holds whatever other fills of the same deal
  arrive in the same tick.
- **§1.1.2** No fill is counted twice: a safety-order fill that arrives
  together with an add-funds fill moves the ledger once.
- **§1.1.3** Unchanged: the orders a deal places. The take profit keeps
  sizing off the whole position held.

### §1.2 Actual (before)

- **§1.2.1** The add-funds branch of `updateDeal` books the fill as a delta
  (`initialBalances.quote += quote`, `currentBalances.base += base`), saves,
  and only then, from the save's `.then`, calls `updateDealBalances`. That
  call is outside the per-deal `update` lock. It rebuilds both balances from
  the order book while the next queued fill's `updateDeal` is already running.
- **§1.2.2** If the next fill read `currentBalances` before the rebuild landed
  and saved after it, its save replaces the rebuilt `currentBalances` and
  keeps the rebuilt `initialBalances`. Base is right. The quote paid for the
  addition is missing, because the delta it read had not subtracted it (the
  delta model puts it in `initialBalances` instead). Cost and unrealized P&L
  leave the addition out.
- **§1.2.3** If the rebuild ran first, it counted fills that were in the book
  but not yet booked by their own `updateDeal`. Each of those then added its
  delta again, so the fill was counted twice.

## §2 Evidence

- **§2.1** A production paper deal on a LIMIT-entry bot: base order part
  filled, remainder rested as an add-funds LIMIT (spec `111`). The remainder
  and two safety orders reported FILLED in the same millisecond. The deal's
  stored balances match every filled order, but its stored `usage.current`,
  `cost` and `size` equal exactly the quote of every fill except the
  remainder's. Unrealized P&L read about +47% where the fills give about −2%.
  The bot log shows the remainder's `updateDeal` running first, then the two
  safety orders' in the same tick.
- **§2.2** Harness (`src/bot/dca/addFundsFillLostUpdate.harness.spec.ts`)
  driving the real `updateDeal`, `saveDeal`, `updateUsage` and
  `updateDealBalances`: one fill at a time is exact. The production arrival
  pattern gives `quote spent = 21.698` against `36.3816`, missing exactly the
  remainder's quote (§1.2.2). Three fills back to back with no I/O yield give
  base `632.47` against `527.65`, a safety order counted twice (§1.2.3).

## §3 Assumption (root cause)

- **§3.1** The `updateDealBalances` call from the add-funds branch's save
  callback runs outside the deal's `update` lock, so it interleaves with the
  next fill's read-modify-write of `currentBalances`. It also counts fills
  that are in the book but not yet booked (`ordersInBetweenUpdates`).
- **§3.2** Running the rebuild inside the add-funds branch, before its save
  and while the lock is held, and leaving out fills still in
  `ordersInBetweenUpdates`, removes both interleavings. The rebuilt ledger is
  the one the branch saves, and every later fill applies its delta on top of
  it.

## §4 Out of scope

- **§4.1** Which orders are placed, and take-profit sizing (§1.1.3).
- **§4.2** Repairing deals already written with a short ledger. That is a
  data correction, tracked separately.
