# 282 — A "profit unknown" close booked the deal's fees and funding as its profit

Bug spec (Definition / Evidence / Assumption). Amends spec `277` §1.1. Tests:
`src/bot/dca/profitUnknownCloseBooking.spec.ts` (drives the real `closeDeal`),
`src/bot/dca/dealOutcome.spec.ts` (the event wording). Pack: main-app `specs/277.invariants.json`
(`deals.profit-unknown-books-no-profit`).

## §1 Definition

Spec `277` closes a futures deal whose position was closed outside the bot. When the venue's
fills do not account for the close, the deal is flagged `closedExternally` + `profitUnknown` and
closed through `closeDeal` with no price, the "Close without TP order" branch.

- §1.1 **Expected.** The close of a `profitUnknown` deal has no known result, so it books no
  profit: the deal's stored `profit` is what it had realised before the close (0 for a DCA deal
  without partial take profits). Its fees stay recorded in `commission` and its funding in
  `funding`. Neither is netted into `profit`.
  **Actual.** The branch subtracted the deal's fees from `profit` and then folded its settled
  funding in. The result was stored on the deal as a profit.
- §1.2 **Expected.** The bot's `profit` total and the user profit record (`saveProfitToDb`) take
  from the deal only what §1.1 books. **Actual.** Both took fees and funding as profit.
- §1.3 **Expected.** The deal's "Deal closed" event says its profit is unknown and gives no
  number for the close. It does not contain the word "open": the V2 event taxonomy labels any
  deal line holding it as an opening. **Actual.** `Deal closed, id: …, profit: <−fees>$`, written
  before the funding fold, so it agreed with neither the record nor the result.
- §1.4 **Unchanged.** Profit realised before the close (combo grid levels) is booked as before.
- §1.5 **Unchanged.** A no-order close of a deal without `profitUnknown` (a combo whose base
  minigrid completed, a spot close, any close that is not spec `277`'s) books fees and funding
  exactly as before. Closes priced from venue fills are untouched.

## §2 Evidence

- §2.1 A linear-perpetual LONG DCA deal, its price well below its average. The venue refused its
  resting take profit "current position is zero", then refused the user's close for the same
  reason. `settleExternallyClosedDeal` found no fills and closed it `profitUnknown`.
- §2.2 Its stored `profit.total` is exactly `funding.total − commission`, a gain. The bot's total
  took the same figure. The event gave `−commission` in USD, and the price move alone was a loss
  many times larger. The test reproduces the stored figure to the last digit from that ledger.
- §2.3 The fills could not be read because that venue has no account-fills implementation: the
  connector's base `getAccountFills` answers an empty list. So "unknown" was the correct verdict.
  Booking the real result needs that connector method.
- §2.4 Before spec `277` the same branch already booked fees plus funding on these closes. Spec
  277 flagged the deal but kept the booking.

## §3 Assumption

The "Close without TP order" branch and the funding fold after it do not know the close is
unpriced. Gating both on `profitUnknown` makes the stored profit, the totals and the event agree.
Confirmed by the red tests, which reproduce the stored figure from the stored ledger.

## §4 Consumers of a deal's stored profit (affected before this fix)

Bot `profit` total (dashboards, API, `closeAfterXprofit`). User profit records (`saveProfitToDb`:
account P&L, stats). `closeAfterXwin`/`closeAfterXloss` (`profit.totalUsd > 0` counts a win).
The deal-closed alert (`sendDealClosedAlert`, after the fold). The public API deal `profit`. V2's
deal row already hides a `profitUnknown` deal's profit.

## §5 Not in scope

Rewriting deals or bot totals already stored this way (a prod data write; separate repair).
