# 264 — A bot's funding total is charged again when a deal reloads from a stale snapshot

Bug spec (Definition / Evidence / Assumption). Code: `src/bot/dcaHelper.ts`
`processDealFunding` (DCA and combo — combo inherits it).

## §1 Definition

- §1.1 A futures DCA/combo bot's `funding.total` / `funding.totalUsd` equals the
  sum of its deals' `funding.total` / `funding.totalUsd`. Each settlement is
  added once to the deal and once to the bot.
- §1.2 Actual: the bot total grows several times larger than the sum of its
  deals; the deals themselves stay equal to what the venue charged.
- §1.3 Not in scope: deal funding, profit figures, orders. None of them change.

## §2 Evidence

- §2.1 `processDealFunding` commits the deal with a compare-and-swap on
  `funding.offset`, then `$inc`s the bot unconditionally. When the CAS
  matches nothing, the bot is still charged.
- §2.2 The in-memory mirror is saved with `setDeal(live, false)`, which skips
  `setDealToRedis`. A deal with no fills or other saves keeps a Redis
  snapshot whose cursor is the one it had at its last save.
- §2.3 On a worker restart `loadOrders` restores the deal from that snapshot
  and `startFunding` runs `processDealFunding` at once: every settlement after
  the stale cursor is recomputed, the deal CAS misses (Mongo already holds
  them), and the bot is charged for them again. Once per restart.
- §2.4 On a bot whose open deal kept restoring the same snapshot across many
  restarts, replaying the settlements since that snapshot once per restart
  reproduces the bot total's growth to within mark-price rounding, while the
  deal total matched the venue's funding export throughout.

## §3 Assumption

- §3.1 Root cause: the bot `$inc` does not depend on the deal CAS having
  matched (§2.1). The stale snapshot (§2.2) is what makes misses frequent.
- §3.2 Fix: charge the bot only when the deal write matched; on a miss, adopt
  the deal's stored funding into the live deal and touch nothing at bot level;
  on a failed write, move nothing (the cursor stays, the next notify retries).
  Save the advanced cursor to the Redis snapshot. Mirror the bot's in-memory
  total from the stored document.

## §4 Data repair

Rows already written are repaired by the parent's one-off
`scripts/repairBotFundingTotals.ts`, which sets each bot's funding to the sum
of its deals, skipping bots with a deal that has no funding cursor (their
settlements may be on the bot only).

## Invariants

- The bot's funding total equals the sum of its deals' funding within
  max(0.01, 0.1 %), for bots that took funding in the last 12 h — parent pack
  `specs/invariants/deals.invariants.json`, `bots.funding-total-off-its-deals`
  (observe).
