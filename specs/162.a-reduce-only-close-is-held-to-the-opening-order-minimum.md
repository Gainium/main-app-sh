# 162 — A reduce-only close is held to the opening-order minimum

## 1. Definition

### 1.1 — expected

A futures deal's closing remainder — the part of the position left after a
take-profit, sold by the market sweep (`closeDeal` → `sellRemainder`) or
rested by `closeRemainderByLimit` — is sent whenever it clears the pair's
minimum **quantity**, on every venue that accepts a reduce-only close below
its minimum order **value**. On those venues the minimum order value
(`quoteAsset.minAmount`, the pair's min notional) only governs orders that
open or increase a position.

Venues that accept it, and the position modes in which that is established
(§2):

| venue | one-way (reduce-only flag) | hedge (position side) |
|---|---|---|
| Bybit linear | yes | yes |
| Binance USDⓈ-M | yes | yes |
| Bitget USDT-M | **not established** — stays checked | yes |

Their paper twins follow the live venue, so paper behaves as live does.

### 1.2 — actual

All four checks compare the remainder's value against
`quoteAsset.minAmount` on every venue. A remainder worth less than the
venue minimum (5 USDT on most of these pairs) is never sent: the market
sweep is skipped, `sellRemainder` logs "qty less than minimals qty" and
returns, `closeRemainderByLimit` logs "below the exchange minimums, no limit
close placed". The deal is booked closed or left with that position open on
the venue, though the venue would have closed it.

The four checks:

- `closeDeal`, the pre-close sweep after a take-profit fill;
- `closeDeal`, the remainder re-read after the close is booked;
- `closeRemainderByLimit`;
- `sellRemainder`.

### 1.3 — unchanged

- **Every other venue**, including Hyperliquid (accepts a reduce-only close
  below $10 only when it closes the whole position on the venue, which a
  deal's remainder need not be — §2.3), Bitget USDT-M in one-way mode, every spot venue (a spot
  sell is an ordinary order and must meet the minimum), and every
  coin-margined venue (`quoteAsset.minAmount` is the contract size there).
- The minimum **quantity** (`baseAsset.minAmount`) and the zero / step
  rounding guards still apply to every close (Bybit: closing orders are
  "still subject to the preset minimum order size").
- **Reducing deal funds** (`reduceDealFunds`). Its order is sent without the
  reduce-only flag, so a one-way venue treats it as an ordinary order and
  applies the minimum (Binance: `-4164` unless reduce-only).
- Opening orders, take-profit sizing, the multi-TP/SL remainder fold, and
  TP-coverage drift detection.

## 2. Evidence

### 2.1 — venue rules

- Bybit (derivatives trading rules): closing orders are not restricted by the
  minimum notional value but remain subject to the minimum order size; the
  API announcement introducing the minimum: "This limit will not affect
  closing position orders."
- Binance USDⓈ-M: error `-4164` — notional must be no smaller than the
  minimum "(unless you choose reduce only)"; contract-specs FAQ: a
  reduce-only order is not affected.
- Bitget mix (v1 docs): a position under the minimum is closed with a
  reduce-only order for the remaining quantity; the same docs state that
  route does not apply in hedge (`double_hold`) mode, and the current API
  pages state no exemption either way.
- Hyperliquid: `MinTradeNtl` — orders must have a minimum value of $10.

### 2.2 — orders the venues filled

Filled take-profit closes worth less than the venue's minimum order value,
read from the order history:

| venue | one-way | hedge |
|---|---|---|
| bybitLinear | filled (reduce-only) | filled, many, down to fractions of a cent |
| binanceUsdm | filled (reduce-only) | filled, down to fractions of a cent |
| bitgetUsdm | none attempted — not established | filled, down to cents |

These are take-profit closes, which `getTPOrder` / `prepareTpOrder` already
exempt from the quote minimum on futures; they show the venue accepting
exactly the order shape this spec sends.

### 2.3 — orders the venues refused

- No minimum-value refusal (`-4164`, `110094`, Bitget `45110`) of any futures
  close. Every Bitget "less than the minimum amount" refusal of a take-profit
  came from a **spot** bot.
- Hyperliquid refuses a reduce-only close for value when it is only part of
  the venue position: its user stream reports
  `"reduceOnly":true … "status":"minTradeNtlRejected"` for a
  `hyperliquidLinear` HYPE-USDC close of 0.11 at 81.865 (≈ 9.0), a deal
  sharing its account and pair with another open deal. Every sub-$10
  reduce-only close it filled was a deal alone on its account and pair, and
  every such refusal was a deal sharing it.

## 3. Assumption (hypothesis)

The four checks read `quoteAsset.minAmount` directly, with no notion that the
order they guard is a reduce-only close. A test that asks `sellRemainder` /
`closeRemainderByLimit` on `bybitLinear` (minimum 5) to close a remainder
worth ~2 fails today with nothing sent.

## 4. Open questions

- **Bitget USDT-M one-way** stays checked until a sub-minimum reduce-only
  close is observed filling (or refused) there.
- **Hyperliquid** accepts a reduce-only order below $10 that closes the
  *whole* venue position (§2.3). Not used here: the sweep sizes from the
  deal's own books, not the venue position, so it cannot tell a whole close
  from a partial one.
- A deal-close on Hyperliquid whose deal shares the venue position with
  another open deal, and is worth under $10, can be closed by no order the
  venue accepts. Today it is retried and then stops the bot with a generic
  order-parameter error. Separate change.
- Reduce funds could be sent reduce-only on futures, which would let it join
  §1.1; that changes the order the venue receives and is a separate change.
