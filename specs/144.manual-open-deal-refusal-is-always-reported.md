# 144 — A manual "open deal" refused for a standing condition says nothing

Bug spec.

## §1 Definition

- §1.1 **Expected.** Every manual "open deal" request (dashboard button,
  public API → `openNewDealMan`) that is refused because a combo pair's
  budget cannot fund its base grid at the venue minimum (spec 087), or
  because the account cannot fund the deal ("Not enough balance", spec 008),
  reports the reason to the user — the first click and every click after it.
- §1.2 **Actual.** Both refusals report through
  `standingConditionLatch.shouldReport`, which answers `true` once per
  (pair, condition) and `false` until the condition clears or the re-arm
  window passes. The latch exists to mute the AUTOMATIC retry loop, but the
  manual path reaches the same check, so a click after the first one is
  refused in silence: the button appears to do nothing.
- §1.3 **Unchanged.** Whether a deal opens. Both refusals and the budget
  threshold stay exactly as they are. Automatic retries (asap, timer,
  indicator, webhook, dynamic) are still reported once per (pair, condition):
  a manual report neither consumes nor resets the latch. Terminal DCA deals
  keep their existing exemption.

## §2 Evidence

- §2.1 Prod combo-bot log, one Bitget USDT-M combo bot, 2026-10-09: eight
  `Open new deal manualy` lines (14:57, 16:11, 16:16, 16:18, 16:25, 16:26,
  16:51, 16:58 UTC) and two `Budget … is below the minimum …` warnings, at
  14:57 and 16:11 only.
- §2.2 Code: `openNewDealMan` calls `openNewDeal(botId, symbol, true)` with no
  trigger; `comboHelper.refuseDealBelowMinimumBudget(symbol)` takes no
  trigger; the combo and DCA balance refusals and the single-position entry
  balance refusal gate on `shouldReport` alone (DCA exempts terminal only).

## §3 Assumption

The latch cannot tell a click from a retry because nothing tells it:
`openNewDealMan` does not label its call, and the refusal sites never look at
the trigger. Labelling the call `'manual'` and reporting manual refusals
without touching the latch fixes §1.2 without moving §1.3.

## §4 Out of scope

- §4.1 The DCA "order below exchange minimum" refusal
  (`refuseOrderBelowExchangeMin`) uses the same latch; not covered by this
  report.
