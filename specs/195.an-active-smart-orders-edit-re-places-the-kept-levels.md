# 195 — An active-smart-orders edit re-places the kept levels

Bug spec. Follows the take-profit-only deal edit (`isTpOnlyDealEdit`,
spec 194) in `src/bot/dcaHelper.ts`.

## §1 Definition

### §1.1 Expected

- **§1.1.1** Lowering an open DCA deal's number of active smart orders
  (`activeOrdersCount`) cancels only the resting safety orders the smaller
  count drops — the levels furthest from price. The safety orders that stay
  and the take profit are not cancelled or placed again.
- **§1.1.2** Raising it places only the missing next levels; nothing resting
  is cancelled.
- **§1.1.3** This applies to a patch whose only changed setting is
  `activeOrdersCount`, compared by value against the deal's effective
  settings (a whole form with only that key changed qualifies). DCA only, like
  §194's take-profit-only edit.
- **§1.1.4** If a resting safety order of the deal is partly filled, the edit
  takes the full rebuild, as before.
- **§1.1.5** NOT CHANGING: an edit to order step, sizes, total order count or
  volume scale — alone or together with the active count — still cancels and
  re-places the whole ladder. (The total order count is a count-only edit since
  spec 196.)

### §1.2 Actual

- **§1.2.1** `updateDealSettings` sent every patch that was not
  take-profit-only through the full `rebuildDealOrders`: `cancelAllOrder`
  pulled the take profit and every safety order, then the regenerated ladder
  was placed again — the kept levels at the very same prices, after losing
  their place in the book.

## §2 Evidence

- Production: `updateDCADealSettings` with the patch `{"activeOrdersCount":3}`
  on an open DCA deal resting five smart orders. Within two seconds the take
  profit and the safety orders were cancelled; within the next three, three
  safety orders and the take profit were placed again at the same prices.
- `createCurrentDealOrders` applies `activeOrdersCount` only as a final
  `slice(0, activeOrders)` over the unfilled levels nearest the price; no
  level's price or size reads it.

## §3 Assumption

Hypothesis: the full rebuild is the only reason the kept levels are
re-placed. Diffing the regenerated ladder's safety orders against the resting
ones by price and side (`findDiff`, the routine re-size after a safety-order
fill) yields exactly the dropped levels to cancel and the added levels to
place; the take profit is not in the diff, so it stays.

## §4 Fix

- **§4.1** `isActiveOrdersCountOnlyDealEdit` — DCA, and the changed keys
  (`changedDealSettingKeys`, shared with `isTpOnlyDealEdit`) are exactly
  `activeOrdersCount`.
- **§4.2** `rebuildDealOrders({ activeOrdersOnly })` skips `cancelAllOrder`,
  regenerates the ladder as before, then hands `placeOrders` the diff of the
  regenerated `dealRegular` levels against the deal's resting `NEW` ladder
  orders. No take profit is cancelled or placed; pending funds are not
  re-sent, since nothing of theirs was cancelled.
- **§4.3** Any resting `PARTIALLY_FILLED` ladder order → full rebuild (§1.1.4):
  the re-size diff cancels only `NEW` orders.

## §5 Tests

`src/bot/dca/activeOrdersCountDealEdit.harness.spec.ts` — §1.1.3 detection
cases; 5 → 3 cancels the two furthest levels only (and they resolve to the
resting rows through `findOrderForGrid`); 3 → 5 places the two missing levels
only; §1.1.4 partial fallback; §1.1.5 step / size / count / scale edits still
run `cancelAllOrder`. `tpOnlyDealEdit.harness.spec.ts` stays green.
