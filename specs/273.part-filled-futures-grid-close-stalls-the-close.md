# 273 — A part-filled futures grid close stalls the close

Bug spec.

## §1 Definition

### §1.1 Expected

When a user closes a futures grid "at limit" or "at market"
(`CloseGRIDTypeEnum.closeByLimit` / `closeByMarket`), the bot keeps working
until the position on the exchange is closed. A reduce-only LIMIT close that
has not filled is re-priced every `limitRepositionTimeout`. If a close order
fills only partly, the part it sold is booked into the bot's position and
profit, and the rest of the position is closed by the next order.

"Cancel" (stop without closing) still leaves the position open: nothing new is
placed. A close order left part-filled is still booked, so the bot's position
says what is left on the exchange.

### §1.2 Actual

`processSellAtStop` did all of its work only when no close order was held as
`FILLED` **or `PARTIALLY_FILLED`**. Once the resting close was held as
`PARTIALLY_FILLED`, the re-price timer, and any "close at market" sent after
it, entered and returned without doing anything: no cancel, no new close, no
timer. The close was stuck until something outside the bot acted.

A Bybit LIMIT close is held `PARTIALLY_FILLED` from its placement answer alone.
The connector reads the order back right after creating it, so a close that
traded part of itself on arrival needs no user-stream report to reach that
state.

When the user then pressed "cancel", the close was cancelled (or found already
cancelled) with its executed part, and the bot stopped. Nothing booked that
part: `position.qty` kept the full size, and the profit left out the part sold.

### §1.3 The cancel promotion made the same stall worse

A close that rested as `NEW` while it part-filled without a stream report was
cancelled at the next re-price by `cancelAllOrder`. `cancelOrderOnExchange`
promotes a `CANCELED` order with fills to `FILLED` by default. The promoted
close reached `processFilledStop`, which booked the **whole** position as
closed at that price, reset it to 0 and stopped the bot, while the rest of the
position was still open on the exchange.

## §2 Evidence

Production, Bybit USDⓈ-M, LONG futures grid at 20× (identifiers withheld,
because this file is public):

- The user closes at limit. Reduce-only closes for the full position are placed
  and re-priced every ~11 s; each re-price logs `Reposition limit stop order`,
  the old close is cancelled and a new one is saved.
- The fifth close is saved. Ten seconds later `Reposition limit stop order` is
  logged, and then nothing: no cancel of that close and no new close. No user
  stream report for that close (or for the one before it) was ever processed.
- Two "close at market" requests reach main-app over the next two minutes. The
  bot worker logs nothing for either.
- Five minutes later the user presses "cancel". The cancel is answered
  `Order not found`; the read-back says `CANCELED` with 150 of 350 executed. The
  bot closes with `position.qty` still 350 and no profit for the 150 sold.

## §3 Assumption (hypothesis)

The fifth close was held `PARTIALLY_FILLED` from its placement answer, so the
guard in `processSellAtStop` turned every later call into a silent no-op. The
log fits that and nothing else seen: the re-price entered the method (its log
line is the timer's own) and reached neither the cancel nor a placement, and
the later "cancel" ran its own cancel sweep within 260 ms, so the worker was
not stalled. The exact moment the close became `PARTIALLY_FILLED` in memory is
not observed in any log, because the placement answer's status is not logged.

## §4 Required behaviour

- §4.1 A futures re-price that finds a close held `PARTIALLY_FILLED` cancels
  it, books its executed part (position fold + profit, like `processFilledStop`
  books a full close), places a reduce-only close for the rest of the position
  and keeps the re-price timer running. The part is booked once, however many
  passes run.
- §4.2 A "close at market" arriving while a part-filled LIMIT close rests does
  the same, and sweeps the rest at market.
- §4.3 If the cancel does not go through, nothing is booked or placed (two
  closes would rest at once), and the re-price timer retries.
- §4.4 If the venue says the close filled in full, it goes to the fill path as
  before and nothing new is placed.
- §4.5 The close cancel never promotes a part-filled close to `FILLED`
  (§1.3).
- §4.6 "Cancel" books the executed part of a part-filled close and places
  nothing.
- §4.7 A key that may not cancel is reported once (spec 232) and neither
  re-tried nor stacked with a second close.
- §4.8 Spot is unchanged: its close books balances, not a position.

## Invariants

- A futures grid that is `closed` must not have, as its newest close order
  (`typeOrder: stop`), a `CANCELED` row with `executedQty > 0` and no `FILLED`
  close after it. That shape is a close that stopped part-way. Pack:
  main-app `specs/invariants/grid.invariants.json`
  (`grid.futures-close-stopped-part-filled`).
