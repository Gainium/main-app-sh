# 294 — DCA safety orders past the deal's ladder are not re-priced to the market

Bug spec (gainium-dev-flow, bug flavour). Builds on 248 (safety orders the price
ran through are re-sent at the market), 251 (Binance `PERCENT_PRICE*` is a price
band refusal), 254 (same for take profits), 164 (the ladder wall) and 283 (held
orders are noticed once, never a bot error).

## Definition

When the market has gone **far past the deepest level of a DCA deal's safety
ladder**, the engine must not move a refused safety order to the market price
and send it again. That order is not in the deal's DCA range at all:

- moved to the market, a level sized for a price several times higher is worth a
  fraction of what it was sized for, and on Binance it is then refused as below
  the pair's minimum order value (`Filter failure: NOTIONAL`). That second
  refusal is not a price refusal, so it was reported as a bot error;
- the next cycle rebuilt the same level under a new id, sent it at its own
  price, had it refused on the price band, moved it to the market and had it
  refused on value again. Repeated every cycle, with the bot in error.

### Required behaviour

1. **§1.1** A safety order (`dealRegular` with a deal) refused on a price band
   while the market is beyond the deal's deepest safety level (by the tolerance
   in §1.4) is **not re-priced**. It is held by level and the user is told once,
   with the subType `Order waiting for price` (a warning, never a bot error).
2. **§1.2** While the market stays beyond the ladder, a rebuild of the held level
   is served from the hold: the venue is not asked and nothing is reported. Once
   the market is back inside the ladder the level is sent as usual (and the
   spec 248 re-price applies again if the venue still refuses it on price).
3. **§1.3** A safety order **already re-priced** to the market (spec 248) and
   then refused as below the venue's minimum order value is held the same way,
   told once, and not re-sent at the market by later cycles. It is released when
   the market is no longer through the level, i.e. when its own price is a
   resting limit again.
4. **§1.4** "Beyond the ladder" is `isDisplacementInLadder` against
   `toleratedLadderEnd` (spec 164: the deepest level widened by
   `LADDER_END_SLACK` of the ladder's depth), with one addition: the tolerance is
   never wider than `LADDER_END_SLACK` of the deepest level's own price. A long
   ladder deeper than half its start (here 1890.92 → 166.43) otherwise has a
   tolerated end below zero, and no market would ever be beyond it.

### Not changing

- A safety order or take profit the price ran through **inside** the ladder is
  still re-sent at the market exactly as today (specs 248/254). A level 1% past
  the market with the ladder still below it is re-sent.
- Grid (`dealGrid`) orders, take profits, add/reduce funds orders, MARKET orders.
- The spec 164 gap path in `dcaHelper` (breakpoints) is unchanged; the floor of
  §1.4 lives in a new helper and only this check uses it.
- The CANCELED write-off row and spec 221 id bookkeeping for a refusal the venue
  answered.

## Evidence

- A long spot DCA deal on a Binance pair, start 1890.92, remaining safety levels
  531.36 / 357.58 / 166.43 (qty 0.01 / 0.02 / 0.04), market 74.57. Each cycle,
  the logs show `refused on price band, market 74.57. Re-send at 74.57` followed
  by `Filter failure: NOTIONAL` on the re-priced order (0.01 × 74.57 ≈ 0.75 USDT,
  below the 5 USDT minimum), the bot then in error.
- `toleratedLadderEnd(1890.92, 166.43, true)` = 166.43 − 0.1 × 1724.49 < 0, so
  the existing spec 164 check calls 74.57 "inside the ladder".
- The re-price for `dealRegular` was added by spec 248; Binance's
  `PERCENT_PRICE_BY_SIDE` started reaching it with spec 251. Neither asked
  whether the market was still inside the deal's DCA range.

## Assumption

- The deal's `initialOrders` safety levels on the order's side give the ladder's
  deepest level; `deal.initialPrice` gives its start. When either is unknown the
  order is treated as inside the ladder, i.e. today's behaviour.
- A held level's price is placeable again once the market is back inside the
  ladder: the venue's band is relative to the market, and the level's own
  notional is what the deal sized it for.

## Invariants

`deals.safety-order-repriced-below-min-notional` (main-app
`specs/invariants/deals.invariants.json`, observe): a bot with a `NOTIONAL` error
row in the last 24 h **and** a safety level (`dealRegular`, same deal and
`origPrice`) written off CANCELED at a price other than its `origPrice` two or more
times in that window, i.e. re-priced to the market and refused again, cycle after
cycle. Order rows do not store the refusal, so the error row is the join key: a
repeat count alone also matches the balance-refusal retry, which re-prices and
writes off a level once an hour by design. After this fix: 0 hits.
