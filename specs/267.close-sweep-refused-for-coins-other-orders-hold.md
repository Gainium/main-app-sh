# 267 — A spot LONG close whose sweep is refused for balance closes as an ordinary profit

Bug spec (Definition / Evidence / Assumption). Tests:
`src/bot/closeSweepShortfall.spec.ts`.

## §1 Definition

A spot LONG DCA deal closes by take profit. When the take profit sold only
part of the position, `closeDeal` sends a MARKET sweep of the rest first
(spec `157`). Spec `157` §1.5 keeps closing the deal when that sweep is
refused for any reason other than a limit-only book, and spec `211` keeps it
open when the deal's OWN resting take profit holds the coins.

The remaining case: the sweep is refused for insufficient balance and no
order of this deal holds the coins. Other open orders on the same account and
pair (another bot, a manual order) lock them, or they are gone.

**Expected.**

- §1.1 If the account still holds at least the remainder (free + locked, read
  from the venue after the take-profit fill), the deal stays open exactly as in
  spec `211`: the fill is kept as a partial take profit and the rest is closed
  by limit (`closeRemainderByLimit`), whose refusals go to the existing
  refused-close recovery. The user gets a warning naming the locked quantity.
- §1.2 Otherwise (or when the balance cannot be read) the deal closes as spec
  `157` §1.5 does, with the same profit arithmetic, but the close is stamped
  with `unsoldBase` (the base the sweep could not sell) and the user gets a
  warning "take profit sold X of Y, Z unsold".
- §1.3 Unchanged: a sweep the venue accepts, a refusal for another reason, the
  limit-only branch (spec `157`) and the own-take-profit branch (spec `211`).
  None of them reads the balance, stamps the deal or sends a message.

**Actual.** The deal is written `closed` with `closeTrigger: tp`; the unsold
coins are booked as `profit.pureBase` and no message reaches the user. The
coins stay in the account, managed by no deal.

## §2 Evidence (prod, read-only, 2026-11-23)

- Kraken XRP-USD spot LONG deal `6afdf5b2…`: entries 431.31 XRP. A spot SHORT
  bot on the same pair and account had sold some of those coins and locked the
  rest under its resting sells. The take profit was re-sent sized to the free
  balance and sold 285.76. At `19:00:04.832Z` the sweep `D-SR-Sevw5opnO6awk`
  (MARKET SELL 142.10404) was refused `EOrder:Insufficient funds`; the bot's
  message read `balance total - 400.44 XRP, free - 285.76 XRP, required -
  142.10 XRP`. That row is the STORED balance from before the take-profit
  fill (free == the take profit's size): after the fill the account held about
  114.68 XRP, all locked. The deal closed `tp` with `pureBase` 145.55 XRP.
- Coinbase XRP-USDC long deal `6b01f84b…`: same shape, 129.97 XRP left.
- The existing invariant `dcadeals.closed-spot-long-keeps-base` flagged both.

## §3 Assumption (hypothesis, confirmed by the red tests)

`closeDeal` treats every non-limit-only balance refusal without an own resting
take profit as "the remainder is not there" and closes silently. It never asks
the venue whether the coins are held, and never records the shortfall.

The stored balance row cannot answer that question: it predates the fill that
just landed (§2), so it would call coins gone "locked". The venue is read
directly (`checkAssets(true, true)`).

## §4 Non-goals

- Whether a spot SHORT deal may sell coins an open spot LONG on the same
  pair/account holds (product decision; spec `261` covers the hedge legs).
- Correcting already-closed deals.

## Invariants

`dcadeals.closed-spot-long-keeps-base` (main-app `specs/invariants/deals.invariants.json`)
keeps flagging silent short closes; a close stamped `unsoldBase` has been
reported to the user and is skipped. A locked-coins close no longer closes, so
it no longer appears there.
