# 285 — One-way futures: safety orders against an opposite deal, and an unreadable venue position

Bug spec. Follows spec 262 (`262.one-way-opposite-direction-deals.md`), which
refuses a NEW deal against the opposite direction on a one-way futures account
and leaves two cases out on purpose (§4.1.3, §5). This spec closes both.

## 1. Definition

### 1.1 Expected

On a one-way futures account (`futures && !hedge`) the venue keeps ONE net
position per symbol. When another bot of the same user, on the same account
and symbol, holds the opposite direction:

- §1.1.1 A DCA or combo **safety order** that would add to this deal's side is
  held back, not sent. It would only shrink the other bot's position on the
  venue while both deals keep recording their own.
- §1.1.2 A held safety order is retried every 60 s
  (`OPPOSING_POSITION_RETRY_MS`) and placed as soon as the opposite deal is
  gone and the venue no longer holds the other side. The bot reports the hold
  once per re-arm window (standing-condition latch), like spec 262 §4.1.4.
- §1.1.3 When the venue position **cannot be read**, a new deal or safety
  order is held (and retried) whenever the user has an opposite-direction bot
  on that symbol and account. An unreadable answer is then not evidence that
  the position is safe to add to.
- §1.1.4 A symbol the netting left **completely flat** while an opposite deal
  is still open in the database is held on the deal: the open opposite deal is
  checked first, so a flat venue does not let the entry through.

### 1.2 Actual

- Spec 262 §5: "Safety orders of a deal that is already open are not gated."
  An opposite pair opened before spec 262 shipped keeps netting through its
  safety orders.
- Spec 262 §4.1.3: an unreadable venue answer does not refuse. With no open
  opposite deal in the database, a failed read opens the deal.

## 2. Evidence

Read-only, production, one krakenUsdm account with a LONG and a SHORT DCA bot
on the same pairs:

- ETH-USD order rows (`orders`, by `dealId`): the LONG deal's base and two
  safety orders filled (+0.045). The SHORT deal opened 14:00 the next day
  (−0.027), then its two safety orders filled 15:01 and 17:42 (−0.010 each)
  while the LONG deal was still open. The net position went from +0.045 to
  −0.002: the SHORT deal's safety orders closed the LONG deal's position on
  the venue.
- Bot log, the next day 13:00:01: `Close order for deal … rejected because the
  position is already closed (wouldNotReducePosition)`, then `position closed
  outside the bot and the venue fills do not account for it — closing the
  deal, profit unknown`.
- After spec 262 was deployed (bot log, every minute): `Not opening a SHORT
  deal on ETH-USD: … That LONG position belongs to open deal …`. The new-deal
  gate runs. A new LONG deal opened before the deploy still has two safety
  orders resting, and its first safety order filled while the SHORT deal was
  open.
- Venue now (dashboard `getAllOpenPositions`, read-only): ETH-USD net SHORT
  0.015 against open deals LONG 0.032 and SHORT 0.047. BTC-USD net LONG 0.0014
  against open deals LONG 0.0014 and SHORT 0.0049. Neither SHORT side exists on
  the venue as recorded.
- Code: `placeOrdersHoldingDealLock` and `checkDCAByMarketLevel` send safety
  orders with no direction check. `refuseNewDealAgainstOpposingPosition` returns
  `false` on `{ kind: 'unavailable' }`.

## 3. Assumption (root cause)

Spec 262 put the per-deal direction check on the new-deal path only, and made
an unreadable venue answer fail open. Safety orders reach the venue through
`placeOrdersHoldingDealLock` (limit ladder, DCA and combo) and
`checkDCAByMarketLevel` (DCA by market), and neither asks.

## 4. Change

- §4.0 Pure decision `opposingHold` in `dca/opposingNetPosition.ts`. In order:
  an open opposite deal holds (`deal`); a venue net position on the other side
  holds (`venue`); an unreadable venue holds only if the user has an
  opposite-direction bot on the symbol and account (`unreadable`); anything
  else allows.
- §4.1 `holdSafetyOrderAgainstOpposingPosition(symbol)` (DCA and combo,
  one-way futures only). It asks for the open opposite deal first
  (`findOpposingDealHolder` with the opposite strategy). With none, it asks
  whether an opposite-direction bot exists (`hasOpposingBotOnSymbol`). With
  neither, it allows at once and reads nothing from the venue. A one-direction
  bot therefore places safety orders exactly as before. Otherwise it reads the
  venue once and applies §4.0.
  - §4.1.1 A hold is cached per symbol for the retry window, so a by-market
    level that re-triggers on every price tick does not re-query.
  - §4.1.2 Reported as a bot warning once per re-arm window, keyed by pair
    (`safetyOrderAgainstOpposingPosition`).
- §4.2 `placeOrdersHoldingDealLock`: the position-adding `dealRegular` orders
  of the pass (LONG buys, SHORT sells) are taken out when §4.1 holds. The rest
  of the pass (take-profit, cancels) runs unchanged. The held levels are
  placed again after `OPPOSING_POSITION_RETRY_MS` through `placeOrders`. Only
  the levels the deal's ladder still wants and that are not resting are placed
  (`venueCanceledSafetyLevel`, spec 247). The retry goes through §4.1 again.
- §4.3 `checkDCAByMarketLevel`: a held level is skipped without marking it
  processing, so the next tick past the level asks again (cached, §4.1.1).
- §4.4 `refuseNewDealAgainstOpposingPosition` (spec 262 §4.1): an unreadable
  venue with an opposite-direction bot refuses with a message that says the
  position could not be read. With no such bot, unchanged: the deal opens.

### 4.5 What a pre-existing opposite pair does

On its next safety order, each side of the pair is held while the other
side's deal is open (§1.1.4). Neither side averages further until one deal
closes. Resting safety orders placed before this change are not cancelled.
They are on the venue already and can still fill. They are listed in the
repair proposal for the operator.

## 5. Not changed

- Hedge-mode accounts, spot, grid bots: never checked.
- A bot with no opposite-direction bot and no open opposite deal on the
  symbol and account: no new read, no hold. That includes an opposite position
  opened by hand outside Gainium.
- Take-profits, closes, add/reduce funds, combo minigrid levels (`dealGrid`).
- Nothing is repaired in the database. Rows left by netting before this change
  need an operator decision, per pair.

## 6. Open questions

- §6.1 Hold or allow on an unreadable venue (§1.1.3): **hold**, scoped to
  accounts that have an opposite-direction bot. The other choice, allow, is
  how the opposite deal opens. Without such a bot, an unreadable venue keeps
  allowing, so a Kraken outage does not stop single-direction trading.

`main-app` `specs/invariants/deals.invariants.json`,
`deals.one-way-safety-order-against-opposite-deal` (observe): a DCA safety

`orders` index rule. Spec 262's `deals.one-way-opposite-direction-open`
lists the pairs. This one shows that a pair is still netting.
