# 130 — Smart Grids trimmed levels are never re-placed

Combo bot · `core/src/bot/comboHelper.ts` · `processCanceledOrder` (dealGrid branch)

## 1. Definition

### 1.1 Expected

With Smart Grids on (`comboUseSmartGrids`, `comboSmartGridsCount = N`), a combo
deal keeps only the N grid levels nearest the price on the book. Levels further
away are cancelled **by the bot** and stay on their minigrid's ladder
(`minigrid.currentOrders`), so that `findDiffCombo` re-places them when the
price comes back into the window. A level the bot trimmed is off the book, not
off the ladder.

### 1.2 Actual

Since spec 077, `processCanceledOrder` removes every `CANCELED`/`EXPIRED`
`dealGrid` level from the ladder, whoever issued the cancel. A Smart Grids trim
therefore erases the levels it trims. `findDiffCombo` builds its window only
from `currentOrders`, so the erased levels can never return. The minigrid is
not closed either while it holds base, so it stays `range` with an empty ladder
(`grids: {buy: 0, sell: 0}`) for the rest of the deal. A long deal whose upper
minigrids' sells were trimmed while a lower minigrid traded is left holding a
position with no sell order at all. Only a rebuild from the stored levels
(`prepareMinigrids`, run on a settings save) restores the ladder, until the
next trim.

## 2. Evidence

Production, long Hyperliquid combo bot with Smart Grids (5 orders):

- A DCA fill opened a new minigrid; in the same worker process, within ~1.2 s
  per order, the five sells of the next minigrid up were reported `CANCELED`
  and the new minigrid's five sells were then placed — the cancel-then-place
  burst `placeOrders` issues for a `findDiffCombo` diff.
- The next day two of those new sells filled. At that price the trimmed sells
  were again among the 5 nearest levels, but each fill placed only the
  replacement buy.
- When the lower minigrid closed, the bot cancelled its buys and placed only
  the DCA buy. The account held the position with no sell order for four days.
- Both open minigrids were stored with `grids: {buy: 0, sell: 0}`.
- A settings save reloaded the bot, and within seconds the five trimmed sells
  were back on the book.

The cancels are recorded in `ownCancels` before the venue round trip
(`cancelOrderOnExchange` → `noteOwnCancel`, spec 095 §4.1), and the CANCELED
reports arrived seconds later in the same process, inside the 60-minute
lifetime of that record.

## 3. Assumption (root cause hypothesis)

The dealGrid branch of `processCanceledOrder` does not ask `isOwnCancel`, so a
bot-issued Smart Grids trim is treated like a venue/user cancel and pruned.

A test that records the cancel through the real `noteOwnCancel` and then
delivers the CANCELED report must see the level removed today (red) and kept
after the fix.

## 4. Scope

- A cancel this bot did not issue (venue, account owner) is still pruned and
  its reservation released, exactly as spec 077 requires.
- Every other bot-issued grid cancel needs no prune: placement diffs cancel
  levels the regenerated ladder no longer holds, and a minigrid or deal close
  removes the ladder itself.

## 5. Known limit

`ownCancels` lives in memory. A trim whose CANCELED report is processed only
after a worker restart is still pruned. There is no persisted marker of who
issued a cancel on the order row; adding one is a shared-schema change and is
out of scope here.
