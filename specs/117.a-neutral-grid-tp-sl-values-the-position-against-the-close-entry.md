# 117 — A neutral grid's value-changed TP/SL values the position against the entry the close books

Follow-up to spec `099`. Since `099`, a NEUTRAL futures grid **books** its close
leg against the entry of the fills the round-trip ledger left unpaired
(`closeEntryPrice()`), because `data.position.price` still averages every fill
that ever added to the position, paired ones included. The "value changed"
take-profit / stop-loss check in `tpSl()` was left valuing the open position
against `data.position.price`. The trigger and the booking therefore measure
two different results, and the trigger counts the gap between the two entries
as profit that does not exist.

## §1 Definition

### §1.1 Expected

For a futures grid, the value-changed check's result at price `p`

```
(realized profit.total + open position valued at p) / (initial value / leverage)
```

is the result the bot books if it closes at `p` (before the close order's fee):
the open position is valued against the SAME entry `closeEntryPrice()` would
book the close against — the unpaired-fills entry for a NEUTRAL, non-COIN-M
grid, `position.price` everywhere that helper returns `position.price`
(LONG/SHORT strategy, COIN-M, a ledger that does not net to the position, a
failed ledger read).

A TP of 3% therefore fires when the bot is up about 3%, and a stop-loss fires
when it is actually down by its setting — not later.

### §1.2 Actual

`tpSl()` computes `qty × (lastPrice − position.price)` (sign per side). On a
neutral grid where the unpaired fills sit on the other side of
`position.price` from the price, that overstates the result, so TP fires
early and SL fires late.

## §2 Evidence

Two paper NEUTRAL futures grids (lev 1, TP 3% / SL −4%, both value-changed),
read read-only over the dashboard GraphQL:

- §2.1 BR bot: 22 FILLED regular fills of 27, 9 paired transactions, 4 unpaired
  SELLs (0.88711, 0.91002, 0.93352, 0.95764 → entry 0.9220725). Position SHORT
  108 at `position.price` 0.97663. Realized before close 5.6916; initial value
  162 × 0.86034964 + 147.72537 = 287.10. Prod log: `Position unPnL 3.08%, in
  settings 3%, TP trigger` at price 0.94742, then `Close entry: 0.9220725 from
  unpaired fills (position price 0.97663)`. 3.08% = (5.6916 + 108 × (0.97663 −
  0.94742)) / 287.10. Booked result 2.9546 = 1.03%.
- §2.2 NIL bot: 55 fills of 134.3, 24 pairs, position LONG 940.1 at
  `position.price` 0.10412, unpaired entry 0.11115, realized 9.9064, initial
  value 281.13, close at 0.10314. Trigger 3.2%; booked 2.3764 = 0.85%.

## §3 Assumption (root cause)

`tpSl()` values the open position against `data.position.price`; `099` moved
only the close booking to the unpaired-fills entry. Test: replaying §2.1's
fills through the real `calculatePosition` and calling the real `tpSl(0.94742)`
fires TP at 3.08%.

## §4 Required behaviour

- §4.1 `tpSl()` values the open position against the close entry; for §2.1 it
  returns no trigger at 0.94742 (≈1.03%), for §2.2 none at 0.10314 (≈0.85%).
- §4.2 The trigger's percentage equals the result `profitAfterPositionClosed()`
  then books at that price.
- §4.3 SL is evaluated on the same basis (fires at the real −4%, not later).
- §4.4 `tpSl()` runs per price tick and stays synchronous: it never reads the
  ledger itself. The entry is cached per position (side, qty, price) and
  recomputed after each fill's transaction is booked; a position the cache was
  not computed for (restart, settings change) is valued against
  `position.price` until a refresh — started from the tick — lands.
  *(Superseded by spec `135`: a cold cache now skips the check until the
  refresh lands.)*
- §4.5 Fallbacks match `closeEntryPrice()` exactly; LONG/SHORT/COIN-M grids
  never read the ledger for this.
- §4.6 Not changed: how the close is booked (`099`), and the per-bot
  transaction mutex (spec `073`).
