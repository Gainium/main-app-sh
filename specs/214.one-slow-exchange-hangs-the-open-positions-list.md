# 214 — one slow exchange hangs the open positions and open orders lists

Bug spec (SKILL.md Part 0). Scope: `src/graphql/handlers/orders.handler.ts`
(`getAllOpenPositions`, `getAllOpenOrders`) and the two list queries in
`src/graphql/resolvers.ts` that call them.

## §1 Definition

### §1.1 Expected

- §1.1.1 The open positions and open orders lists answer within a bounded
  time (about 15 s) even when one of the user's exchanges is slow or not
  answering. The positions/orders of every exchange that did answer are
  returned; the slow exchange contributes nothing, exactly as when its
  request fails today.
- §1.1.2 A late exchange is logged with its provider and account uuid, so a
  missing list is attributable.

### §1.2 Actual

- §1.2.1 Every exchange is awaited with `Promise.all` and no deadline. When
  one venue stalls (its own client is sleeping on a rate limit, or retrying
  timeouts), the whole list waits for it — up to 100 s — and the dashboard,
  whose request gives up at 30 s, shows an error instead of the positions it
  already had.

## §2 Evidence

- Production `SlowGraphQL getAllOpenPositions ms=100342` while the
  Hyperliquid client logged `clearinghouseState failed … TimeoutError`
  repeatedly for the same two minutes; another sighting at `ms=52736`
  alongside `Hyperliquid request must sleep for 49.615s. Method:
  getClearinghouseState`.
- Code: each exchange's promise has only `.then`/`.catch`; the handler ends
  in `await Promise.all(queries)`.

## §3 Assumption (root cause, hypothesis)

The list is as slow as its slowest venue because no per-exchange deadline
exists. Racing each venue's promise against a deadline that resolves to the
existing failure value (`[]`) bounds the list without changing what a
healthy or a failing venue returns.

## §4 Requirements

- §4.1 Both handlers take an optional per-exchange deadline (ms). When it is
  set and a venue has not settled in time, that venue resolves to `[]` and a
  warning naming the provider and uuid is logged; the other venues' data is
  returned unchanged.
- §4.2 Without a deadline argument the handlers behave as before. The
  import-order and close-position mutations keep calling without one: they
  read leverage/margin type from the position, and silently falling back to
  defaults on a slow venue would change what they build.
- §4.3 The `getAllOpenPositions` and `getAllOpenOrders` GraphQL queries pass
  `OPEN_LIST_DEADLINE_MS` (15 000).
- §4.4 The deadline timer is cleared when the venue answers in time.

## Invariants

None — the defect is latency, it leaves no wrong state in data.
