# 212 — A ledger rebuild counts partial take profits the close subtracts again

Bug spec (Definition / Evidence / Assumption). Test:
`src/bot/tpHistoryLedgerRebuild.spec.ts`.

## §1 Definition

A DCA deal's take-profit that part-fills and is then cancelled (a re-size, or a
venue that never reports `PARTIALLY_FILLED`) leaves a partial take profit in
`deal.tpHistory`. On an open DCA deal `tpHistory` is that sale's only owner:
the fill path does not move `currentBalances` for it, and `closeDeal` subtracts
`orderQty + Σ tpHistory` from `currentBalances` when the deal closes.

**Expected.**

- §1.1 Rebuilding an open DCA deal's ledger (`updateDealBalances`) yields the
  same ledger the fill path would have: a `dealTP` row carried in `tpHistory`
  is not counted there. So the close measures the remainder sweep
  (`preBase = ledger − qty`) against what the account actually holds and sells
  all of it.
- §1.2 The close books the held remainder and the take-profit proceeds once:
  `currentBalances`, `profit.pureBase`/`pureQuote` are measured against one
  count of each partial sale.
- §1.3 Unchanged: a deal with no `tpHistory` is rebuilt from every executed row
  exactly as before; a Combo deal (whose take-profit is sized directly off the
  rebuilt ledger) and an already closed deal keep every row.

**Actual.** `updateDealBalances` counts every `FILLED`/`CANCELED` row with an
executed quantity, cancelled part-filled take-profits included. It runs on an
add-funds / base-remainder fill, on a non-service restart and on a settings
update. After it has run, `closeDeal` subtracts the same partial sales a second
time: the remainder sweep is sized short (or not sent at all when the
double-subtracted remainder is ≤ 0) and the close books a base balance short
by Σ tpHistory — negative when the deal was in fact fully sold.

## §2 Evidence (prod, read-only)

- §2.1 Coinbase spot SUP-USDC long deal (the deal of spec 211). Order rows:
  buys executed 11367.2 + 882 + 1531.8 + 1969.2 + 9936.9 = 25687.1 (base order
  plus base-remainder fills); four `dealTP` rows, each `CANCELED` with fills,
  7116 + 44.8 + 3929.2 + 50.3 = 11140.3. The account held 14546.8.
- §2.2 The close was taken on the 3929.2 take-profit; `tpHistory` carried the
  other 7211.1, so `qty = 11140.3`. The sweep `D-SR-…` was sent for exactly
  3406.5 = 14546.8 − 11140.3, and the deal row booked
  `currentBalances.base: 3406.5`, `profit.pureBase: 3406.5`. A ledger of
  14546.8 before the close is buys − all four cancelled take-profit sales,
  which only `updateDealBalances` produces (it ran on the base-remainder fill
  at 00:31:09); the fill path alone would have left 25687.1.
- §2.3 Other closed long DCA deals with `tpHistory` book a base balance of
  exactly −Σ tpHistory: the account was already empty, the double count made
  the remainder negative, no sweep was sent, and the close booked minus the
  partial sales.
- §2.4 Harness: driving the real `updateDealBalances` then `closeDeal` over the
  §2.1 rows reproduces the 3406.5 sweep to the unit.

## §3 Assumption (root cause — hypothesis)

Two places own a partial take profit after a rebuild: `updateDealBalances`
(the cancelled row) and `tpHistory` (subtracted by `closeDeal`). Making the
rebuild leave `tpHistory` rows to `tpHistory`, as the fill path and the
lost-ledger replay in `closeDeal` (`closedTp`) already do, removes the double
count. A test that rebuilds the §2.1 rows and closes must see a 3406.5 sweep
today and 14546.8 after.

Not changed: Combo. Its take-profit quantity is read straight from the rebuilt
ledger (`updateDealBalances` then `currentBalances.base`), so excluding the
rows there would over-size its take-profit. Whether the Combo close
double-counts the same way is not established here.
