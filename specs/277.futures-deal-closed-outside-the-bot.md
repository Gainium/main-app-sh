# 277 — A futures deal whose position was closed outside the bot

Bug spec (Definition / Evidence / Assumption). Tests: `src/bot/dca/venueCloseEvidence.spec.ts`
(the `spec 277` cases), `src/bot/dca/positionFlatDirectionRefusal.harness.spec.ts` (unchanged
assertions). Pack: main-app `specs/277.invariants.json`. Connector side: exchange-connector-sh
spec `054` (Bitget USDT-M account fills).

## §1 Definition

A futures position can be closed without the bot: by the venue's liquidation engine, by the user on
the exchange, by another tool. The bot only finds out from a refusal ("no position to close") or from
an empty position read.

- §1.1 **Expected.** The deal closes, its result is booked from the venue's own closing fills
  (`venueCloseEvidence`: volume-weighted price, the deal's own fees plus the fills' fee, or the taker
  estimate where the venue reports none), its `closeTrigger` says `liquidation` or `manual`, it is
  flagged `closedExternally`, and the user gets a bot message saying what happened. Where the fills
  cannot be read or do not cover the deal's size, the deal closes flagged `closedExternally` +
  `profitUnknown`, and the message says the profit is unknown. Never a made-up exit price, never a
  last-price estimate.
  **Actual.** A closing order refused "position already closed" (`isPositionAlreadyClosedReason`
  branch of `closeDealById`) called `closeDeal` with no price: the deal booked only what it had already
  realised minus fees (about zero), with no flag and no message.
- §1.2 The combo path reaches §1.1 too: spec `175`'s flat probe (`settleFlatDealAfterGridRefusal`)
  confirms the venue holds nothing and closes with trigger `base`; that close order is refused for the
  same reason. It is marked (`markPositionClosedOutside`) so the refusal settles as §1.1.
  **Unchanged:** a combo deal whose BASE MINIGRID completed (trigger `base`, unmarked — ec8642b) was
  flattened by its own grid orders; it closes exactly as before. Spot closes exactly as before.
- §1.3 Spec `143`'s resting take-profit refused for a vanished position: the first "no position"
  reading of an episode holds the take profit back and warns the user, as before. Once the venue has
  kept answering "no position" for **≥ 1 h** (first reading of the episode to this one,
  `RetryBackoff.firstAt`), the deal goes through `reconcileDealPosition` (two more agreeing readings,
  spec `005`) and is settled as §1.1. The warning no longer asks the user to close the deal by hand.
  **Actual.** Without fills the deal stayed open for good, its bot unable to open a new deal; with
  fills it went to the reconciler on the first reading.
- §1.4 **Actual (defect in the fills settle).** `settleFromVenueFills` closed through
  `closeDealById(…, CloseDCATypeEnum.leave, …, price)`. The `leave` branch cancels the deal's orders,
  announces a left-open position and returns: the deal was never closed. It now closes through
  `closeDeal(botId, dealId, undefined, price, fee)`.
- §1.5 Venue fills from Bitget carry no client order id; `source` (`enterPointSource`) says who placed
  them. A fill counts as "not the bot's" when it has no client order id and its source is not `API`;
  a forced close also counts. `burst_*` (Bitget liquidation) and `*_adl_*` are forced closes.
- §1.6 **Unchanged.** No order is placed or cancelled beyond what `closeDeal` already does for any
  close. A deal whose position the venue still reports, or whose position read fails, is treated as
  today. `processLiquidationOrder` (OKX/Bybit/paper/Kraken liquidation reports) and `closeDeal`'s
  liquidation branch without the new `externalCloseFee` argument are untouched. Already-closed deals
  are not rewritten.

## §2 Evidence

- §2.1 Bitget USDT-M combo deal, long at 5x: the venue position disappeared near its liquidation
  price; a closing grid level and then the market take-profit were refused "no position to close";
  the bot logged `Close order for deal … rejected because the position is already closed … Closing
  deal without order` → `Close without TP order` → `Deal closed …` with a profit of a few cents, where
  the price move alone was a loss of several USDT. No user message about the position.
- §2.2 Bitget USDT-M DCA deal: an indicator close (MARKET) refused "no position to close" while the
  deal was well in profit; the deal closed at about zero in the same second, no message.
- §2.3 Kraken Futures DCA deal, long at 3x, price later about half its average: the resting take
  profit was refused `wouldNotReducePosition` on every pass for days; the deal stayed open and the bot
  opened nothing.
- §2.4 `settleFromVenueFills` → `closeDealById(leave)`: read in code (`closeDealById`, the
  `closeType === CloseDCATypeEnum.leave` early return); its spec stubbed `closeDealById`, so nothing
  noticed.

## §3 Assumption

The three paths share one gap: nothing asks the venue what closed the position, and the "already
closed" close has no way to say its result is unknown. Bitget published no fills to ask
(exchange-connector-sh spec 054).

## §4 Data

Two new `DCADealFlags` values (`closedExternally`, `profitUnknown`) in the existing `flags: [String]`
of both deal collections; exposed by the existing GraphQL `flags` field. No schema change.
