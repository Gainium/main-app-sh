# 099 — A neutral futures grid's close is valued against the whole-position average

A futures grid bot with `futuresStrategy: NEUTRAL` books its result in two
places that use two different cost conventions:

- **round trips** — `createTransaction()` pairs each fill with the opposite
  fill one grid level away and books `qty × (sell − buy)` at those two level
  prices. Those two fills are then out of the residual for good;
- **the close leg** — `profitAfterPositionClosed()` (and the liquidation branch
  of `processLiquidationOrder()`) values the residual that is still open at
  close as `qty × (close − data.position.price)`.

`data.position.price` is the fold `calculateAbstractPosition` keeps: a weighted
average of every fill that ever *added* to the position since it last crossed
zero. A fill that reduces the position leaves the average unchanged. So the
average still carries the adding fills that the round-trip ledger has already
paired off and booked, and it misses the ones that are actually still open.
Each convention is consistent on its own; mixed, they double-count the paired
fills and misprice the residual.

LONG and SHORT futures grids do not have this problem: their round trips are
booked against `position.price` too (`matchedId: 'position price'`), so the two
legs share one basis.

## §1 Definition

### §1.1 Expected

For a neutral futures grid that goes from flat to flat, the reported result
equals its cash flow:

```
Σ transactions.profitQuote + closeLeg == Σ sells − Σ buys      (before fees/funding)
```

That means the close leg must equal the net cash of the fills the round-trip
ledger left **unpaired** plus the closing fill. For a short residual of `Q` held
by unpaired sells, that is `Σ unpaired sell quote − Q × close`.

### §1.2 Actual

The close leg is `Q × (position.price − close)`, where `position.price` is the
all-adding-fills average. The two agree only by accident.

## §2 Evidence

Bot `6aa8d2fd4f67072ebb34903f`: paper futures grid, `AINUSDT`, `NEUTRAL`,
leverage 1, 17 levels, closed 2026-09-15T10:36:09Z. Read over the dashboard
GraphQL with a minted token (read-only).

- §2.1 34 FILLED regular fills of 131 each, then `GRID-TP-7UOn…`, a reduceOnly
  MARKET BUY of 786 at 0.15807. The prod `Gainium-Grid-bot` log has that close
  (`Processing msg AINUSDT GRID-TP-7UOnIGefDcBi8c9IBrnP8kFSVXy, FILLED, 0.15807,
  base: 786`).
- §2.2 The transactions ledger pairs 14 of those fills and leaves 6 SELLs
  unpaired: 0.13873, 0.14242, 0.14621, 0.1501, 0.15409, 0.15819. Their net is
  786 short at a cash-weighted 0.148290; the true close leg is
  786 × (0.148290 − 0.15807) = **−7.68708**.
- §2.3 Replaying the 34 fills through the `calculateAbstractPosition` fold gives
  `position.price` 0.162 at the close, so the booked close leg was
  786 × (0.162 − 0.15807) × (1 − maker) = **+3.0884** — exactly the gap between
  the stored `profit.total` (+10.8594) and the paired-transaction sum.
- §2.4 Net over the bot's life: bought 2620 for 414.47745, sold 2620 for
  414.62417. The report overstated the result by ≈10.78 USDT on a ~300 USDT bot.

## §3 Assumption (root cause, hypothesis)

The close leg of a NEUTRAL futures grid is valued against
`data.position.price` instead of the cost of the fills the round-trip ledger
left unpaired. Valuing it against the unpaired fills' net cash makes §1.1 hold;
nothing else in §2 needs explaining.

## §4 Fix

§4.1 Before the close leg of a NEUTRAL, non-COIN-M futures grid is booked,
derive its entry from the ledger: the bot's FILLED regular orders since the
last close (`updateTime` after the previous stop fill and after
`lastPositionChange` — the same slice `loadOrders` rebuilds the position from),
minus every fill a paired transaction (`idBuy` and `idSell` both set) already
booked. The entry price is that set's net cash divided by the position quantity.

§4.2 The unpaired set is used only when its signed net quantity equals the
signed position being closed. Otherwise — or if either read fails — the close
falls back to `position.price`, today's behaviour, and says so in the log.

§4.3 LONG/SHORT futures grids, COIN-M grids, spot grids, the transactions rows
users see and the `(1 − maker)` factor on the close are unchanged.

## §5 Not in scope

§5.1 The `valueChanged` TP/SL trigger (`checkTpSl`, spec 064) marks an open
neutral position at `position.price` too, so it is off by the same amount while
the bot runs. That changes when orders fire, not what is booked, and is left
for its own issue.
