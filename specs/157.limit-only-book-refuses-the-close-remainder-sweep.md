# 157 — A limit-only book refuses the close's remainder sweep and the deal closes anyway

Bug spec (Definition / Evidence / Assumption). Tests:
`src/bot/limitOnlyCloseRemainder.spec.ts`.

## §1 Definition

When a DCA take-profit closes only part of a deal's position — typically a
partly filled LIMIT take-profit that a close request cancels, which
`cancelOrderOnExchange` promotes to `FILLED` — `closeDeal` books the deal
closed and then `sellRemainder` sells what is left with a MARKET order
(`D-SR-…`).

**Expected.**

- §1.1 If the venue refuses that MARKET sweep because the book is in limit-only
  mode (`limitOnlyReasons`, `limitOnlyEntry.ts`), the deal is NOT closed: its
  status stays `open`, no close profit is written to the deal, the bot or the
  profit history, and `processDealClose` is not called.
- §1.2 The take-profit's fill is kept as a partial take profit (`tpHistory`),
  so the deal's next close counts it exactly once.
- §1.3 The rest of the position — exactly what the refused sweep carried — is
  then offered as one LIMIT take-profit at the latest price, so the deal
  closes, and books its profit, only when that sell fills, through the ordinary
  take-profit close. It never stacks on another close: a take-profit another
  close request rested meanwhile is replaced (cancelled as a re-size, its fills
  kept as a partial take profit). A refusal of that LIMIT is reported and goes
  to the existing refused-close recovery.
- §1.4 Unchanged: a sweep the venue accepts is booked exactly as before, and
  sent exactly once.
- §1.5 Unchanged: a sweep refused for any other reason behaves as before — the
  deal closes, the refusal is reported, nothing is retried.

**Actual.** The deal is written `closed` before the sweep is sent. The refusal
is logged as a warning and nothing else happens: the remaining coins stay in
the user's account, tracked by no deal, and the deal's "realized" profit values
them at the take-profit price as if they had been sold.

## §2 Evidence (prod, read-only)

- §2.1 A Coinbase spot DCA deal, closed by the user with *close by limit*.
  `Gainium-DCA-bot`, 2026-10-13:
  - `12:16:43.292Z Сlose deal …` (API `closeDCADeal … "closeByLimit"`).
  - `12:16:43.730Z TP order FILLED` — the resting LIMIT take-profit
    `D-TP-oMrP…` (54,950,024 placed, 51,694,544 filled) was cancelled and
    promoted.
  - `12:16:44.149Z [WARN] … Reason Orderbook is in limit only mode - please use
    limit order type Method limitOrders() Step Send new order request D-SR-…,
    qty 72699924, price 0.00001519, side SELL`.
  - `12:16:44.171Z` the bot moves on to the next deal.
- §2.2 The deal row: `status: closed`, `sellRemainder: true`,
  `currentBalances.base: 72699924`, `profit.total: -714.61`, which is
  `quote delta + 72699924 × 0.00001519 − commission` — the unsold coins valued
  at the take-profit price. The `D-SR-…` order row is `MARKET, CANCELED,
  executedQty 0`.

## §3 Assumption (root cause — hypothesis)

`closeDeal`'s take-profit branch commits the close (status, profit, bot
profit, profit history) before the remainder sweep is attempted, and
`sellRemainder` sends only a MARKET order and has no answer to a refusal. A
limit-only book can never accept a MARKET order, so the sweep is guaranteed to
fail while the close is already written. Sending the sweep BEFORE the close is
committed, and handing a limit-only refusal back to the close path instead of
committing, removes the failure; a test driving the real `closeDeal` with a
sweep refused as in §2.1 must observe `status: closed` today and `open` after.

Not in scope: why the take-profit in §2.1 was sized short of the position (a
reduce-funds entry left pending after its order was cancelled). That is a
separate defect with its own fix; this spec only makes sure a remainder that
does exist is sold rather than written off.
