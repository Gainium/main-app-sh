# 293 — A restart re-closes a deal its own close had kept open, and books unsold coins as profit

Bug spec (Definition / Evidence / Assumption). Tests:
`src/bot/closeSweepShortfall.spec.ts` §6, §6.5, §7.

## §1 Definition

A spot LONG DCA deal's take profit FILLS in full but is smaller than the
position. `closeDeal` sends a market sweep of the rest first (spec `157`). When
the venue refuses it and the coins are still held (limit-only book, spec `157`;
the deal's own resting take profit, spec `211`; other orders locking them, spec
`267` `locked`), the deal stays open: the fill is booked in `tpHistory` as a
partial take profit and the rest is closed by limit.

**Expected.**

- §1.1 After a bot restart, `restoreWork` leaves such a deal open: every FILLED
  take profit of it is already in `tpHistory` (for at least its executed
  quantity) and the deal still holds more base than the venue minimum after
  those fills. The limit close carries on as before the restart.
- §1.2 Unchanged: a deal whose take profit sold the whole position still closes
  on restart exactly as today. Such a take profit is never in `tpHistory` before
  its close; a partial-fill progress row of it is smaller than the fill, or
  leaves nothing held. A later take profit not yet booked, a lost ledger, or
  dust below the minimum also close as today.
- §1.3 When a close does end short (spec `267` `unsold`, `unsoldBase` stamped),
  `profit.total` (and so `totalUsd`) counts only what the sold coins realised.
  The unsold coins stay at the deal's average cost: the formula no longer values
  them at the close price. `pureBase` still records them. Closes with nothing
  unsold book exactly the same profit as before.

**Actual.** `restoreWork` read the FILLED take profit as "the deal is done" and
called `closeDeal` again. That re-ran the refused sweep, which now fell into
the spec `267` `unsold` branch, and closed the deal with the rest unsold. Its
profit priced the unsold coins as if sold at the take-profit price.

## §2 Evidence (prod logs, read-only, 2026-11-27)

A Kraken XRP spot LONG deal bought 508.07 XRP. Its take profit of 262.93
filled; the sweep of 245.13 was refused and the bot logged "… is locked by
other open orders … Deal stays open, closing the rest by limit" (spec `267`
`locked`). The limit close was refused for balance every hour after that.
After a service restart (a new worker PID), `restoreWork` logged "Found filled
tp … change deal status to closed", then "No profit in deal, process full
close". Three seconds later the deal closed with "take profit sold 262.931127
of 508.065008 XRP, 245.133881 XRP unsold", and the profit was positive though
nearly half the position was never sold.

## §3 Assumption (hypothesis, confirmed by the red tests)

The restart check predates "FILLED take profit, deal deliberately open"
(introduced by spec `157` and widened by spec `267`). It only excused take
profits the venue under-filled (`underfilledTpQty`). A take profit booked as a
partial by `closeDeal` is the marker that the close already ran and kept the
deal open. The profit formula's `(cur.base − init.base) × lastPrice` term values
whatever base is left as sold; spec `267`'s own user message says those coins
are not in the profit.

Red before the fix: §6.5 (`restoreWork` re-closes the deal), §7.1 (profit
8.6262 instead of the realised 5.7152).

## Invariants

The existing `dcadeals.closed-spot-long-keeps-base` (main-app
`specs/invariants/deals.invariants.json`) skips stamped `unsoldBase` closes. A
restart re-close leaves a stamped close behind, so a new observe-mode invariant
`dcadeals.unsold-close-books-unsold-as-profit` flags a stamped close whose
profit still includes the unsold coins at the close price.
