# 261 — A spot hedge DCA bot's short leg sells the coins its long leg holds

Bug spec (Definition / Evidence / Assumption).

## §1 Definition

§1.1 A hedge DCA bot on a spot venue runs a LONG and a SHORT leg on the same pair, from one
account. The long leg buys the coin and holds it (`currentBalances.base`) until its take profit
sells it. The short leg sells the coin first (base order, then safety orders) and buys it back.

§1.2 Nothing on the venue locks the long leg's coins while its take profit is not resting:
smart orders place it only near its price, and a refused or re-priced take profit is off the
book too. Outside spec 044 (hedge legs with a start action, stamped `baseFundingBeta`), no
balance check subtracts them. So the short leg's new-deal check and every one of its sells see
the long leg's coins as free, and spend them.

§1.3 Actual: the long leg's take profit is refused for balance on every attempt, for as long as
the deal stays open, while its deal shows a large unrealised profit.

§1.4 Expected: a spot hedge short leg (no hedge start action, not a spec 044 deal) treats the
long leg's coins as held. A deal it cannot fund from the rest is not opened (the existing
new-deal "Not enough balance" refusal); a base or safety sell that does not fit is refused as
not enough balance before it reaches the venue, through the existing not-enough-balance report.
The long leg's coins that its own resting sells already lock are not counted twice.

§1.5 Unchanged: futures hedge bots, spec 044 deals (stamped, or with a hedge start action),
ordinary (non-hedge) spot bots, the long leg's own orders, and the short leg's buys. What is a
spec 044 deal is decided per deal (its stamp or its action), never by the user's base-funding
gate: that gate is released to everyone, so excluding "users the gate allows" excludes every
bot (§3.6).

## §2 Evidence

- Data: on one live spot hedge bot, the long deal holds 1.66 coins and the account holds none.
  The short deal's venue fills are all SELLs, 2.60 in total, opened five days after the long
  stopped buying.
- Runtime: the long leg's take-profit refusals print the account's balance. Its total drops by
  exactly each short safety-order fill (−0.16, then −0.15, within the hour of each fill), from
  2.609 before the short opened to 0. No other movement of the coin appears in between.
- Code: `checkBalance` subtracts only `reservedFundingBase` (stamped deals) from free base;
  `sendOrderToExchange` sends a SELL the venue will accept. Neither knows the sibling leg.

## §3 Assumption (hypothesis)

The short leg's sells are funded from free base that includes the long leg's held coins,
because neither the new-deal gate nor the order site subtracts them. Tests must fail because a
sell that only the long leg's coins cover is sent to the venue, and because the new-deal gate
passes on them.

§3.1 The fit: `free base − held by the long leg ≥ qty` (pure, `hedgeShortSellFits`).
§3.2 The read: Σ `currentBalances.base` of the hedge's open, unstamped LONG deals, less the
base their resting take profits lock (`FundingReservation.heldByHedgeLongLegs`); unreadable is
`null`, not 0.
§3.3 The order site: a short leg's `dealStart` / `dealRegular` SELL that does not fit is refused
locally as not enough balance (served locally: the write-ahead row is taken back, nothing is
recorded as a venue refusal); decided on stored balances and confirmed live before refusing; an
unreadable read sends the order as before. The reported "free" is net of the long's coins.
§3.4 The new-deal gate: `checkBalance` subtracts the same amount; unreadable is an unknown
balance (the deal is not opened this cycle), as for `reservedFundingBase`.
§3.5 What does not change: §1.5.
§3.6 Revision: the first implementation also skipped users the spec 044 gate allows. Since
the gate was released to everyone that is every user, so neither §3.3 nor §3.4 ever ran in
production (a short safety sell covered only by the long's coins still reached the venue and was
refused there). The tests stubbed the gate to "not allowed", which hid it; they now run with the
real gate (released) and no stub.
§3.7 Revision: §3.3 checked each sell on its own, against a balance read once, and skipped the
live read whenever the stored balance said the sell fit. Sells checked together (a deal opening's
base sell and every safety sell, sent in one burst) each fit the spare and together spent up to
N times it, and the stored balance does not show a sell for as long as the user stream takes. Now
each accepted sell is reserved (per bot, per coin) until its send ends; a live read that began
after a send ended reflects it, a pending one is subtracted from the free base. The decision and
the reservation follow the balance read with no await in between, so concurrent checks share one
spare. While any short sell of the coin is pending or ended within the last 5 minutes, the stored
balance is not trusted to pass a sell and the live read decides. A single sell with none recent
is decided exactly as before.

## Invariants

- An open spot hedge LONG leg's open deals hold more of their coin than the account holds
  (`main-app/deals` pack, `dca.hedge-spot-long-leg-holds-more-than-account`, observe).
