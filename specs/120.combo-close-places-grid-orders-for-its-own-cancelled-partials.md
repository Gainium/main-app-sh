# 120 — A combo deal close places grid orders for its own cancelled partial fills

Bug spec (Definition / Evidence / Assumption). Tests:
`src/bot/combo/closeInFlightGrid.harness.spec.ts`.

## §1 Definition

§1.1 Expected. Once a close is requested for an open combo deal (close by
market or by limit, from the user, TP, SL or the base minigrid), no grid
order is placed for that deal while the close is running, or while its
closing order is still live in this worker. A grid order that turns out to be
partly filled when the close cancels it is still recorded: the minigrid's
transaction count, profit, fee and ladder, and the deal's balances and size.

§1.2 Actual. The fill goes through `updateMinigrid`, which rebuilds the
minigrid ladder at the cancelled order's price and sends the difference to
the venue. Its only gate is `deal.closeBySl`, which a manual close never
sets. So the bot places fresh grid BUYs and SELLs in the middle of a close.
A BUY can land above the market and fill at once, adding to the position the
close is selling. A SELL can ask for base the close order already holds, and
the venue refuses it with "Not enough balance".

§1.3 Out of scope, unchanged: how the close itself sells the position (the
TP order, its sizing, retries and reposition), and how filled or partly
filled grid orders are counted in the deal's size.

## §2 Evidence

Production, Kraken spot SN64-USD long combo, pm2 `Gainium-Combo-bot`:

- 2026-09-28 18:57:12.745: close of a deal starts. Four grid orders come
  back CANCELED with partial fills (SELL 22.585 base 0.9, SELL 22.472 base
  1.99966, SELL 21.885, BUY 20.118). 13.070: the limit close order rests at
  21.652. 13.667: about 10 grid BUYs at 21.985–22.492 are placed, above a
  21.65 market, and fill at 21.646 (about 25 SN64 bought during the close).
  Each fill triggers another rebuild. The close is reposted, and at 54.882 a
  grid SELL is refused with "Not enough balance".
- 2026-09-29 13:56:24.794: close starts. SELL 22.539 is CANCELED with base
  0.30436 and goes through `Update minigrid`. 25.159: the close order rests.
  25.791: a new grid BUY 22.376 is placed. 26.367: grid SELL 22.603 is
  refused, "Not enough balance … required 1.98381 SN64".

Code path. `closeDealById` sets `allowToPlaceOrders(dealId) = false`, awaits
`cancelAllOrder(0, dealId, true)`, then deletes the flag straight away.
`cancelOrderOnExchange` promotes a part-filled CANCELED order to FILLED, and
`handleUnknownOrder` → `processFilledOrder` → `updateMinigrid` runs
un-awaited (fire-and-forget) behind a mutex. By the time it reaches
`placeOrders`, the flag has been deleted, `closeBySl` is false and the close
order is resting. Nothing refuses the placement.

## §3 Assumption (root cause)

The only "close in flight" signal `updateMinigrid` reads is `closeBySl`. The
close path's own gate (`allowToPlaceOrders`) is scoped to the cancel call,
not to the close. Fills that call spawns are processed after the gate has
lifted.

## §4 Required behaviour

§4.1 While a combo `closeDealById` (market or limit, deal `open`) is running,
`updateMinigrid` for that deal places nothing.

§4.2 After it returns with the deal still open and its close order (dealTP)
live in this worker, `updateMinigrid` for that deal places nothing.

§4.3 In both cases the fill is still accounted: minigrid transactions,
profit, ladder and status are updated exactly as before.

§4.4 A close that ends with no live close order and the deal still open
(refused or abandoned) stops blocking. Later grid fills place orders as
before. The block needs no clean-up to lift.

§4.5 Deals with no close requested behave exactly as before.
