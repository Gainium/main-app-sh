# 235 — A filled MARKET entry waits for a user-stream report

Bug spec (Definition / Evidence / Assumption). Code: `src/bot/main.ts`
(`sendOrderToExchange` placement-answer save, `processOrderQueue`),
`src/bot/comboHelper.ts` (`placeBaseOrder`), new `src/bot/marketEntryFill.ts`.

## §1 Definition

§1.1 Expected. A Combo deal whose MARKET base order the venue has executed in
full opens within seconds — it is booked, and its take-profit and grid are
placed — whether or not the user stream ever reports the fill.

§1.2 Actual. When the placement answer is not `FILLED` — in the observed case
a Kraken Futures MARKET entry answered `PARTIALLY_FILLED` with its whole size
executed — the row is saved as such, and Combo `placeBaseOrder` only opens the
deal on a `FILLED` answer. A Combo entry arms no base-order timer, so the only
thing left that can move the deal is a user-stream report. When the stream is
silent, the deal stays in `start` with an unbooked position and no take-profit
until the stream reconnects and the reconnect pass asks the venue.

§1.3 Not changing. How or when Combo places, cancels or re-places a base
order. A genuinely part-filled MARKET order (executed < requested), any LIMIT
order, and coin-margined accounts (whose figures are converted through a price)
keep today's handling. The re-check (§4.3) only reads the venue and books what
it reports FILLED; a lookup that gets no answer never writes the order off.

## §2 Evidence

§2.1 Production, Kraken Futures Combo bot: the deal was created and its base
order sent at 12:52:35Z; `Save order <base>` at 12:52:37Z; no further line for
the order, the deal, or any stream message of the bot until
`Subscribed to user … / Check order after user stream reconnect` at 18:58:23Z,
same worker pid. The reconnect pass then read the order (`FILLED`) and the
deal opened (`Base order FILLED`, minigrids created) at 18:58:30Z — 6h06m with
a live position and no take-profit. The sentinel invariant
`deals.start-market-entry-row-unsettled` sampled the row meanwhile as
`PARTIALLY_FILLED 310/310`. Two earlier Combo deals on the same venue showed
the same stall (196 and 48 minutes).

§2.2 Code. `placeBaseOrder` (Combo) branches on `result.status === 'FILLED'`;
any other answer goes to `checkUnfilledBaseEntryCancel`, which acts only on
`CANCELED`. The `PARTIALLY_FILLED`-for-its-full-size → `FILLED` upgrade in
`processOrderQueue` is Bybit-only and absent from the placement-answer save.

## §3 Assumption (hypothesis)

Two gaps, either sufficient for the observed stall: (a) a MARKET order's
`PARTIALLY_FILLED` status is believed even when executed ≥ requested, on the
placement answer and on non-Bybit stream reports; (b) nothing re-reads a
Combo MARKET entry from the venue unless the stream speaks. Why Kraken
Futures answers `PARTIALLY_FILLED` for a full fill is a connector question,
not settled here (no connector log line exists for the order).

## §4 Tests — `src/bot/marketEntryFullFill.spec.ts`

- §4.1 Placement answer: a MARKET `PARTIALLY_FILLED` answer with executed ≥
  requested is saved and returned `FILLED`; a real part-fill and a LIMIT keep
  their status (§1.3).
- §4.2 Stream report: the same shape reaches the filled callback on any
  non-coin-margined venue; a real part-fill still reaches the part-filled one.
- §4.3 Combo re-check: `marketEntryRecheckDelayMs` after a MARKET entry the
  venue accepted (real exchange id) whose answer was not `FILLED`, if the row
  is still `NEW`/`PARTIALLY_FILLED` and the deal still `start`, the order is
  read through `_handleUnknownOrder(…, justPlaced = true)`; a `FILLED` answer
  goes through `handleUnknownOrder` → `processFilledOrder` → `startDeal`.
  Not armed for a LIMIT, a `-1` id or a deal past `start`; nothing asked when
  the stream already settled the row; re-armed while the venue still reports
  a part-fill, at most `marketEntryRecheckMaxAttempts` times; cleared by
  `clearClassProperties`.

## Invariants

No new invariant: the state this leaves is exactly what the existing
`deals.start-market-entry-row-unsettled` (main-app pack `deals`) detects, and
it is what raised this defect.
