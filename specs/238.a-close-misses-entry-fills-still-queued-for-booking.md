# 238 — A take-profit close counts entry fills still queued for booking

Bug spec.

## §1 Definition

### §1.1 Expected

- **§1.1.1** When a DCA take-profit fills, every entry fill of the deal the
  venue has already reported as FILLED is part of the position the close
  measures, whether or not its own `updateDeal` has run yet. A take-profit
  that bought back (short) or sold (long) the whole position leaves no
  remainder, and no `D-SR` market order is sent for it — neither by the close
  nor afterwards by the queued `updateDeal` of those fills.
- **§1.1.2** The closed deal's ledger (`currentBalances`) and booked profit
  include those fills at their own fill price.
- **§1.1.3** Unchanged: a take-profit that closed LESS than the filled
  position still leaves a remainder that is swept at market. An entry fill the
  venue reports after the close is still sold back per fill (spec `112`).
- **§1.1.4** Unchanged: if the close does not commit (the pre-close sweep is
  refused and the deal stays open, specs `157`/`211`), queued fills are left
  to their own `updateDeal`, exactly as before.

### §1.2 Actual (before)

- **§1.2.1** Several safety orders fill in one venue message. Each is marked
  in-flight (`ordersInBetweenUpdates`) and queued on the deal's `updateDeal`
  lock; only the first is booked into `currentBalances` before the take-profit
  — sized from every FILLED row, so it covers all of them — fills. `closeDeal`
  measures the position from `currentBalances`, which is missing the queued
  fills. It books their base as held and their proceeds as never received.
- **§1.2.2** The queued `updateDeal`s then run, find the deal closed, and send
  each fill to `sellRemainder` as a late entry fill. On a spot short that is a
  market BUY of coins the take-profit already bought back: the account ends
  holding them and the deal books a loss.

## §2 Evidence

- **§2.1** Production, Binance spot SHORT TIAUSDT, no stop loss. One user-stream
  message carried three safety-order fills (32, 81.92, 51.2 base, SELL). The
  engine logged `Regular order FILLED` for the 32 only, then the take-profit:
  `FILLED, 0.4089, base: 205.12 … BUY` = 20 + 20 + 32 + 51.2 + 81.92, the whole
  position. Then two `D-SR` BUYs: 81.92 (refused, not enough balance) and
  51.2 (FILLED @ 0.4095). A second deal of the same bot repeated it 28 minutes
  later (SR BUY 32 filled).
- **§2.2** The closed deal's row: `size` 205.12, `initialBalances.base` 545.91,
  `currentBalances.base` 679.03. 679.03 − 545.91 + (20 + 20 + 32) = 205.12: the
  ledger booked the take-profit's 205.12 against only 72 sold. The missing
  133.12 is exactly the two queued fills.
- **§2.3** `closeDeal`'s two remainder reads (`preRemainder` before the close,
  `remainder` after) are both derived from `currentBalances`; on this ledger
  both are negative, so neither swept. The sweeps came from `updateDeal`'s
  closed-deal branch (`sellRemainder(…, lateEntryFill = true)`).

## §3 Assumption (root cause, hypothesis)

- **§3.1** The take-profit and the close disagree about what the position is:
  the take-profit is sized from FILLED rows, the close from a ledger that only
  holds fills whose `updateDeal` already ran. Folding the deal's FILLED,
  still-queued, non-add-funds entry fills into the ledger at close (and marking
  them booked in `dealUpdateOrders`, so their queued `updateDeal` returns
  "already processed") makes the existing remainder math measure the real
  position. No new remainder formula is needed.

## §4 Fix

- **§4.1** `closeDeal` (take-profit path, DCA only — combo sizes and books from
  its own rebuilt ledger) reads the queued entry fills before the pre-close
  sweep and measures `preRemainder` from the ledger plus them.
- **§4.2** Once the close is committed (after the keep-open refusals), it adds
  them to `currentBalances`, removes them from `ordersInBetweenUpdates` and
  records them in `dealUpdateOrders`. Re-read at that point, so a queued
  `updateDeal` that ran meanwhile is not counted twice.
- **§4.3** Only on a usable ledger (finite, not 0/0); a lost ledger is rebuilt
  from every filled row by the existing spec `024` path, as before.

## Invariants

- **§5.1** A spot SHORT DCA deal that closed does not book base above its
  allocation (`currentBalances.base - initialBalances.base`) beyond what its
  profit keeps in base. Pack: main-app `specs/invariants/deals.invariants.json`
  `dcadeals.closed-spot-short-keeps-base` (observe).
