# 105 — A Combo reload duplicates a safety order whose ladder price moved

A Combo deal's safety ladder is rebuilt from its start price whenever the bot
loads the deal from the database: on a settings save, or on a restart that
cannot restore the deal from Redis. The restart order check then compares that
rebuilt ladder with the orders resting on the exchange. When a level's price
comes out different from the order already resting at that level, the deal ends
up with two orders at that level.

## §1 Definition

### §1.1 Expected

- **§1.1.1** After the restart order check, an open Combo deal has at most one
  resting safety order (`dealRegular`) per ladder level (`dcaLevel`). A level
  that already rests an order is left alone, even when the rebuilt ladder prices
  that level differently.
- **§1.1.2** A level that has no resting order is still placed. The check keeps
  repairing a ladder that is missing a level.
- **§1.1.3** Orders that already match the rebuilt ladder by price are neither
  placed again nor cancelled, as today.

### §1.2 Actual

- **§1.2.1** `checkOrders` (restart branch) places every entry of the diff's
  `new` side and ignores its `cancel` side. Combo's `getDiffForCheckOrders`
  matches rebuilt levels to resting orders by exact price. A level that moved by
  one tick is therefore placed again, and the order already resting at that
  level is never cancelled. The level ends up with two orders and roughly twice
  the capital the user configured.

## §2 Evidence

Production, 2026-09-24.

- A Coinbase SOL-EUR Combo deal opened 2026-09-23 13:47Z with a base fill at
  102.35. Its safety orders rested at 98.26, 94.17, 90.08 and 85.99
  (`dcaLevel` 2–5), priced by the per-level rounding that shipped before
  2026-09-24.
- On 2026-09-24 the ladder builder stopped carrying each level's rounding into
  the next (`createInitialDealOrders`, the Combo copy of the same change made
  for DCA). Rebuilt from 102.35, the same ladder is 98.26, 94.16, 90.07, 85.97.
- At 18:27:36Z the user saved a settings change. The worker logged `Reload bot`,
  `Checking for existing deals` and `Get order from redis …` for each resting
  order. Then, before `Found 1 open deals` (so inside `checkOrders`, not in
  `restoreWork`'s own placement), it placed 94.16, 90.07 and 85.97. The orders
  collection now holds two NEW `dealRegular` rows each at `dcaLevel` 3, 4 and 5.
- A second deal (Kraken, a different user) shows the same shape at levels 5, 6
  and 7. Those were placed at 09:39Z, right after the workers restarted to
  deploy that change. A scan of all NEW `CMB-RO` orders finds these two deals and
  nothing else.
- A cold restart that restores the deal from Redis keeps the ladder it had, so
  it does not diverge. The same bot's 09:32Z restart placed nothing.

## §3 Assumption (hypothesis)

The duplicate is placed by `checkOrders` → Combo `getDiffForCheckOrders` →
`findDiff`, because `findDiff` pairs a rebuilt level with a resting order only
on an exact price. Nothing on that path pairs them by level, and nothing acts on
the unmatched resting order. Any change to how the ladder is priced (a rounding
fix, a precision update from the exchange) reproduces this for deals opened
before the change.

## §4 Change

- **§4.1** In Combo's `getDiffForCheckOrders`, drop from `new` any `dealRegular`
  level for which a resting `dealRegular` order of the same deal, side and
  `dcaLevel` was passed in. Keep the resting order. It is the deal's live order
  for that level. Fills settle by the order's own `dcaLevel`. The fill and
  minigrid paths already cancel and replace it against the ladder when they next
  reprice.
- **§4.2** Levels without a `dcaLevel` on either side keep today's price match.
- **§4.3** The ladder rounding itself is unchanged.

## §5 Out of scope

- **§5.1** Duplicates already resting on the two affected deals. The fix stops
  new ones. It does not cancel the extra order at a level, because which of the
  two the user has already cancelled on the venue is not known here.
- **§5.2** DCA's `getDiffForCheckOrders`. DCA ladder levels carry no `dcaLevel`,
  so this pairing is not available there.
