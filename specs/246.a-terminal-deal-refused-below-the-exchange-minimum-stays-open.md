# 246 — A terminal deal refused below the exchange minimum stays open

## 1. Definition

### 1.1 — expected

A terminal trade is a one-shot bot: it opens one deal, and it stops when that
deal ends or cannot open. A terminal deal that the exchange-minimum refusal
(`refuseDealBelowExchangeMin`) turns down stops its bot, as a terminal deal
turned down for balance (`openNewDealBody`) or refused by the venue on its
base order (`placeBaseOrder`) already does. The stopped bot is `closed` and
releases its bot credits. The refusal itself is reported to the user as
before: a `Bot warning` event and a bot message naming the minimum and the
configured size.

### 1.2 — actual

When the base order of a new terminal deal was below the exchange minimum and
the trade did not allow raising it, `openNewDealBody` returned at the refusal
with nothing else done. The terminal bot stayed `open` with no deal
(`deals.all: 0`). It kept its bot credits locked, no list of bots, deals or
positions showed it, and the user had no way to cancel it there. On a worker
restart it tried again, was refused again and stayed open.

### 1.3 — unchanged

- A regular DCA, combo or hedge bot refused on a pair keeps running; the pair
  opens no deal and the rest of the bot trades on.
- The refusal decision, its message and its per-pair latch.
- A terminal trade that allows raising the order to the minimum.

## 2. Tests — `src/bot/terminalDealBelowExchangeMin.spec.ts`

- §2.1 a refused terminal deal stops its bot.
- §2.2 a refused regular DCA deal does not stop its bot.
- §2.3 a terminal deal that is not refused opens and does not stop.

## 3. Evidence

Prod logs for four terminal trades created 2026-11-15 on Binance USDⓈ-M with
base orders of 2–10 USDT and `allowRaiseToExchangeMin: false`: each logged
`Open new deal <pair>` then `Deal refused on <pair>: 1 order(s) below
exchange minimum` within about a second, and nothing further until the next
worker restart repeated the refusal. The bots read `status: open`,
`deals.all: 0`, `cost: 10`. Trades of 20 and 50 USDT placed in the same
minutes opened normally.

## 4. Assumption (root cause)

The exchange-minimum refusal was extended to terminal deals without the
terminal stop that the other final refusals of `openNewDealBody` carry.
Confirmed by §2.1 failing (0 stops) before the change and passing after it.

## Invariants

- `bots.terminal-open-without-deal` (main-app `specs/invariants/deals.invariants.json`,
  observe): a terminal `dcabots` row `open` with `deals.all: 0` for more than
  15 minutes.
