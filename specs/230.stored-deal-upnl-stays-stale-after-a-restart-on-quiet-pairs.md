# 230 — The stored deal uPnL stays stale after a restart on quiet pairs

Template: bug spec (Definition / Evidence / Assumption). Follow-up to spec 229.

The dashboard sorts open deals server-side by the stored fee-inclusive uPnL
(`stats.unrealizedProfitNet` / `stats.unrealizedPercentNet`, main-app spec 019
§5) and shows each row priced live. Spec 229 made a window write the sample
that closed it. A value stored before that fix, or stored from an older price,
is still only replaced when the deal is sampled **twice** after a load. A deal
is sampled only when its pair's price changes, so on a pair that has not
traded since the restart the stale value stays indefinitely and sorts the deal
out of place.

## §1 Definition

### §1.1 Expected

- **§1.1.1** The first sample a process takes of an open deal writes the deal
  when its stored uPnL is stale: the measured `unrealizedProfitNet` differs
  from the stored one and the stored one is older than `NET_STATS_REFRESH_MS`
  (or absent). This replaces spec 229 §1.1.2's "never stored" condition, which
  is the special case "absent". A first sample that only finds a new extreme
  keeps the normal 30 s window (no double write).
- **§1.1.2** After a bot loads, every open deal gets one stats sample, priced
  from the latest price the bot already holds for its pair (`lastStreamData`,
  filled by the stream or by `getLatestPrice` during load). The sample comes
  from the price timer, the path both `start()` implementations arm, and adds
  no exchange request. A deal whose pair has no known price yet is sampled by
  the REST fallback poll in the same timer run, as before.
- **§1.1.3** A deal already sampled since the load (stream tick, REST poll or
  the seed) is not seeded again. The seed runs outside the price timer's
  per-exchange mutex.
- **§1.1.4** Unchanged: the stats payload sent for DCA and for combo deals,
  the 30 s window, the 10 min refresh, the flush on close.

### §1.2 Actual

- **§1.2.1** A deal's first sample after a load only opens a window
  (`time - start` is 0), so a stale stored value is not written. The second
  sample needs a price change. On a quiet pair it never comes.
- **§1.2.2** On a pair whose price the bot already holds at load, the REST
  fallback poll returns early in `priceUpdateCallback` because the price is
  unchanged, so the deal is not sampled at all.

## §2 Evidence

- One deal on a paper Kraken pair stored its uPnL at the trade that wrote it,
  priced at the trade before (the spec 229 lag). The public tape shows no trade
  on the pair since. 20 min after the bot workers restarted with spec 229,
  the stored value was unchanged.
- Of 118 open paper Kraken deals of one account still carrying a pre-restart
  value, 109 sat on pairs with no public trade since the restart. The other 9
  had 1–5 trades, which is consistent with §1.2.1: one sample, window opened,
  nothing written.

## §3 Assumption (root cause)

Writing a deal's stored uPnL needs two samples after a load (§1.2.1), and a
quiet pair produces at most one, or none (§1.2.2). The fix lets the first
sample write a stale value, and gives every open deal that first sample at
load from the price the bot already holds.

## §4 Tests

| § | Test |
|---|---|
| §1.1.1 | `src/bot/dealStatsCurrentSample.spec.ts`: a stale stored deal is written on its first sample; a fresh one is not; a first-sample extreme is not written early |
| §1.1.2, §1.1.3 | `src/bot/dealStatsSeedOnLoad.spec.ts`: the price timer seeds each unsampled open deal once at the held price; a deal with no held price is left to the poll; a sampled deal is not seeded |

## Invariants

- An open DCA deal whose `stats.updatedAt` is older than the last bot-worker
  restart by more than the refresh interval, while its pair's current price
  implies a different stored uPnL. Not added as a pack entry: the sentinel has
  no price source, and spec 229's `deals.open-no-stored-stats` already covers
  the never-stored case. Verified post-deploy by the check in the plan.
