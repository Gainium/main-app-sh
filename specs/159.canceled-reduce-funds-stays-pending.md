# 159 — A cancelled reduce-funds order stays pending and undersizes the close

Bug spec (Definition / Evidence / Assumption). Tests:
`src/bot/dca/canceledReduceFunds.harness.spec.ts`.

## §1 Definition

A DCA deal's "reduce funds" with a limit price places a `dealTP` order carrying
`reduceFundsId`, and records the request in `deal.pendingReduceFunds` until it
is settled. `getTPOrder` sizes the deal's close net of every pending entry
(`getPendingReduceFunds`), net of `tpHistory` (partial take profits), and net
of executed `reduceFunds`.

**Expected.**

- §1.1 When the reduce order ends CANCELED — by the venue, or by the user — its
  entry leaves `pendingReduceFunds`.
- §1.2 What it executed before the cancel is a withdrawal: booked into
  `reduceFunds` (and out of `size`) like a filled reduce, not left in
  `tpHistory`, and counted once.
- §1.3 The deal's close is then sized for everything the deal still holds, and
  re-placed at that size.
- §1.4 Unchanged: when the BOT cancels a reduce order that is still pending (a
  reload, a deal settings update), the entry stays, so `resendPendingFunds`
  (spec 110) places it again.

**Actual.** Only a FILLED reduce order ever removed its entry. A venue-cancelled
one stayed pending for good, and its partial fill was also booked into
`tpHistory` (by `processCanceledOrder`, as for any take profit). Every later
close was sized `held − partial − whole pending quantity`: the partial was
subtracted twice, and the unfilled rest of the reduce was kept out of the close
though no order was selling it. Those coins had no close order at all.

A user's cancel of a resting reduce order (`cancelTerminalDealOrder`) filtered
`pendingReduceFunds` by `order.addFundsId` — always undefined on a reduce
order — so it left its entry too.

## §2 Evidence (prod, read-only, 2026-10-13)

Coinbase BLAST-USDC, spot long, zero fee, base step 1:

- Entries 155,913,933; one earlier reduce FILLED 9,868,421 → 146,045,512 held.
- A reduce of 1000 USDC @ 0.0000144 placed `D-TPR-…` origQty 69,444,444. The
  venue reported PARTIALLY_FILLED 21,651,044 and then CANCELED it ~1.5 s later,
  with no cancel request from the bot.
- Afterwards the deal document still held that entry in `pendingReduceFunds`,
  `reduceFunds` did not contain it, and `tpHistory` held the 21,651,044 partial.
- Every later close order was 54,950,024 = 146,045,512 − 21,651,044 −
  69,444,444 (rounded to nearest; zero-fee account). 69,444,444 coins stayed
  with no close order and were left over when the deal closed.

§2.1 `canceledReduceFunds.harness.spec.ts` reproduces 54,950,024 exactly from
that state with the real `getTPOrder`.

## §3 Assumption (root cause)

`processCanceledOrder` treats a cancelled reduce order like a cancelled take
profit — it books the partial into `tpHistory` and never touches
`pendingReduceFunds` — and nothing else settles a CANCELED reduce order. The
`cancelTerminalDealOrder` filter reads the wrong id field.

Test sections:

- §3.1 venue cancel with a partial fill → entry gone, partial in `reduceFunds`
  not `tpHistory`, close = 124,394,468 (146,045,512 − 21,651,044), re-placed;
  same whether or not a PARTIALLY_FILLED event came first.
- §3.2 venue cancel with nothing filled → entry gone, close = 146,045,512,
  re-placed.
- §3.3 the bot's own cancel of a still-pending reduce → entry kept (§1.4).
- §3.4 a user cancel via `cancelTerminalDealOrder` → entry gone.
- §3.5 a PARTIALLY_FILLED event processed after the settlement does not put the
  partial back into `tpHistory`.
