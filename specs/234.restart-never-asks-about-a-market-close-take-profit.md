# 234 — A restart never asks the venue about a market-close take-profit

Bug spec (Definition / Evidence / Assumption). Code: `src/bot/dcaHelper.ts`
`checkOrders` (the service-restart branch).

## §1 Definition

§1.1 Expected. When a bot worker restarts while a deal that closes at MARKET
holds a take-profit it already sent (`dealTP`, `NEW` or `PARTIALLY_FILLED` in
the restored order map), the restart pass asks the venue about that order,
the way it does for every other take-profit. A FILLED answer goes through the
normal fill path, which closes the deal. A PARTIALLY_FILLED answer is booked
as one.

§1.2 Actual. For a deal whose settings close at MARKET (the "TP level check"
deals, `isDealForTPLevelCheck`), the restart pass skips its take-profits
entirely. A market take-profit sent moments before the restart, whose fill
report reached the old process too late or not at all, stays `NEW` in memory
and in the `orders` collection. The deal stays open on a position the venue
already sold until something unrelated (a later close signal, a user-stream
reconnect) asks the venue about the order.

§1.3 Not changing. Which orders are placed and when. For a market-close deal
the restart pass still never places, re-places or retires a take-profit: a
CANCELED or not-found answer leaves the order as it was, exactly as today.
Only a fill the venue reports is acted on.

## §2 Evidence

§2.1 A DCA deal closed at MARKET by a close signal: the market take-profit's
write-ahead row was saved (orderId `-1`) and sent, the venue filled it at
once, and the worker restarted before handling the fill report. The new
worker's log shows the deal restored and TP-level-checked, and no lookup of
the take-profit's client order id until the next close signal's cancel, which
the venue answered `FILLED`. The deal stayed open on a sold position for that
whole interval.

§2.2 Code: `checkOrders` computes `forTPCheck = isDealForTPLevelCheck(deal)`
and guards the take-profit reconcile loop with `!forTPCheck`. Introduced with
market take-profits (v1.3.4), on the premise that a market-close deal never
rests a take-profit — true of a resting LIMIT order, not of a market order
in flight across a restart.

§2.3 The user-stream reconnect pass (`checkOrdersAfterReconnect`) asks about
every open order with no such filter, so the gap is the restart pass only.

## §3 Assumption (hypothesis)

The `!forTPCheck` guard on the reconcile loop is the whole cause: lifting it
for the lookup, while keeping every re-placement in that loop and the grid
comparison below it behind `!forTPCheck`, makes the restart pass recognise
the fill. The lookup itself (`getOrderForReconcile` → `getOrder`) already
handles a `-1` placeholder per venue (client-id lookup, spec 184), so no new
lookup path is needed.

## §4 Tests

`src/bot/restartMarketCloseTakeProfit.spec.ts`:

- §4.1 a FILLED answer is booked and goes to `processFilledOrder` (§1.1).
- §4.2 a PARTIALLY_FILLED answer is booked as one (§1.1).
- §4.3 a CANCELED answer places nothing and leaves the order as it was (§1.3).
- §4.4 a not-found answer retires nothing and places nothing (§1.3).
- §4.5 a LIMIT-close deal keeps today's behaviour (re-place on CANCELED).

## Invariants

The wrong state is already covered by main-app `specs/invariants/deals.invariants.json`
`orders.resting-limit-stored-as-market` (a MARKET order still NEW after 15
minutes), which is how this was found. No new invariant.
