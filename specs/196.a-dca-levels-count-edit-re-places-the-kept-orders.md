# 196 — A DCA levels count edit re-places the kept orders

Bug spec. Extends spec 195 (active smart orders count) to the deal's total
number of safety orders, in `src/bot/dcaHelper.ts`. Supersedes 195 §1.1.5 for
the total order count only.

## §1 Definition

### §1.1 Expected

- **§1.1.1** A patch whose only changed settings are the total safety-order
  count (`ordersCount`, or `dcaLevelsCap`, its form on an indicator / custom
  ladder, spec 118) and/or `activeOrdersCount` is a count-only edit. Changes
  are compared by value against the deal's effective settings, so the
  `useDca: true` that "Change DCA levels" always sends is not a change while
  DCA is already on. DCA only.
- **§1.1.2** Raising the total count cancels nothing. It places only the
  levels that now rest and did not before. With smart orders on and the
  nearest levels already resting, that is nothing at all. The take profit is
  not cancelled or placed again. `levels.all` follows the new count.
- **§1.1.3** Lowering it below the levels filled plus resting cancels only
  the resting levels the smaller ladder no longer holds.
- **§1.1.4** NOT CHANGING: switching DCA on or off, or an edit to order step,
  sizes, step scale or volume scale, alone or together with a count, still
  cancels and re-places the whole ladder. A partly filled resting level still
  falls back to the full rebuild (195 §1.1.4).

### §1.2 Actual

- **§1.2.1** `isActiveOrdersCountOnlyDealEdit` accepted `activeOrdersCount`
  alone. A "Change DCA levels" patch (`{ordersCount, useDca: true}`) took the
  full `rebuildDealOrders`: `cancelAllOrder` pulled the take profit and every
  resting safety order, then placed them again at the same prices and sizes.

## §2 Evidence

- Production: `updateDCADealSettings` with `{"ordersCount":40,"useDca":true}`
  on an open percentage-ladder DCA deal with three active smart orders, after
  the spec 195 fix was live. The take profit and the resting safety orders
  were cancelled, and within about two seconds the three safety orders and
  the take profit were placed again at identical prices and sizes.
- `createInitialDealOrders` builds level `i` from `i` and the levels before
  it (step × stepScale^(i-1), size × volumeScale^(i-1), custom row `i`,
  indicator level `i`). The count only decides how many levels are built.
  `createCurrentDealOrders` takes the deepest `ordersCount − filled` levels,
  then the nearest `activeOrdersCount`. `getTPOrder` does not read the count.

## §3 Assumption

Hypothesis: as in 195 §3, the full rebuild is the only reason the kept orders
are re-placed. The 195 diff (regenerated `dealRegular` levels against the
resting NEW ladder orders, by price and side) gives exactly the added or
dropped levels for a count change too, because no kept level's price moves.

## §4 Fix

- **§4.1** `isActiveOrdersCountOnlyDealEdit` accepts a non-empty change set
  inside `{activeOrdersCount, ordersCount, dcaLevelsCap}`
  (`ladderCountDealKeys`). Routing through `rebuildDealOrders({ activeOrdersOnly })`
  is unchanged from 195.

## §5 Tests

`src/bot/dca/activeOrdersCountDealEdit.harness.spec.ts`: detection of
`ordersCount` alone, with `activeOrdersCount`, in a whole form, and with
`useDca: true`. Not detected: switching DCA on or off, or a count with
step / size / scale changes (§1.1.1, §1.1.4). 8 → 12 with `useDca: true`
cancels and places nothing and sets `levels.all` (§1.1.2). 8 → 12 with all
levels active places only the four added levels (§1.1.2). 8 → 3 cancels only
the two dropped levels (§1.1.3). The NOT CHANGING list now includes
`ordersCount` + step / size / scale (§1.1.4).
`tpOnlyDealEdit.harness.spec.ts` "a ladder edit" now uses `step`.
`changeDcaLevelsIndicatorLadder.harness.spec.ts` (spec 118) keeps its
`levels` assertions over the count path. Its fake base gains `findDiff`.
