# 266 — A combo deal keeps re-sending closing grid levels the venue refuses because it holds less than the deal records

A futures combo deal is open. The exchange holds a position for it, but a
SMALLER one than the deal's own books say (`deal.currentBalances.base`). The
reduce-only closing-side grid levels already resting on the book cover the
whole venue position, and the exchange caps a symbol's total reduce-only
quantity at the position size. Kraken Futures refuses every further closing
level as `wouldNotReducePosition`.

Spec 175 asks the venue about the position after such a refusal, but it acts
only when the venue holds NOTHING. A smaller, non-zero position falls into its
"the position is there" branch, and nothing happens. The refused levels are
missing from the book, so the next grid diff (every take-profit recalculation,
reload or fill) sends the same levels again, and they are refused again. The
refusal subtype (`Futures position`) is `showUser: false`, so the user is never
told that the exchange holds less than the bot thinks.

## §1 Definition

### §1.1 Expected

- **§1.1.1** The exchange refuses a closing-side combo grid order (`dealGrid`,
  futures, reduce-only side) with a reason in `positionAlreadyClosedReasons`.
  Spec 175's probe runs as before. If it is answered and finds a position on the
  deal's side that is SMALLER than the absolute `deal.currentBalances.base`, that
  is a **shortfall**.
- **§1.1.2** On a shortfall the user gets ONE warning bot message for the deal
  (type `warning`, event `Deal`). It names the exchange, the quantity the exchange
  holds and the quantity the deal records. It also says that the refused levels
  are held back and that nothing about the deal was changed. Further shortfall
  readings for the same deal do not repeat it until a reading finds no shortfall
  (that ends the episode).
- **§1.1.3** On a shortfall, the refused levels are put on a per-deal **hold**,
  each identified by minigrid, side and price. While the hold is valid, the
  combo order placement drops those levels from what it sends. It sends
  everything else as before: opening-side levels, closing levels at other
  prices, safety orders and the take-profit.
- **§1.1.4** A closing-side level refused while the hold is valid joins the hold
  without another probe.
- **§1.1.5** The hold ends, and the levels are sent again as before, when any of
  these happens: the deal's recorded base or its completed-level count changes;
  a placement pass cancels a closing-side grid order of the deal (the resting
  reduce-only total changed); `GRID_SHORTFALL_HOLD_MS` passes; the bot reloads.
  The next refusal then probes again.
- **§1.1.6** Nothing about the deal's books changes: `currentBalances`,
  `avgPrice` and profit are not written by any of this. A probe that finds the
  venue holds at least the deal's base, or that is not answered, changes
  nothing. A flat venue goes to spec 175's close, unchanged.

### §1.2 Actual

- **§1.2.1** The probe's "venue still holds X" answer is logged at debug and
  dropped. No message, no hold: the refused levels go out again on every grid
  pass. Production on Kraken Futures: an ONDO-USD LONG combo deal recorded 198
  base against a 38 venue position (read 2026-11-23; 259 against 99 on
  2026-11-25). 258 `wouldNotReducePosition` refusals between 2026-11-14T15:44Z
  and 2026-11-23T16:14Z, each burst re-sending the same 8–9 levels, none shown
  to the user.

## §2 Evidence

- Production log, `Gainium-Combo-bot`: from 2026-11-14T15:44:45Z bursts of
  `Reason wouldNotReducePosition Method limitOrders() Step Send new order
  request CMB-GR-…, qty 16–19, price 0.3237–0.384, side SELL`, repeated with
  fresh client ids at each take-profit recalculation.
- Read as the owner (2026-11-25): the deal's FILLED orders net to 259 base, and
  `deal.currentBalances.base` = 259. The venue's ONDO-USD position (one-way) is
  LONG 99. The deal's resting grid SELLs are 21+20+20+20+18 = 99, exactly the
  venue position, so any further reduce-only SELL exceeds it.
- `comboHelper.settleFlatDealAfterGridRefusal` → `reconcileDealPosition`: its
  `!verdict.closeDeal` branch logs `venue still holds …` at debug and returns.

## §3 Assumption / out of scope

- Assumption: the refusals repeat only because the diff re-sends levels missing
  from the book, and the venue refuses them for as long as the shortfall lasts.
  Holding them back until the deal's position or levels change removes the
  repeats without changing what rests while the position is there.
- Out of scope: reconciling the deal's books to the venue (the deal's position,
  average price and profit are not rewritten), and where the gap came from.
  Read from the deal's own orders, sibling deals on the same one-way position
  over-sold it at their take-profit closes. That is part of the gap, not all of
  it, and needs the venue's fill history to settle.

## Invariants

None added. The wrong state is a venue-versus-books divergence that Mongo
alone cannot see. The deal records its base correctly from its own fills.
