# 014 — The take-profit coverage check reports the fee as drift

Issue #700, follow-up to #696 (`013.tp-coverage-drift-after-partial-tp`).

## 1. Definition

The coverage check of spec 013 compares two quantities per open deal:

    R = Σ over live dealTP orders of (origQty − executedQty)
    P = |deal.size| − (base this deal has already closed)
    healthy  ⟺  R = P            (013 §1.1)

**§1.1 — that equality is not what the engine arms.** `getTPOrder` does not
size the take-profit at the position. On spot it applies a fee factor to the
gross entry volume (`dcaHelper.ts:13563`):

    qty = grossEntry × (long ? 1 − fee : 1 / (1 − fee)) + alreadyClosed

because closing a long *sells* `gross × (1 − fee)` — one fee less than the
entry bought — and closing a short *buys* base back, itself charged in base.
Futures zero that factor (the margin is charged in quote and never touches the
position), so `qty = grossEntry + alreadyClosed` there.

**Expected.** A deal whose resting take-profit is exactly what `getTPOrder`
would arm for it reads as **covered**.

**Actual.** It reads as drifted by exactly one fee — `under` for a long,
`over` for a short — on every reconcile pass, forever. Neither shape
self-clears: `placeOrders` re-arms the same fee-shaved quantity every time.

**§1.2** The magnitude is the same in both directions: `P × fee / (1 − fee)`.
For the fleet's common `fee = 0.001` that is `0.1001%` of the tracked position.

**§1.3** It only *reports* above the venue minimum (013 §1.5), so the deals it
reaches are the large ones — a fleet-wide false positive that presents as a
handful of the biggest positions, which is what made it read as a real defect.

**§1.4** The correction of 013 §1.4 does not touch these deals: their single
take-profit is `NEW`, so `staleTps` is empty and `rearm` is false. The cost is
purely that an operator cannot trust the warn stream, and 013 §4.2's
`BOT_TP_COVERAGE_REPAIR` cannot be armed on the strength of it.

**§1.5 — the fix must not be a fee subtraction inside `trackedPosition`.**
Which of `getTPOrder`'s fee branches produced a given resting order is not
knowable from the deal: the quantity leg is zeroed on futures, zero for a
`zeroFee` API key (`main.ts:2174` answers `{maker: 0, taker: 0}` before ever
reaching the venue), re-derived from quote profit on a `profitCurrency: base`
deal (`dcaHelper.ts:13712`), and floored to the pair's base step afterwards.
Subtracting a fee unconditionally would silence the deals in §1.1 and start
reporting, in the opposite direction, every deal whose take-profit is *not*
fee-shaved.

**§1.6** The tolerance is therefore stated on the DRIFT, not on the position:
the part of a drift that one fee explains is not evidence of anything, in
either direction. What remains — the residue a repair would actually have to
place an order for — goes through 013 §1.5's existing venue-minimum test.

## 2. Evidence

Production `Gainium-DCA-bot` / `Gainium-Combo-bot` logs, fixed string
`tp-coverage`, and read-only `mongo`, both on 2026-09-07 (VPS triage key).
840 log lines, **195 distinct deals**.

**§2.2 — the short gross-up, the same magnitude the other way.** Four deals

**§2.3 — the rest of the population is not fee-shaped.** Of the 195 reported
deals, 9 are within one fee (§2.1 + §2.2, one of the SUPRAUSDT pair being a
second deal of the same shape) and 2 more are within one fee plus one base
step. The remaining 184 start at **1.2453%** of the tracked position
(BARUSDT `68ab6b4824`, `over`) and run to **98%** and beyond — 83 of them
report more than half the position uncovered. A one-fee tolerance is an order
of magnitude clear of the narrowest of them.

**§2.4 — why a subtraction inside `trackedPosition` was rejected (§1.5).**
Sampling 2,000 open DCA deals and keeping the 403 in this check's scope
(`useTp`, single take-profit, non-trailing, closes on take-profit) with exactly
one live take-profit row:

| resting ÷ tracked | deals | reading |
|---|---|---|
| exactly `1` | 220 | take-profit sized at the position, no fee applied |
| `0.999` | 45 | long shave (§2.1) |
| `1 / 0.999` | 22 | short gross-up (§2.2) |
| anything else | 116 | genuinely drifted, or another sizing branch |

More than half of the in-scope fleet rests a take-profit at *exactly* the
tracked position. A subtraction would have turned that majority into a new
`over` family of the same 0.1% shape this spec exists to remove.

## 3. Assumption

The root cause is that spec 013 §1.1 defines coverage as `R = P` while the
code that arms `R` targets `P` **net of the fee it will pay to close** — an
invariant stated one way in the check and another way in `getTPOrder`. The
check is measuring correctly and comparing against the wrong target.

**Falsifiable form:** a deal reported `under`/`over` whose live take-profit
quantity equals `trackedPosition × (1 − fee)` (long) or
`trackedPosition ÷ (1 − fee)` (short), with no stale partial and no second
resting order, is healthy — the engine will re-arm the identical quantity on
its next pass and the report will repeat unchanged. §2.1 and §2.2 are ten such
deals, and 150 of the 195 repeated their report at ≥ 2 passes more than 26 h
apart.

## 4. Scope

**§4.1** Detection only. Nothing here arms, cancels or places an order; the
change can only ever make the check report **less**, never more, so no deal
that is silent today becomes noisy and `BOT_TP_COVERAGE_REPAIR` gains no new
targets.

**§4.2** Out of scope, and still open on issue #700: the 184 deals that are
genuinely drifted. In particular the shape where one deal alternates between a
full-position and a base-order-sized take-profit across restarts, leaving the
base-order-sized one resting last (ACTUSDT `675dccc2a6`: 194.4 and 15.8 against
a position of 194.6; LISTAUSDT `68e67313c7`: 428.7 and 9.8 against 428.7).
That is a second sizing path and a separate spec.

**§4.3** Out of scope: widening `staleTps`/`rearm` so a resting `NEW`
take-profit materially under the tracked position becomes cancellable. That
decision cancels a healthy-looking order with real money behind it and must
follow §4.2, not precede it.
