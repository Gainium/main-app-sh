# 218 — A combo base minigrid is anchored on the request price instead of the fill

Bug spec.

## §1 Definition

### §1.1 Expected

When a combo deal's base order fills, `createMinigrid` lays the base minigrid
around the price the base order FILLED at: a LONG's range runs from the fill up
one base step, a SHORT's from the fill down one base step. The buy-backs of a
SHORT therefore rest below its entry and the sells of a LONG above it.

### §1.2 Actual

`createMinigrid` anchored a base order on `price` only when the order was
recorded as MARKET; any other base order was anchored on `origPrice`, the price
that was requested. When the recorded type is not what was traded, the
minigrid is laid around a price the deal never traded at.

Hyperliquid has no native market order. The connector sends a MARKET as a GTC
limit padded 10% through the requested price (×0.9 for a sell, ×1.1 for a
buy). When that order rests for a moment instead of filling at once, the
placement answer carries the venue's `Limit` type and the un-padded request
price, so `placedOrderType` books the entry as a LIMIT at the request price.
The fill then reports the real execution into `price`; `origPrice` keeps the
request.

On a thin book the fill is far from the request: a SHORT entry requested at
0.050678 filled at 0.04561 (0.050678 × 0.9). The base minigrid got the range
0.0497–0.0507 — every buy-back above the entry and above the market — while
`deal.initialPrice` and the rest of the deal used 0.04561. When those buy-backs
were (re)placed they filled at once, the base minigrid closed, and the deal was
closed at market at a loss.

## §2 Evidence

- Production order rows of the affected deal: base order `type: LIMIT`,
  `price 0.04561`, `origPrice 0.050678`, filled 5 s after placement; the
  deal's `initialPrice` 0.04561.
- The bot log shows the venue acknowledging the order `NEW, 0.04561` and
  filling it at 0.04561, i.e. the order rested at the padded price.
- The venue's 1-minute candles: no trade between 21:20 and 21:29 (last
  0.050701, the stale price the request was sized on), then 0.04561 high in
  the minute of the fill.
- The base minigrid's buy-backs were at 0.050108–0.050564.
- Reproduced with the real `createMinigrid` → `generateGridsOnPrice` chain
  (`src/bot/combo/baseMinigridAnchor.harness.spec.ts`): before the fix the
  SHORT's range top is 0.050678, the request price.

## §3 Assumption (hypothesis, confirmed by §2's harness)

The anchor choice in `createMinigrid` trusts the recorded order type to decide
whether `price` or `origPrice` is the entry. For a base order the entry is
always the fill, `price`; `origPrice` is a request and only equals the entry
when the venue honoured it exactly.

- §3.1 A SHORT base recorded LIMIT with `price` ≠ `origPrice` is anchored on
  `price`: range top = fill, every buy-back ≤ fill.
- §3.2 The same for a LONG: range bottom = fill, every sell ≥ fill.

## §4 What must not move

- §4.1 A MARKET base is anchored on its fill, as before.
- §4.2 A base whose `price` is not a usable positive number falls back to
  `origPrice`.
- §4.3 A safety-order (`dealRegular`) minigrid still anchors on its level
  price, `origPrice`.

## §5 Out of scope (stated, not resolved here)

- The connector reporting a resting padded market order as `LIMIT` at the
  request price. The engine's fix is venue-independent; the connector's label
  is the trigger, not the defect in the anchor.
- Labelling of a deal closed after its base minigrid closes (`closeTrigger`):
  `processDealTP` deliberately turns `base` into `tp`; changing what users see
  is a product decision.
- Minigrids created before this fix keep the range they were created with.
