# 098 — A DCA close is sized gross of the entry fee the venue took in base

## §1 Definition

### §1.1 Expected

A long spot DCA deal's close (take-profit, stop loss, market close, and every
other size built by `getTPOrder`'s non-combo path) sells no more base than the
deal holds: its gross filled entry quantity net of the base the venue took as
fees on those entry fills.

### §1.2 Actual

The only fee correction is an ESTIMATE, `_qty × (1 − maxFee)` with
`maxFee = worstFee(await getUserFee())`. On an exchange account carrying the
user-set `zeroFee` flag, `getUserFee` short-circuits to `{maker: 0, taker: 0}`,
so the correction is 0 even when the venue charged the entry fee in the BASE
asset and reports it on the order's own fill rows. The close is sized at the
gross quantity; for a `zeroFee` non-combo deal it is also rounded to NEAREST
rather than floored, so even a correctly netted fractional holding would round
back up to gross. The venue refuses the close for insufficient balance and the
deal is left open with no exit.

## §2 Evidence

1. Code: `getTPOrder` non-combo path —
   `qty = _qty × (futures || thirdAssetOnly ? 1 : long ? 1 − maxFee : 1/(1 − maxFee)) + add`;
   `tpOrder.qty = round(qty, precision, down = !(futures || kucoinSpot || (zeroFee && !combo)))`.
2. Production, long spot DCA on `zeroFee` accounts (bot-error log):
   - BNBUSDT: "balance … free 0.014985 BNB, required 0.015 BNB" — shortfall
     exactly 0.1 % of the 0.015 entry.
   - BONKUSDT, recurring: "free 6,126,463.41 BONK, required 6,132,596 BONK" —
     shortfall exactly 0.1 % of the entry.
3. Harness (`dca/dcaCloseEntryFee.harness.spec.ts`) over those two shapes with
   `getUserFee` → 0 and a base-denominated fee on the entry row returns 0.015
   and 6,132,596 — the refused quantities, to the unit.

## §3 Assumption (hypothesis)

Same as spec 097 for combo: the missing input is the fee the venue reported.
Per filled entry row, take the LARGER of the estimate and the row's observed
base fee (`comboEntryBaseFee`); use it only when it exceeds the whole-position
estimate `_qty × maxFee`, so every case where the estimate already covers the
fee keeps today's expression. When an observed fee IS netted, the size is
floored, not rounded to nearest.

## §4 Behaviour

- §4.1 Long spot, not third-asset-only: the close is
  `_qty − max(_qty × maxFee, Σ max(executedQty × maxFee, observedFeeSplit(row).base))`
  over the base order and filled safety rows, floored to the base precision.
- §4.2 When the observed legs do not exceed the estimate (configured-rate
  accounts, quote-denominated fees, rows without fee data) the size and its
  rounding are unchanged.
- §4.3 The spec-015 all-third-asset gate still sizes at gross.
- §4.4 Out of scope, unchanged: shorts (`1/(1 − fee)`), futures, combo (spec
  097), the Kraken `fciq`/`fcib` `feeFactor` pairing in `createOrder` (a Kraken
  BUY reports its fee in quote, so it keeps the estimate).
