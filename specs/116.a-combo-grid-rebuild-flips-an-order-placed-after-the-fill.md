# 116 — A combo grid rebuild flips an order placed after the fill

Bug spec. The behaviour already exists and is wrong.

## §1 Definition

### §1.1 Expected

Every fill of a combo deal-grid order rebuilds that minigrid's ladder
(`updateMinigrid` → `generateGridsOnPrice`). The ladder has one empty level,
at the fill: levels below it rest BUYs and levels above it rest SELLs. In
effect the rebuild says "the price moved here". Every resting order between
the old empty level and the new one is taken to have filled, and its level
turns to the other side. That inference holds only for orders that were on
the book while the price moved.

An order placed after the fill being processed cannot have been filled by
that fill's price move, because it was not on the book. The rebuild must not
count it as filled:

- §1.1.1 After a buy fill, the ladder never rests more SELLs than the base
  the minigrid holds. No SELL is sent for base already sold.
- §1.1.2 After a sell fill, the ladder never leaves base the minigrid holds
  without a resting SELL, and never rests more BUYs than the quote it holds.
- §1.1.3 The ladder recorded after the rebuild (`lastPrice`/`lastSide`, and
  the last-order marker `isLastMinigridOrder` reads) is the ladder on the
  book. The next fill, and a worker restart that rebuilds the ladder from
  `lastPrice`, continue from it.

### §1.2 Actual

`isLastMinigridOrder` makes any fill on the opposite side of the last
processed fill the "latest" fill, whatever order the venue reported them in.
The ladder is then rebuilt at that fill's price, and every level between it
and the previous price is flipped. That includes counter-orders the engine
placed after this fill happened, in reaction to other fills of the same
burst. Each flipped BUY becomes a SELL the account has no base for, so the
venue refuses it (`Not enough balance … deal grid order`). The mirror case
cancels fresh counter-SELLs and places BUYs above the market. Those BUYs fill
at once and leave their base with no sell.

### §1.3 Unchanged

- §1.3.1 A fill whose rebuild meets no order placed after it is processed
  exactly as today. This covers the counter-SELL after a buy fill, the
  counter-BUY after a sell fill, and the catch-up over orders that were
  resting when the price moved.
- §1.3.2 The not-latest path (`isLastMinigridOrder` false) and the
  minigrid-close decision are untouched.

## §2 Evidence

Production, Kraken spot, a long combo's deal minigrid (low 0.0805, top
0.082564, 10 levels, 506.53547 base per level), 2026-09-27. Taken from the
`Gainium-Combo-bot` log (UTC; the trailing field is the venue time):

```

04:41:31.597  SELL 0.08273 FILLED                    1790484091582
04:41:31.673  BUY  0.08215 NEW (counter)             1790484091660
04:41:31.782  BUY  0.08215 FILLED                    1790484091769
04:41:32.233  Update minigrid … SELL 0.08273
04:41:32.291  BUY  0.08236 NEW (counter of 0.08273)  1790484092280   ← placed after the 0.08215 fill
04:41:32.856  Update minigrid … BUY 0.08215           ← opposite side ⇒ "latest"
04:41:32.968  BUY  0.08236 CANCELED
04:41:33.563  Not enough balance … side: sell, price - 0.08273 … free - 0 GNOT
```

The same shape occurs at 04:40:15–18. Three sell fills get counter-BUYs at
0.08112/0.08133/0.08153. A buy fill that the venue reported 1 ms later then
cancels all three and re-sends SELLs at 0.0819/0.08169/0.08149, and the third
is refused. At 04:42:13–15 the mirror case happens: counter-SELLs at
0.08169/0.08149, placed after the 0.0821 sell filled, are cancelled for BUYs
that fill at once at 0.08122. Afterwards the deal holds about three levels of
GNOT with no sell resting.

The same refusal shape predates spec `115`'s deploy, which came five
minutes before this burst. Spec `115` only changes
`createCurrentDealOrders` (the `CMB-RO` safety ladder) and never produces a
`CMB-GR` order, so it is ruled out.

## §3 Assumption (root-cause hypothesis)

The ladder generator, `findDiff` and the counter-order arithmetic are each
right for a single fill. The defect is that the rebuild has no notion of time.
It applies "every level between the old and the new price has traded" to
orders that did not exist when the price traded there.

## §4 Fix

### §4.1

In `updateMinigrid`, on the rebuild path only (the fill is the latest one):

- Count the minigrid's resting grid orders (`NEW`/`PARTIALLY_FILLED`) that
  meet all three conditions: on the fill's side, placed after the fill, and
  on a level (the initial grid nearest their limit price on their side) that
  the rebuild turns to the other side.
- For each one, the rebuild counted a fill that did not happen, so move the
  empty level back toward the old one by one level each. Buy fill: up. Sell
  fill: down.
- Rebuild at that level's own price (`generateGridsOnPrice` finds it exactly,
  so the empty level lands there).
- Record that price as the minigrid's `lastPrice` and as the last-order
  marker.
- Take the filled order out of the ladder the diff compares against, so the
  level it traded on is placed again if the new ladder wants an order there.

No re-anchor when:

- no such order exists (the rebuild stands exactly as today);
- the rebuild's empty level cannot be identified (not exactly one missing
  level);
- the move would put the empty level at either end, which would empty a
  whole side.

### §4.2

Times: `updateTime` of the processed fill is the venue's time for that
report, which is the fill. On Kraken it is the v2 execution `timestamp`, on
Binance the transaction time `T`, and on OKX/Bybit the update time.
`transactTime` of a resting order is its placement time from the venue or
connector response. An order with no placement time keeps today's behaviour.

### §4.3 Why not keep the later order where it is

Keeping the later order on its level instead of re-anchoring also stops the
refusal. But the ladder then stops being one a rebuild can produce. When the
kept order fills later, `isLastMinigridOrder` calls that fill not-latest, so
no counter-order is sent and a level's base is left idle. A restart that
rebuilds from `lastPrice` also discards the kept order. Both were reproduced
in the harness before this design was chosen.
