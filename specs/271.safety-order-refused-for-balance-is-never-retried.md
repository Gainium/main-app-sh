# 271 — A safety order refused for balance is never retried

Bug spec. Product ruling: timed retry, as a limit at the level's own price,
on the closed-market back-off, giving up after 24 h with the level recorded
as skipped.

## 1. Definition

**Expected.** When a venue refuses a DCA or Combo safety order because the
account cannot fund it, the bot keeps that level and places it again later.
Each retry is a LIMIT order at the level's own price: never a market order,
never a worse price than the level. Retries back off from 5 min, doubling, to
60 min. If the venue keeps refusing for 24 h, the bot stops, records the level
as skipped and tells the user why. A level refused for balance is always
in one of three states: retrying, filled, or recorded as skipped. It is never
silently dropped.

**Actual.** The refused order is written off (`CANCELED`, `refusal` stamped).
`handleOrderErrors` raises the coalesced "Not enough balance" error and puts
the bot into `error`. Nothing places the level again until something else
rebuilds the ladder: another fill, or a restart. A restart then sends one
level at the market through the gap path, and the levels the price passed
are recorded as skipped (spec 233).

## 2. Evidence

- A live USDT-M silver DCA long deal had its safety orders refused "Not enough
  balance" on four dates between late October and mid November. After the
  last refusal the bot sat in `error` for about 2 days until it was restarted.
  On restart level 20 (47.61) filled at the market (42.72), and levels 17
  (53.19) and 21 (45.36) are on `deal.missedLevels`. API read of that deal:
  `levels {complete:20, all:22}`, `missedLevels [17, 21]`, `lastPrice 42.72`.
- Code: `sendOrderToExchange`'s write-off branch (`core/src/bot/main.ts`)
  holds a refused order for a retry only for `Market closed` (spec 248) and
  `Order price` (spec 250). A balance refusal reaches neither. Both the
  local cooldown short-circuit (`notEnoughBalanceShortCircuit`) and the
  venue's own refusal end the same way.
- `flushMarketClosed` only retried bots in `open` / `range`. A balance
  refusal sets the soft `error` status (`processError` with `setError`), so
  even a held order would have been skipped by the retry pass.

## 3. Behaviour (the fix)

- §3.1 A `dealRegular` LIMIT order (not add/reduce funds; only orders the
  engine places back after a venue cancel, spec 247), refused for balance
  (`matchesNotEnoughBalance`, by the venue or by the local cooldown), is held
  by level on the closed-market queue. The retry pass hands it to
  `replaceVenueCanceled`, which places the level from the deal's current
  ladder as a LIMIT at the level's price, with a fresh client order id.
- §3.2 The retry pass runs while the bot is `open`, `range` or `error`.
  `error` is soft, and the placement pass clears it once the venue accepts.
  It places nothing for a stopped or closed bot.
- §3.3 Back-off: 5 min, doubling, capped at 60 min (`marketClosedRetryDelay`).
  A refusal more than 3 h after the previous one starts a new spell.
- §3.4 Give-up: a refusal arriving 24 h or more after the spell's first is
  not held. On a DCA deal whose ladder records skipped levels (not Combo, not
  indicator-driven DCA) the level goes onto `deal.missedLevels`, which keeps it
  off the book until the user executes it from the deal. Otherwise (Combo,
  indicator DCA) the user is told, and the level is left to the engine's next
  ladder rebuild. A level the ladder no longer wants needs neither: spec 233
  already records it when it is behind the last fill.
- §3.5 The user is told once per pair per spell that the order was refused
  and will be retried for up to 24 h. The existing "Not enough balance" bot
  error is unchanged.
- §3.6 Market orders, base orders, grid levels, take-profits and add/reduce
  funds orders are not affected.

## 4. Price ran through several levels (accepted risk)

When the price falls past several held levels (long; rises for a short), one
retry pass sends each held level as a LIMIT at its own price. A buy limit
above the market executes at once at the market price, which is the same or
better than its level, so several levels can fill together at the market. That
is the accepted risk of the ruling. If the first fill is booked before the next
level is sent, the ladder no longer wants that level (it is behind the last
fill), so `replaceVenueCanceled` does not send it, and spec 233 records it as
skipped with its own notice. No level is ever sent at a worse price than its own.

## 5. Bots already in this state

The hold queue is in memory. Deploying the fix restarts the bot workers, and
the reload rebuilds every running deal's ladder. That is exactly what
recovered the bot in §2: levels still ahead of the last fill are placed again,
and passed ones are recorded as skipped (spec 233). After the deploy, any level
refused again enters the retry. No database repair is needed.

## 6. Assumption (root cause)

The write-off branch had no retry for the balance class, and the shared retry
pass skipped bots in `error`. Holding balance refusals on that queue, and
letting the pass run in `error`, fixes the symptom at its cause. The test
fails without the change because `noteBalanceRefusal` / `holdRefusedForRetry`
/ `giveUpBalanceRefusedLevel` do not exist and `flushMarketClosed` ignores a
bot in `error`.
