# 113 — A Bitget spot market buy is funded at the last trade price

A Bitget spot MARKET BUY comes back one base step short of the quantity the bot
asked for, and the one-step top-up that `buyRemainder` sends to cover the
shortfall is refused as `size 0.000` every time.

## §1 Definition

### §1.1 The market buy fills the quantity it was asked for

**Expected.** A spot MARKET BUY for `q` units on Bitget executes `q` units when
the book's best ask is within a normal distance of the price the bot sized it
at.

**Actual.** Bitget spot sizes a market buy in the QUOTE coin.
`sendOrderToExchange` converts the bot's base quantity to that amount as
`q × lastPrice`, rounded to the quote scale. Bitget fills
`⌊amount ÷ fillPrice⌋` on the base step. A buy fills at the ask, which is at or
above the last trade, so whenever the ask is above the last trade by any amount,
`⌊q × last ÷ ask⌋ = q − step`: the order comes back one step short.

### §1.2 The one-step top-up can never execute

**Expected.** `buyRemainder`'s top-up (`GA-BR`) for the missing step executes.

**Actual.** The top-up is itself a MARKET BUY for exactly one step, converted
the same way: `step × last`. For any ask above the last trade,
`⌊step × last ÷ ask⌋ = 0`, and Bitget refuses the order with
`parameter verification exception size 0.000 > 0`. The deal is left one step
short, silently (`showUser: false` for that subtype).

### §1.3 Unchanged

Every other exchange's market buys, every Bitget market SELL (sized in base),
every LIMIT order, and Bitget futures (`bitgetUsdm` / `bitgetCoinm`, sized in
base) are sent exactly as before. The spec `084` §4.2 cap of 6 decimals on the
Bitget amount and the spec `084` §4.3 zero-size refusal stay in force.

## §2 Evidence

Production, `Gainium-DCA-bot` log, one deal on a live Bitget account
(`ZECUSDT`, base step `0.001`, `quotePrecision 5`), 2026-09-25:

```
11:17:09.856Z Processing msg ZECUSDT D-BO-…, PARTIALLY_FILLED, 1608.8, base: 0.002, quote: 3.2176 BUY
11:17:09.857Z Order D-BO-… not BUY full qty: executed - 0.002, total - 0.003, diff - 0.001, count 1
11:17:10.131Z [WARN] … Reason parameter verification exception size 0.000 > 0
              Method limitOrders() Step Send new order request GA-BR-…, qty 0.001, price 1608.37, side BUY
```

- Base order: `0.003` ordered, `0.002` filled at `1608.8`.
- Top-up: `0.001 × 1608.37 = 1.60837` USDT; `1.60837 ÷ 1608.8 = 0.0009997` →
  `0.000` on the step. Refused.

The same signature (`GA-BR`/`D-SR` of exactly one step, `size 0.000`) recurs on
this account since 2026-09-06, on `ZECUSDT` and `SOLUSDC`. Enabling a
classification rule for the message changed its subtype, not the behaviour.

## §3 Assumption (hypothesis)

The amount is priced at the last trade with no allowance for the ask. Funding
the amount for `q + step/2` instead of `q` makes Bitget's round-down land on
`q` for every fill price in the band
`last × q/(q + step/2) … last × (q + step/2)/q` (strictly: fills `q` iff
`q ≤ (q + step/2) × last ÷ ask < q + step`):

- at `ask = last`, `⌊q + step/2⌋ = q` — no overfill;
- a one-step top-up fills at any ask up to 1.5 × last;
- a larger order still fills in full for an ask within `step / 2q` of last,
  and anything short of that is recovered by the top-up, which now executes.

The half-step is the midpoint between the two failure modes (one step short,
one step over), so neither side of the band is favoured. The extra quote
reserved is at most half a step's worth; Bitget spends only what it fills.

A best-ask read would be exact, but the exchange chain
(main-app → exchange-balancer → exchange-connector) has no order-book route;
adding one is a cross-service change out of proportion to this defect.

## §4 Requirements

- §4.1 A Bitget spot MARKET BUY for `q` at last price `p` is funded so that
  `⌊amount ÷ p⌋ = q` on the base step (no overfill at the price it was sized at).
- §4.2 The spec §2 base order (`0.003` at last `1608.37`) funds at least
  `0.003` units at the observed fill `1608.8`.
- §4.3 The spec §2 top-up (`0.001` at last `1608.37`) funds exactly `0.001` at
  the observed fill `1608.8` — it no longer converts to zero.
- §4.4 The amount still carries at most 6 decimals (spec `084` §4.2).
- §4.5 A Bybit market buy and a Bitget market SELL are sent exactly as before.
