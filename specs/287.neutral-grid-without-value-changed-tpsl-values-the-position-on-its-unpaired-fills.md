# 287 — A neutral grid without a value-changed TP/SL values its open position on its unpaired fills

Follow-up to specs `099`, `117` and `124`. A NEUTRAL, non-COIN-M futures grid
books its realized profit per matched grid pair (`createTransaction`) and books
its close leg against the entry of the fills those pairs left unpaired
(`closeEntryPrice()`, spec `099`). Spec `124` made the run-up, drawdown, live
stats and the dashboard value the open position against that same entry, but
only for grids whose TP or SL is value-changed (spec `124` §4.6, the `117`
refresh gate). Every other neutral grid keeps valuing the coins it still holds
at `position.price`, the average of every fill that ever added to the position,
paired ones included — fills whose cost realized profit has already accounted
for.

This spec is about **internal consistency** of the bot's own books. It makes no
claim about matching any exchange's figures: an exchange values a position at
its own average entry, so the bot's unrealized and the exchange's can differ.

## §1 Definition

### §1.1 Expected

For every NEUTRAL, non-COIN-M futures grid with an open position, whatever its
TP/SL settings:

- §1.1.1 The bot publishes `closeEntry` `{side, qty, price, entry}` for its
  current position (spec `124` §4.1), with `entry` the unpaired-fills entry
  `closeEntryPrice()` would book a close at (or `position.price` when the
  ledger does not net to the position, as today).
- §1.1.2 Its booked realized profit plus its open position valued against that
  entry equals the cash its own fills produced plus the position at the mark:
  `realized + qty·(mark − entry)·sign = Σ sells·price·qty − Σ buys·price·qty + signedQty·mark`
  (gross of fees on both sides).
- §1.1.3 A position no fill has refreshed — after a restart, or a grid whose
  price has left its range and stops filling — is published too.

### §1.2 Actual

The bot returns from `refreshTpSlEntry()` without reading the ledger unless
TP or SL is value-changed. `closeEntry` is never published, so every consumer
(spec `124` §1.1) falls back to `position.price`, and realized + unrealized no
longer adds up to what the fills did.

## §2 Evidence

Live NEUTRAL futures grids on one account (all `tpSl:false`, `sl:false`), read
read-only over the dashboard GraphQL, their transactions, and the prod grid
worker log:

- §2.1 One bot, 697 fills: position LONG 61 @ `position.price` 0.2384; the
  fills the ledger left unpaired net to exactly LONG 61 at 0.268864. At mark
  0.2227: realized 0.46, cash + position at mark −2.356. Valued on the unpaired
  entry the books give 0.46 − 2.816 = −2.356 (exact). Valued on
  `position.price` they give 0.46 − 0.958 = −0.498, about 1.86 less loss than
  the fills actually produced. The dashboard showed the latter.
- §2.2 `closeEntry` is null on every such bot document; bots of the same
  account with a value-changed TP/SL carry it.
- §2.3 The close path is already right: the grid worker logged
  `Close entry: 0.2661… from unpaired fills (position price 0.2311)` for the
  same bot when it closed a position. Only the valuation of the still-open
  position diverges.

## §3 Assumption (root cause, hypothesis)

`refreshTpSlEntry()` (core `src/bot/helper.ts`) gates on
`(tpSl && tpSlCondition==='valueChanged') || (sl && slCondition==='valueChanged')`.
That gate was a deliberate scope choice of spec `124` §4.6, made when the
entry was only thought of as the TP/SL's input. It is the only thing between a
neutral grid and a published `closeEntry`. Test: with TP and SL off, a filled
order leaves `closeEntry` undefined and the ledger unread.

Second half: the only refresh not caused by a fill comes from the TP/SL tick
(`tpSlEntryPrice()`), so lifting the gate alone still leaves a restarted or
out-of-range grid on `position.price` until its next fill.

## §4 Required behaviour

- §4.1 `refreshTpSlEntry()` runs for every NEUTRAL, non-COIN-M futures grid
  (the `usesUnpairedEntry` + `futures` condition alone). Spec `124` §4.6's
  "which grids read the ledger" clause is reversed by this spec.
- §4.2 The once-a-minute live-stats sample starts (and awaits) a refresh when
  the cached entry was not computed for the current position, so §1.1.3 holds
  from the first sample after a restart. A cached entry is reused; the sample
  never reads the ledger twice for one position.
- §4.3 §1.1.2 holds for a neutral grid with TP and SL off.
- §4.4 Not changed: the close booking (`closeEntryPrice()` /
  `profitAfterPositionClosed()`), and when TP/SL fire. A position closed with
  TP/SL off books exactly the same profit before and after this spec.
- §4.5 Not changed: LONG/SHORT-strategy and COIN-M grids. They book their
  reductions against `position.price` (`matchedId: 'position price'`), so
  `position.price` is already the consistent valuation for them; they publish
  nothing and read nothing.
- §4.6 No contract change: `closeEntry` already exists on the bot document,
  GraphQL type, settings event and snapshots (spec `124` §4.7).
- §4.7 Cost: one ledger read (FILLED regular/stop orders + paired
  transactions of the bot) per fill, now for every neutral futures grid, plus
  at most one per position from the live-stats sample. This is the read every
  value-changed neutral grid already does, and the one its close does.

## §5 Open questions

- §5.1 Net P&L % basis: out of scope.
- §5.2 Already-persisted `$max` run-up / drawdown values recorded on the
  average basis stay until a larger one replaces them (as spec `124` §5.1).

## Invariants

Pack: `main-app/specs/287.invariants.json`.

- `287.neutral-grid-open-position-has-close-entry` (observe): an open NEUTRAL,
  non-COIN-M futures grid whose position is open but whose `closeEntry` is
  missing or was computed for a different side/qty/price, for longer than
  15 minutes. A user then reads an unrealized / net P&L that does not add up to
  what the bot's own fills did.
