# 249 — A venue-cancelled LIMIT remainder sweep is never placed again

Bug spec (Definition / Evidence / Assumption). Tests:
`src/bot/limitOnlyLateFillSweep.spec.ts`, block "spec 249". Follow-up of spec
`225`, which added the LIMIT sweep.

## §1 Definition

When a limit-only book refuses a closed deal's MARKET remainder sweep, spec
`225` places the same quantity as a reduce-only LIMIT `D-SR` order.

**Expected.** If the venue ends that LIMIT CANCELED or EXPIRED, and the bot did
not ask for the cancel:

- §1.1 the unsold quantity is placed again as a reduce-only LIMIT at the latest
  price, under a new `D-SR` id (spec `221`), DCA and Combo alike;
- §1.2 a part the cancelled order sold is not lost: only the rest is placed
  again, and when the sweep ends, everything it sold across its orders is
  booked to the closed deal ONCE, as one sale at the average price — the same
  booking spec `225` §1.2 gives a single fill;
- §1.3 this happens at most 3 times per sweep. After that, or when the rest is
  below the venue minimums, nothing more is placed and what was sold is booked;
- §1.4 a cancel the bot itself asked for is not placed again.

**Actual.** The cancel reaches the generic TP-cancel handler, which logs
"TP … was CANCELED on the exchange with nothing filled, not by the bot.
Checking the deal in 15s". The deal is closed, so the check does nothing. The
coins stay in the account, tracked by no deal and sold by nothing. A part a
cancelled LIMIT sold before the cancel goes to `updatePartiallyFilledTP`,
which books only on an open deal, so it is dropped.

## §2 Evidence (prod, read-only)

Coinbase spot, a limit-only book. Bot log:

- `… D-SR-aCZG… refused, … book is in limit only mode. Selling the remaining
  465.12 of closed deal … by limit` (01:56:32Z), then 17 s later `TP
  D-SR-IvQe… of deal … was CANCELED on the exchange with nothing filled, not by
  the bot. Checking the deal in 15s`. No order follows.
- The same sequence on a second deal of the same bot two days earlier, the
  LIMIT cancelled 1 s after placement.
- `orders`: both `D-SR` LIMIT rows are CANCELED with `executedQty 0` and no
  later `D-SR` row on their deal (the invariant query below, 7 days).

The cancel reason is not visible from our side. The timing matches self-trade
prevention against the bot's next base-order buy (spec `226` evidence), so a
re-placement can be cancelled the same way — hence the cap in §1.3.

## §3 Assumption (root cause)

`sweepRemainderByLimit` remembers the sweep in `limitRemainderSweeps`, but
only the FILLED path (`bookLimitRemainderSweep`) reads that map.
`processCanceledOrder` never consults it, so a cancelled sweep is handled as a
TP of a deal that is already closed: nothing re-places it and nothing books a
partial fill.

Tests: §1.1 (DCA and Combo), §1.2, §1.3, §1.4 — each named after its section.

## §4 Not changed

- The LIMIT price stays the latest price (not a maker-side price): the book
  accepted spec `225`'s LIMITs, and a maker-only price would rest without
  selling.
- The MARKET sweep and the close path (spec `157`, `226`) are untouched.
- Known limit, as spec `225` §4: the sweep's booking context is in memory. A
  restart between a part fill and the end of the sweep leaves that part
  unbooked; the coins are still sold by the resting order.

## Invariants

`orders.limit-remainder-sweep-ended-unsold` in the main-app pack
`specs/invariants/deals.invariants.json` (observe): a closed deal's last `D-SR`
LIMIT ended CANCELED/EXPIRED with quantity unsold. After this fix, hits are
expected only when the 3 re-placements run out or the bot cancelled the sweep
itself.
