# 274 — A part-filled futures grid take-profit / stop-loss close stalls the close

Bug spec. Sibling of spec 273, which fixed the same stall for the user's
"close at limit / at market".

## §1 Definition

### §1.1 Expected

When a futures grid's take profit or stop loss fires with "stop and sell", the
bot keeps working until the position on the exchange is closed. A reduce-only
close that has not filled is re-placed every `orderLimitRepositionTimeout`, and
a LIMIT close moves to MARKET after `enterMarketTimeout` when set. If a close
fills only partly, the part it sold is booked into the bot's position and
profit, and the rest of the position is closed by the next order.

### §1.2 Actual

`closeBotByTp` re-runs itself on a timer with the original trigger
(`oldTpSl`). On that re-run it looked at the first close order (`typeOrder:
stop`) it held. If that close was `PARTIALLY_FILLED`, it logged
`Found close order with status partially filled` and returned. No cancel, no
new close, no new timer. The enter-market timer re-runs the same method and hit
the same guard. The rest of the position stayed open on the exchange with
nothing managing it, and the part already sold was never folded into the
position or the profit.

### §1.3 The sweep's promotion made a silent part-fill worse

A close that rested as `NEW` while it part-filled without a user-stream report
was cancelled on the next re-run by `cancelAllOrder`, which promotes a
`CANCELED` order with fills to `FILLED`. The promoted close reached the fill
path, which books the WHOLE position as closed and stops the bot while the
rest of it is still open (spec 273 §1.3, same mechanism).

### §1.4 Restart

After a restart the re-run timers are gone. `checkOrders` read a close the venue
reports `PARTIALLY_FILLED` and either saved it (when held `NEW`) or logged
`TP/SL order not changed` (when already held `PARTIALLY_FILLED`). Nothing
resumed the close.

## §2 Evidence

- Code: `src/bot/helper.ts` `closeBotByTp`, the `oldTpSl` guard on
  `find?.status === 'PARTIALLY_FILLED'` returned before any timer was armed;
  the `limitTimer` / `enterMarketTimer` are only armed further down. The
  restart branch in `checkOrders` had no futures `PARTIALLY_FILLED` route.
- Runtime: the re-run path runs on production. Its sibling guard
  (`Found close order with status filled`) was logged once in the retained
  grid-bot logs (about 50 days). The part-filled guard's line was logged 0
  times in the same window, across every service's logs. A Bybit LIMIT close is
  held `PARTIALLY_FILLED` from its placement answer alone (spec 273 §1.2), and
  `closeBotByTp` places its close through the same `sendGridToExchange`. So the
  state is reachable, and no production occurrence is known.
- Reproduction: `src/bot/gridTpSlClosePartialFill.spec.ts` drives the real
  `closeBotByTp` and `checkOrders`. Before the fix every §4.1–§4.7 and §4.9 case
  failed: nothing was cancelled or placed, the position stayed 350, and the
  silent part-fill was cancelled with promotion.

## §3 Assumption (hypothesis, confirmed by §2's reproduction)

The guard was written to stop a re-run from re-selling while a close was
working, which is right for spot (its balances are not a position). For futures
it turned the only retry loop into a no-op.

## §4 Required behaviour (futures only)

- §4.1 A re-run that finds a close held `PARTIALLY_FILLED` cancels it without
  promotion, books its executed part once, places a reduce-only close for the
  rest of the position and keeps the re-run timer going.
- §4.2 The enter-market re-run does the same and closes the rest at market.
- §4.3 A stop loss (MARKET or LIMIT) behaves like the take profit.
- §4.4 A resting close the stream never reported is cancelled without
  promotion, so its part is booked as a part, not as a full close.
- §4.5 If the cancel does not go through, nothing is booked or placed, and the
  re-run timer retries.
- §4.6 If the venue says the close filled in full, it goes to the fill path and
  nothing new is placed.
- §4.7 A key that may not cancel is reported once, and both timers are
  cleared (spec 232).
- §4.8 Unchanged: a close held `FILLED`, a first trigger with nothing resting,
  and every spot grid behave as before. `processSellAtStop` and `stop` (spec 273)
  are not changed.
- §4.9 A restart that finds a futures close `PARTIALLY_FILLED` hands it to
  `processSellAtStop`, the path a restart already uses for a cancelled close.
  That path settles it (spec 273) and closes the rest. Spot is unchanged.

## Invariants

- A running futures grid must not have, as its newest close order, a
  `PARTIALLY_FILLED` row or a `CANCELED` row with `executedQty > 0` that is
  more than 15 minutes old. Pack: main-app
  `specs/invariants/grid.invariants.json`
  (`grid.futures-close-stalled-part-filled-running`, observe). The closed-bot
  shape stays `grid.futures-close-stopped-part-filled` (spec 273).
