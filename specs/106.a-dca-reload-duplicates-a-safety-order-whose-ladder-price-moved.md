# 106 — A DCA reload duplicates a safety order whose ladder price moved

The DCA twin of spec 105. A DCA deal's safety ladder is rebuilt from its start
price whenever the bot loads the deal from the database (a settings save, or a
restart that cannot restore the deal from Redis). The restart order check then
compares that rebuilt ladder with the orders resting on the exchange. When a
level's price comes out different from the order already resting at that level,
the deal ends up with two orders at that level.

## §1 Definition

### §1.1 Expected

- **§1.1.1** After the restart order check, an open DCA deal rests at most one
  safety order (`dealRegular`) per ladder level. A level that already rests an
  order is left alone, even when the rebuilt ladder prices it a tick away.
- **§1.1.2** When the ladder has more unmatched levels than there are unmatched
  resting orders on that side (a level's order is missing), the check keeps
  placing the unmatched levels as today. The pairing is only trusted when it is
  one-to-one.
- **§1.1.3** Orders that already match the rebuilt ladder by price are neither
  placed again nor cancelled, as today.
- **§1.1.4** The prices of a newly built ladder do not change. Only which of
  its levels the restart check places does.

### §1.2 Actual

- **§1.2.1** `checkOrders` (restart branch) places every entry of the diff's
  `new` side and ignores its `cancel` side. DCA's `getDiffForCheckOrders`
  matches rebuilt levels to resting orders by exact price only, so a level that
  moved by one tick is placed a second time next to the order already resting
  there.

## §2 Evidence

Production, 2026-09-24.

- An ETH-USD DCA deal rested safety orders at 2589.9, 2505.2, 2407.8, 2295.8,
  2167 and 2018.9 (placed 2026-09-23 14:41Z, under the per-level rounding that
  shipped before 2026-09-24).
- At 08:01:30Z the worker logged `Reload bot` → `Checking for existing deals`,
  then placed 2407.9, 2295.9, 2167.2 and 2019.1 before `Found 1 open deals`,
  i.e. inside `checkOrders`. 2589.9 and 2505.2 matched by price and were not
  re-placed. Four unmatched rebuilt levels, four unmatched resting orders.
- DCA ladder grids and `D-RO` orders carry no `dcaLevel`, so spec 105's
  level-keyed pairing cannot apply here.
- Every other deal with a same-side pair placed by one reload batch shows the
  same shape: the number of levels the batch placed equals the number of
  resting orders the rebuilt ladder failed to match.

## §3 Assumption (hypothesis)

The duplicate is placed by `checkOrders` → DCA `getDiffForCheckOrders` →
`findDiff`, because `findDiff` pairs a rebuilt level with a resting order only
on an exact price, and nothing pairs the leftovers by ladder rank. A test
driving the real `checkOrders` with the production prices must place the four
shifted levels while the assumption holds.

## §4 Out of scope

- **§4.1** Deals that already hold duplicates. Which copy each user cancelled on
  the venue is unknown; the repair is an operator/user call.
- **§4.2** Ladder pricing (#946's rounding) is unchanged.
