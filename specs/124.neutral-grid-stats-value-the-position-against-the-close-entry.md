# 124 — A neutral grid's run-up, drawdown and unrealized PnL value the position against the close entry

Follow-up to specs `099` and `117`. Since `117`, a NEUTRAL, non-COIN-M futures
grid's value-changed TP/SL values the open position against the entry the
close books (`closeEntryPrice()` / the unpaired-fills entry). The statistics
the user reads to judge that TP/SL — Run up, Drawdown, Net PnL, Unrealized
PnL — still value it against `position.price`, the average of every fill that
ever added to the position, paired ones included. The bot can then show a
run-up above its TP while the TP correctly has not fired.

## §1 Definition

### §1.1 Expected

Wherever a futures grid's open position is valued at price `p` for display or
statistics, it is valued as `qty × (p − entry)` (sign per side) against the
SAME entry `tpSl()` uses (spec `117` §4.4/§4.5): the cached unpaired-fills
entry while it was computed for the current position (side, qty, price), and
`position.price` otherwise. A run-up of `x %` therefore means the value-changed
TP saw `x %`.

Surfaces:

- §1.1.1 `GridMonitor` (persisted `stats.runUpPercent` / `drawdownPercent` and
  the profit/loss time split).
- §1.1.2 `BotMonitor.calculateGridLiveStats` (persisted `liveStats`).
- §1.1.3 The dashboard's grid list/drawer, which recomputes Net PnL, Unrealized
  PnL, Run up and Drawdown client-side from the bot document.

### §1.2 Actual

All three value the position against `position.price`.

## §2 Evidence

Paper NEUTRAL futures grid on AKEUSDT (paperBinanceUsdm, lev 1, TP 3% /
SL −4%, both value-changed), read read-only over the dashboard GraphQL:

- §2.1 position LONG 1210 @ `position.price` 0.031526; the unpaired fills are
  BUY 605 @ 0.033334 and BUY 605 @ 0.032525 → close entry 0.0329295.
  `profit.total` 6.738345; initial value 3630 × 0.03385086 + 164.837695 =
  287.7163. Stored `stats.runUpPercent` 0.030586.
- §2.2 Last fill before the report: SELL at 0.032538, 2026-10-02T09:39:45Z
  (prod grid-bot log). Binance AKEUSDT 1m high after it: 0.033343 at 09:42Z.
  At that price the average basis gives (6.7383 + 1210 × (0.033343 −
  0.031526)) / 287.7163 = 3.11% — the stored 3.06% is a once-a-minute sample
  of it. The close basis gives (6.7383 + 1210 × (0.033343 − 0.0329295)) /
  287.7163 = 2.52%: the 3% TP (spec `117`, deployed 2026-09-27, before the bot
  was created) correctly did not fire. No `TP trigger` line exists for the bot.
- §2.3 The reporter's list screenshot (≈10:35Z) shows Unrealized +$1.10:
  1210 × (0.03243 − 0.031526) = 1.09 on the average basis; the close basis at
  the same price is −$0.60.

## §3 Assumption (root cause)

`gridMonitor.ts` `updateDealStats`/`addNewBot`, `botMonitor.ts`
`calculateGridLiveStats` and the dashboard's `transformGridBot` /
`calculateCurrentStats` value the position against `position.price`; the close
entry `117` computes lives only in the bot worker's private `tpSlEntry` cache,
which none of them can see. Test: feeding §2.1 to `GridMonitor` at 0.033343
records a 3.11% run-up.

## §4 Required behaviour

- §4.1 The bot worker publishes the cache `117` computes as the bot's
  `closeEntry` `{side, qty, price, entry}` — on the bot document, in the
  `bot settings update` event, and in the snapshots handed to `GridMonitor` and
  `calculateGridLiveStats`.
- §4.2 A consumer uses `closeEntry.entry` only when `closeEntry` matches the
  position's side, qty and price; otherwise `position.price` (stale, absent,
  flat position, LONG/SHORT/COIN-M grid — all identical to `117` §4.5).
- §4.3 For §2.1 at 0.033343, `GridMonitor` records a run-up of ≈2.52%, not
  3.11%; a sample at a price where the close basis is negative counts as loss.
- §4.4 `liveStats.valueChange` / `valueChangePerc` use the same entry.
- §4.5 The dashboard selects `closeEntry` and applies §4.2 in its Net PnL,
  Unrealized PnL, Run up and Drawdown, valuing the position as
  `qty × (p − entry)` — the measure of §1.1. Its current
  `qty × ((p − entry) / entry) × p` is the pre-spec-064 expression the bot and
  `liveStats` no longer use, so the same bot shows different numbers depending
  on whether the page has live prices.
- §4.6 Not changed: when TP/SL fire and how the close is booked (`099`/`117`);
  which grids read the ledger, and how often (the `117` refresh gate — a
  grid without a value-changed TP/SL publishes no `closeEntry` and keeps
  `position.price` everywhere, as its TP/SL never values the position).
- §4.7 Additive contract only: one new optional field on the bot document, the
  `Bot` GraphQL type and the settings event. No field is renamed or removed.

## §5 Open questions

- §5.1 Already-persisted `stats.runUpPercent` / `drawdownPercent` are `$max`
  accumulators; values recorded on the average basis stay until a larger one
  replaces them. Rewriting them is a data migration and is out of scope.
