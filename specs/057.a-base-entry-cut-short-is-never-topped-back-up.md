# 057 — A base entry cut short is never topped back up to the configured size

Bug spec. The behaviour already exists and is wrong.

## §1 Definition

A DCA bot's base order size is the user's statement of how much the deal is
worth opening. The entry machinery is allowed to change HOW that size is
bought — reposition the limit, then enter at market when the limit did not
fill — but not how MUCH.

### §1.1 Expected

When the entry window ends with the base order only part filled, the engine
settles it (spec `038`/`048`: cancel the remainder, open the deal on what
executed) **and buys the part that is missing at market**, so the deal opens on
the configured base order size. That is what "enter at market when the limit
has not filled" already means for an entry that filled nothing, and the engine
already owns the machinery for it: `buyRemainder` places a MARKET order for
`origQty - executedQty`, merges it back into the same row at the blended
average price, and declines when the remainder is below the venue's minimum.

### §1.2 Actual

`settlePartialBaseEntry` (`dcaHelper.ts`) opens the deal on the executed
quantity and stops. Nothing tops the entry back up. Spec `038` §5 named this
explicitly as out of its scope ("Topping the entry back up to the configured
base order size with a second, market order … is a separate change") — this
spec is that change.

The deal is then sized off the fraction that filled: its take profit, its
safety-order ladder and its usage are all derived from it, so the first safety
order can be an order of magnitude larger than the base it is averaging into.

`buyRemainder` cannot reach this shape on its own, for two independent reasons:

- its only entry point, `fillPartiallyFilledOrder` (`main.ts`), returns
  unconditionally for `coinbase` before `buyRemainder` runs — an undocumented
  guard that predates the public repository;
- it is only ever called from the order queue and from the synchronous
  placement response, neither of which is the settle.

## §2 Evidence

Production, Coinbase spot, DCA bot with `baseOrderSize: "100"` (USDC),
`remainderFullAmount: false`, 2026-09-18 (times UTC), `Gainium-DCA-bot`:

```
13:46:34.753  Deal <id> created
13:46:57.447  Processing msg IP-USDC D-BO-<id>, NEW, 0.18549, base: 0, quote: 0 BUY
13:46:57.697  Processing msg IP-USDC D-BO-<id>, PARTIALLY_FILLED, 0.18546,
                                                base: 14.62, quote: 2.7114252 BUY
13:47:10.373  Deal <id> enter market price
13:47:10.373  Deal <id> clear limit timer
13:47:10.373  Deal <id> found base order with status PARTIALLY_FILLED
13:47:10.373  Deal <id> base order D-BO-<id> stopped at 14.62 of 539.65 and nothing
                else will check it. Cancelling the remainder and opening the deal
                on what filled
13:47:10.720  Processing msg IP-USDC D-BO-<id>, CANCELED, 0.18546,
                                                base: 14.62, quote: 2.7114252 BUY
```

The deal opened on **2.71 USDC of a configured 100 USDC** base order — 2.7 % —
and its take profit was placed for 14.6 of the 539.65 units requested. No
`Order … not BUY full qty` line exists for the row, in either direction: the
remainder machinery was never entered.

Fleet-wide, over the nine days of `Gainium-DCA-bot` log retention
(2026-09-10 … 2026-09-18, the window in which spec `038`'s settle has existed):
**96 settles across 53 distinct bots**, ~11/day, median fill fraction
**39.6 %** of the requested quantity, 54 of 96 below 50 % and 12 below 10 %.
Every one of them opened a deal smaller than its owner configured.

### §2.1 What the evidence rules out

The shape is NOT the one an order-status gate could explain:

- the row is a **LIMIT** entry (`D-BO-…`, `limit_limit_gtc`), reported by the
  venue as `PARTIALLY_FILLED`;
- the `CANCELED` the row ends on is the **engine's own cancel**, issued by the
  settle 347 ms earlier — not a venue IOC cancel;
- `buyRemainder`'s `MARKET`+`CANCELED` arm is therefore not on this path at
  all, and could not be reached on this venue even if it were, because of the
  `coinbase` return in `fillPartiallyFilledOrder`.

## §3 Assumption (hypothesis)

The root cause is that `settlePartialBaseEntry` books the deal directly from
the settled row (`handleUnknownOrder(settled)`) without ever offering that row
to the remainder machinery the engine already has. The machinery itself is
correct — it is simply never asked.

Two secondary premises, both of which must hold for the fix to work on the
venue the report came from:

- **§3.1** a MARKET remainder is denominated correctly on Coinbase.
  `sendOrderToExchange` converts base→quote for `coinbase`/`bybit`/`bitget`
  MARKET BUYs before the connector builds `market_market_ioc.quote_size`.
- **§3.2** Coinbase reports a market order that could not fill in full as
  `CANCELED` carrying its partial `filled_size` — its connector maps every
  non-`OPEN`/`PENDING`/`FILLED` status to `CANCELED`. `buyRemainder`'s success
  gate recognises that shape only for `bybit`, so a Coinbase top-up that fills
  part way would be BOUGHT AND NOT RECORDED. This is the one part of the
  triage hypothesis that survives, and it is a prerequisite here rather than
  the root cause.

## §4 Required behaviour

### §4.1 The decision

A settled base entry is topped up when, and only when:

- the settle promoted the row to `FILLED` (there is a deal to open at all);
- the row executed strictly less than it requested;
- **the entry decision is still current** — see §4.2.

### §4.2 Only while the entry is still current

The settle has three callers, and two of them act seconds after the venue last
touched the order (the enter-market timer; the order queue's cancel callback
for a MARKET entry). The third is the bot's **restore path**, which recovers
rows that spec `038` measured at 2 h 41 m and 9.9 h old. Buying the missing
part of a nine-hour-old entry at today's price is not what the user asked for,
so age decides it, not the call site.

The bound is the bot's OWN entry window —
`orderLimitRepositionTimeout + enterMarketTimeout` — plus 60 s of slack for
venue and queue latency. At defaults that is 105 s, against a live path that
settles ~13 s after the last venue touch and a restore path measured in hours.

### §4.3 Topping up reuses `buyRemainder` unchanged

No new order-placement, sizing, averaging or minimum-quantity code. The settled
row is handed to `fillPartiallyFilledOrder`, which is what already owns
`allowToProcessBr`, the per-order mutex, the `partiallyFilledFilledSet`
de-duplication and `buyRemainder` itself. `buyRemainder` already:

- declines a remainder below the venue's base/quote minimum (and honours the
  `remainderFullAmount` bot setting where it applies);
- merges the fill back into the same row at the blended average price;
- recurses, bounded at 20, until the row is whole or the remainder is too small;
- falls through to the row it has when the venue refuses.

Because the top-up happens **before** `handleUnknownOrder`, the deal opens once,
on the topped-up quantity, at the blended price — no second open, no resize.

### §4.4 The `coinbase` guard is lifted only for this caller

`fillPartiallyFilledOrder`'s `coinbase` return predates the public repository
and is undocumented, so it is kept for the stream-driven path it was written
for. The settle opts out of it explicitly, because on that path every quantity
involved is a venue-reported BASE amount and the remainder is sent as a fresh
MARKET order whose Coinbase quote-denomination is applied in
`sendOrderToExchange` (§3.1).

### §4.5 The success gate must recognise the venue's IOC shape

`buyRemainder` merges a remainder order it considers successful. Extended from
`bybit` to `coinbase` per §3.2 — otherwise a partly filled Coinbase top-up is a
position the deal does not know it owns, which is strictly worse than the
underfill this spec removes. `bybit`'s existing scope (`order.type === 'MARKET'`
on the ORIGINAL order) is preserved exactly.

### §4.6 The cut-short report

`reportBaseEntryCutShort` (spec `048` §4.4) tells the user the deal is opening
on less than was asked for. When the top-up completes the entry that is no
longer true, so the message is raised only if the row is still short after the
top-up. A partial top-up still reports, naming the quantity finally reached.

## §5 Out of scope

- The `coinbase` guard in `fillPartiallyFilledOrder` for every OTHER caller.
  Its rationale is not recorded anywhere and no evidence was found for or
  against it; it is left exactly as it is.
- `buyRemainder`'s ENTRY gate. The settled row is always `FILLED` by the time
  it arrives, so extending that gate's `bybit`-only `MARKET`+`CANCELED` arm
  would change nothing on this path.
- The reposition timer's treatment of a partial fill (spec `038` §4.3).
- Whether the remainder should be bought as a LIMIT rather than at market.
