# 292 — A DCA close charges the fee of an order that filled part-way and was then cancelled

## Definition
When a DCA deal closes by take-profit, `closeDeal` books
`profit.total = Δbalances − commission`. The balances include every fill of the deal,
among them an order (usually a take-profit) that filled part-way and was then cancelled:
a `CANCELED` row with `executedQty > 0`. The `feePaid` ledger written by the same close
also counts that row's fee.

`commission`, and so the fee subtracted from `profit.total`, came from `getCommDeal`,
which read `FILLED` rows only. The fee the venue charged for the cancelled partial fill
was therefore left out of both. Profit was overstated by exactly that fee, the deal's
commission disagreed with its own `feePaid`, and nothing reported an error.

## Evidence
- E1. `closeDeal` builds `dealOrders` from `['FILLED', 'CANCELED']` rows with
  `executedQty > 0`, excluding `br`/`rebalance`/`funding`, and sums `feePaid` over them.
  It then called `getCommDeal(findDeal.deal)`, which reads `status: 'FILLED'` only.
- E2. Observed on production closed DCA deals, on venues that charge fees in quote:
  `feePaid.quote − commission` equals, within rounding, the fee of the deal's
  part-filled cancelled take-profit. On one such deal,
  `total + commission = pureQuote + feeQuote + (pureBase + feeBase) × price` holds exactly,
  so `feePaid` had the fee and `commission` did not.
- E3. `feePaid` is written only by that loop in `closeDeal`. A stored `feePaid` containing
  the cancelled fee proves the loop saw the row that `getCommDeal` skipped.

## Assumption
- A1. Which orders are placed, and how, is not touched. Only the booked commission and
  profit change.
- A2. Combo deals keep the `FILLED`-only count. Their take-profit price is solved from
  `getCommDeal` (`fullFee`), and their close books against the minigrid ledger, which
  this spec does not cover.
- A3. Every other caller of `getCommDeal` (close without a take-profit, external close,
  cancel with realised profit, multi-TP partial booking, the `fullFee` writes) keeps the
  default `FILLED` set.

## Behaviour
- §1 `getCommDeal(deal, orders?)` sums `orders` when given; without it, it reads the deal's
  `FILLED` rows exactly as before.
- §2 `closeDeal` on a non-combo deal passes `dealOrders`, the same set its `feePaid` loop
  sums. So:
  - §3.1 `commission` includes the fee of a part-filled cancelled order;
  - §3.2 `profit.total` is sold − cost − every fee charged;
  - §3.3 on a quote-profit deal, `commission = feePaid.quote + feePaid.base × lastPrice` when
    every fee is in quote (no double count: one order set feeds both);
  - §3.4 `getCommDeal` with no `orders` still counts `FILLED` rows only.
- §4 Side effect, intended: a `FILLED` `br`, `rebalance` or `funding` row's fee no longer
  enters a DCA close's commission. `feePaid` and the deal ledger already excluded them, and
  funding stays out of the deal's fee ledger by design (spec 044).

## Invariants
- `deals.closed-dca-commission-below-fee-paid` (main-app `specs/invariants/deals.invariants.json`,
  `mode: observe`): a quote-profit DCA deal closed in the last 24 h, all fees in quote, not a
  liquidation, whose `commission` is more than 1% below `feePaid.quote`.
