# 279 — A Kraken Futures partial liquidation is booked on the deals that held the position

Bug spec (Definition / Evidence / Assumption) with its design. Code: `src/bot/dca/partialLiquidation.ts`
(rules), `src/bot/dcaHelper.ts` (`processLiquidationOrder`, `bookPartialLiquidation`,
`sweepPartialLiquidations`), `src/bot/main.ts` (`onLiquidation` carries the chunk's quantity).
Tests: `src/bot/dca/partialLiquidation.spec.ts`, `src/bot/dca/partialLiquidation.harness.spec.ts`,
`src/bot/combo/comboDealFunds.harness.spec.ts` (`spec 279` block). Pack: main-app
`specs/279.invariants.json`. Repair: main-app migration v60. Related: spec `040` (liquidation
closes the deal), spec `277` (a position closed outside the bot), spec `047` (combo add / reduce
funds), websocket-connector-sh `isKrakenLiquidationFill` (the stream flag).

## §1 Definition

Kraken Futures liquidates a position in chunks: one venue order per chunk, about ten seconds apart,
none with a client order id, each tagged `fill_type: partialLiquidation`. It may stop before the
position is gone.

- §1.1 **Expected.** When the venue stops with part of the position still open, the open deals of the
  bot on that symbol give up the liquidated base, newest deal first (a one-way position is shared by
  the bot's deals). The loss is realised at the chunks' volume-weighted price. The deals stay open
  and the bot keeps managing what is left: take profit, grid exits and average follow the smaller
  position. The user gets a bot message. A deal whose whole position went is closed as liquidated at
  the venue's fills (spec `277`).
- §1.2 **Actual.** The stream flags the chunks as liquidations and the engine waits for the venue to
  answer that the position is gone (`processLiquidationOrder`). When it does not, the engine stops
  after 8 checks with a message ("…the deal stays open on what is left") and changes nothing. The
  deal keeps the units the venue sold, so its reduce-only exits are sized above the position and
  refused `wouldNotReducePosition`, its average and P&L are wrong, and the loss is never booked.
  Before the stream flag (websocket-connector-sh, 2026-11-25) the chunks were dropped as unknown
  orders and the same state arose with no message at all.
- §1.3 **Unchanged.** A liquidation that leaves the venue flat closes the deals as before (spec
  `040`, priced from the venue's fills per spec `277`). Binance, Bybit, OKX, Bitget, HL, paper,
  Kraken COIN-M and hedge-mode accounts keep today's behaviour: `partialLiquidationBookable()` is
  Kraken USD-M one-way only.

## §2 Evidence

- §2.1 Kraken Futures combo bot, ONDO-USD long. The account's fill history shows 9 chunks of 3
  (`partialLiquidation`, 10-13 11:16–11:18Z) and 10 venue orders totalling 120 (11-14
  07:19–07:21Z). Neither was booked by the bot.
- §2.2 10-13: the deal held 27, the venue liquidated 27. The next reduce-only exit was refused
  `wouldNotReducePosition` and the deal was closed "without order" with close trigger `manual`,
  booking about +0.30 more than the liquidation's real result.
- §2.3 11-14: the open deal's books hold 238 against 78 on the venue (read 2026-11-26). 120 of the
  160 gap is the unbooked partial liquidation. The other 40 is grid sells stored CANCELED (#1831's
  defect, out of scope here).

## §3 Design

- §3.1 **The quantity lives in an order row.** `updateDealBalances` rebuilds a deal's balances from its
  FILLED/CANCELED rows on every start, so a direct balance edit is overwritten. Each deal's share is
  a FILLED reduce-funds row: client order id `LIQ-<deal tail>-<first chunk id>` (deterministic),
  `reduceFundsId` the same id, `typeOrder: dealTP`, closing side, `orderId` the chunks' venue order
  ids comma separated, price the chunks' VWAP. It is persisted (`saveOrderToDb`), put in the order
  map, and booked by `processFilledOrder`. That routes a reduce-funds fill to the DCA `updateDeal`
  reduce branch, or to the combo funds booking (spec `047` §2.3), which:
  - realises `qty × (price − avg)` (long; mirrored short) at the deal's average;
  - appends `reduceFunds`, takes `size` down (`bookReduceFundsFill`);
  - scales every live minigrid and every unfilled level by `k = 1 − qty / position`, refits the exit
    lines to the lot and caps them at the new position;
  - rebuilds the book, re-reading balances, usage, average and TP from the rows.

  Every ledger reader already counts a reduce row: `updateDealBalances`, both futures average folds
  (a reduction keeps the average), the take-profit sizing, the close, the combo grid ledger and the
  order re-sizing that follows. The unfilled DCA levels shrink by the same `k` as a reduce funds
  would. That is the price of reusing spec 047's one scaling rule, which keeps the combo TP equation
  and the minigrid budgets consistent with the position.
- §3.2 **Capped at what the books hold above the venue** (`planPartialLiquidation`):
  `qty = min(Σ chunks, books − held)`, floored to the lot. The books never end below the venue. A
  repeat of the same chunks books nothing. A bot whose books are below the venue's position (another
  bot shares it) books nothing. Drift from another cause is not booked as a liquidation beyond the
  chunks' own quantity.
- §3.3 **Idempotent on the venue's order ids.** Chunks already named in a `LIQ-` row of this bot and
  symbol (in memory, or in `orders` by the anchored `^LIQ-` prefix on the unique `clientOrderId`
  index) are skipped. Combo bookings are also keyed on `fundsScale` ids, and DCA ones on
  `processedFilled` / `dealUpdateOrders`.
- §3.4 **Where the chunks come from.** On settle exhaustion, the venue's account fills (back to the
  oldest open deal, at most 5 pages): forced-close `fillType`, closing side, symbol. Chunks the
  stream reported that the fills do not show yet are added from the stream (`onLiquidation` now
  receives the chunk's executed quantity; the persisted liquidation row keeps `executedQty: '0'` as
  spec `040` §4.3 requires).
- §3.5 **The sweep** (`sweepPartialLiquidations`) catches a liquidation the stream missed (a
  reconnect gap, a restart mid-liquidation). It is armed with the reconciliation heartbeat on every
  DCA and Combo start path, 90 s after the start and then every 30 min, and stopped with it. For each
  symbol with an open deal that the stream is not settling, it reads the venue position and books as
  above. It reads no fills when the books do not exceed the venue.
- §3.6 Message: "Part of the X position was liquidated by krakenUsdm: Q at P. The deal was reduced by
  that amount and the loss booked; H is still open on the exchange and the bot keeps managing it",
  plus the reduce alert the booking already sends.
- §3.7 **Realised at the average held when it happened** (amendment, bug).
  - *Definition.* A partial liquidation realises `qty × (price − avg)` where `avg` is the deal's
    average at the liquidation. A booking made later (the sweep, or after a restart) realised it at
    the average held at booking time. Safety orders that filled in between had already moved that
    average, so the result was wrong.
  - *Evidence.* An open ONDO-USD long: 120 held at a fold average of 0.3446513 when the venue
    liquidated 120 at 0.3254375. Six safety orders (118) filled before the first sweep booked it, and
    by then the deal's average was 0.2913. The booking realised +4.0965 instead of −2.3057.
  - *Assumption → change.* Both reduce bookings read `deal.avgPrice` as it is now. When a deal row
    has filled after the chunks' time, `bookPartialLiquidation` folds the deal's rows up to that
    time, using the same futures fold as `getAvgPrice` and combo `avgPrice` with an `asOf` cut. It
    hands the result to the reduce booking (`liquidationRealiseAvg`, keyed by the row's id, cleared
    after). The quantity booked does not change. A booking with no later fill passes no override and
    is unchanged.
  - The skip line "Partial liquidation of X not booked: …" is logged at debug level. It fires on
    every sweep.

## §4 Data

- §4.1 New order rows (`LIQ-…`, existing fields only). No schema change.
- §4.2 Repair, main-app migration v60 (operator card, `--version=60 --apply` only): the 10-13
  closed deal gets its liquidation booked. Δ = (its rows' quote + 27 × 0.461) − stored total, about
  −0.30. Close trigger becomes `liquidation`, flag `closedExternally`, backup in `repairbackups_279`,
  compare-and-swap. The running bot's profit takes the same Δ through its worker
  (`applyProfitCorrection`). The 11-14 open deal is booked by the engine's own first sweep after the
  deploy (§3.5). The migration only reports it.
