# 167 — Deals left on indicator close after the bot moves to a percentage take profit

Bug spec (Definition / Evidence / Assumption).

## §1 Definition

- **§1.1 Expected.** A deal keeps the close conditions it was opened with
  (`deal.settings`). When the user switches the bot from "close by indicator"
  to a percentage take profit without applying the change to open deals, the
  deals opened before the switch still close when their close-deal indicator
  group fires, and only once their own minimum profit (`useMinTP` / `minTp`,
  or a P&L indicator ANDed into the group) is met. The same holds for a stop
  loss by indicator.
- **§1.2 Actual.** Those deals never close. They have no resting take profit
  (they were opened under indicator close), and the bot no longer subscribes
  its close-deal indicators, so no signal ever reaches `closeAllDeals`, which
  is the one place that would still select them by their own settings.
- **§1.3 Unchanged.** Deals opened under the percentage take profit keep their
  resting limit take profit exactly as now; an indicator signal never closes
  them (`closeAllDeals` keeps selecting deals by their own close condition).

## §2 Evidence

- A live bot was switched from close-by-indicator (one RSI close group plus a
  1 % minimum profit) to a 2 % percentage take profit. Deals opened after the
  switch rest a LIMIT take profit and close. Every deal opened before it still
  reads `dealCloseCondition: techInd, useMinTP: true, minTp: 1` in its own
  settings, has no take-profit order, and stays open far past +1 %.
- `isIndicator` and the per-indicator gate in `connectSettingsIndicator` read
  only the bot-level aggregated settings (`getAggregatedSettings()` with no
  deal). With `dealCloseCondition: tp` both reject every `closeDeal`
  indicator. A settings save rebuilds the subscriptions (`reloadBot` with
  `rebuildIndicators`), so the close indicators are dropped at once.
- `checkMinTp` returns `true` (no minimum) whenever the bot-level
  `allowedMethods` lacks `checkMinTp`, which `checkAllowedMethods` also derives
  from the bot-level settings only. Its P&L-indicator branches likewise read
  `this.data.settings.dealCloseCondition(SL)` instead of the deal's.

## §3 Assumption (root cause)

- **§3.1** Whether close-deal indicators are needed is decided from the bot's
  current settings alone, although the deals they close are selected — and
  their minimum profit is defined — by each deal's own settings.
- **§3.2** Re-subscribing alone would be unsafe: with the bot-level gate,
  `checkMinTp` would wave every such deal through, so an always-true signal
  would market-close deals below their own minimum profit.

## §4 Required behaviour

- **§4.1** `isIndicator` is true while an open deal's own settings close by
  indicator (`useTp` + `dealCloseCondition: techInd`, or `useSl` +
  `dealCloseConditionSL: techInd`) and the bot has close-deal indicators for
  that section.
- **§4.2** `openIndicators` subscribes the close-deal indicators of a section
  on every symbol that has such a deal, even when the bot's own close
  condition for that section is no longer indicator.
- **§4.3** `checkMinTp` applies the deal's own minimum profit when the deal's
  own settings close by signal, regardless of the bot-level `allowedMethods`
  flag; its P&L-indicator branches use the deal's close condition.
- **§4.4** §1.3 holds: a percentage-take-profit deal on the same symbol is not
  closed by the signal.
