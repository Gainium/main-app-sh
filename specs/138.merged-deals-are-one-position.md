# 138 — Merged deals are one position

Bug-fix spec for the manual "merge deals" action (`mergeDeals` /
`mergeComboDeals`). Prerequisite for any automatic merge: a merge has to leave
the statistics, the realtime feed and both bot types correct before it can be
triggered by the engine instead of a user.

## §1 How a merge works

- §1.1 The engine (`dcaHelper.mergeDeals`) reads the source deals' filled
  orders, creates a NEW deal (`parent: true`, `childIds`) holding the combined
  position, cancels every source deal through the ordinary close path
  (`CloseDCATypeEnum.cancel`, so `status: canceled`, `initialPrice > 0`), and
  marks the sources `{ child: true, parentId }`. The source deals' filled
  orders are re-pointed to the new deal.
- §1.2 A source deal is therefore a **merged-away deal**: its position did not
  end, it continues in the parent. Only the parent ever closes the position.

## §2 Behaviour wanted

### §2.1 Statistics — a merged-away deal is not a deal

- §2.1.1 A merged-away deal is excluded from every **per-deal** statistic:
  closed-deal counts, wins / losses, win rate, streaks, best / worst deal,
  average deal profit / loss, deal durations (avg / max, winning / losing),
  intra-deal drawdown, DCA levels triggered, price deviation, usage maxima,
  and the per-deal profit-percent history.
- §2.1.2 **Money it already booked still counts** in the read-side folds:
  realized profit, gross profit / loss (by its sign), profit factor, fees and
  the equity drawdown. The cancel path has already credited any such profit
  to the bot's profit total. (A DCA source normally books nothing while open;
  combo merges made before §2.4 left booked minigrid profit on their
  sources.) The engine's incremental `botUpdateStats` skips a merged-away
  deal entirely.
- §2.1.3 **Capital.** A merged-away deal held its capital from its creation
  until the merge, and the parent holds the combined capital from the merge
  on. The peak-concurrent-capital sweep counts a merged-away deal only up to
  the parent's creation time, so the moment of the merge is not counted twice
  (the parent is created before its sources are cancelled). When the parent
  is not in the population being swept, the source keeps its own close time.
- §2.1.4 Applies to the read-side folds (`pairStats.ts`, `botWindowStats.ts`)
  and to the engine's incremental `botUpdateStats` alike. The engine learns a
  canceled deal was merged away from the deal document: the merge writes
  `{ child: true, parentId }` on the sources **before** it cancels them (it is
  kept after the cancel too, unchanged).
- §2.1.5 No migration. Existing incremental `bot.stats` keep whatever earlier
  merges added; the read-side folds are corrected for all history.

### §2.2 Counters that measure opened deals

- §2.2.1 "Close after X deals opened" (bot controller) counts deals the bot
  opened. A merge opens nothing: the merged deal (`parent: true`) is not
  counted, its sources still are. Merging N deals leaves the count at N
  (it used to become N + 1).
- §2.2.2 The DCA-usage histogram buckets the DCA orders each deal actually
  filled. The merged deal carries a synthetic one-level ladder, so it is not
  bucketed; its sources are (each ran its own ladder).

### §2.3 Realtime order event

- §2.3.1 When a merge re-points a filled order to the merged deal, the emitted
  order carries the new `dealId`, `botId` and `typeOrder` at the top level —
  the same values written to the database. (It used to carry the old values,
  with the update nested under a `data` key.)

### §2.4 Combo deals are not merged

- §2.4.1 A combo position is spread over its minigrids: their fills move the
  deal's balances, and the deal books minigrid profit that only its close
  credits to the bot. One merged deal cannot take that over (it has no
  minigrids, and re-pointing the fills would book their profit twice), so
  combo merge is not offered — as in the legacy dashboard, which never showed
  it for combo bots.
- §2.4.2 The engine refuses `mergeDeals` on a combo bot, and
  `mergeComboDeals` answers `NOTOK` ("Merging deals is not available for
  combo bots") without dispatching. The mutation stays in the schema.
- §2.4.3 The redesign dashboard does not offer Merge for combo or hedge-combo
  deals, and — as the legacy dashboard did — only for deals of one bot.

### §2.5 Merged deal fields

- §2.5.1 The merged deal is seeded with a funding cursor at the merge time,
  as `createDeal` does (the funding commit is a compare-and-swap on
  `funding.offset`; without it every funding write is dropped).
- §2.5.2 It carries the deal flags that every source carries.

## §3 Out of scope

- Automatic merging / one position per pair.
- A merged deal's take-profit: it gets one only when the bot closes by
  indicator or webhook (`useTp` is cleared otherwise) — unchanged here.
- Rewriting historical `bot.stats` documents.
