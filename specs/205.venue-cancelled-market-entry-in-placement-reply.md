# 205 — a MARKET entry the venue answers CANCELED waits on a stream report that may never come

Bug spec. Follows `263de26`/`f4416a9` (the cancelled-entry check) and spec
`203` (the same check for entries that hold reposition timers).

## 1. Definition

### 1.1 Expected

When the venue's answer to a deal's MARKET base order already says CANCELED
with nothing executed, and the bot did not ask for that cancel, the deal is
cancelled about `canceledBaseEntryDelayMs` later and the bot is free to open
a new one. The order queue's cancel report does not have to arrive for this.

### 1.2 Actual

Only the order queue's CANCELED report (`processCanceledOrder`) reaches
`checkUnfilledBaseEntryCancel`. `placeBaseOrder` — DCA and Combo — handles an
answer that is not a string and not FILLED by arming the reposition and
enter-market timers, and only for a LIMIT entry. A MARKET entry arms nothing.
If the user stream loses the cancel report (a user-stream restart in the same
second is the observed case), the deal stays in `start` with nothing on the
venue until the old-start-deals cron cancels it 24 h later. Nothing is logged.

## 2. Evidence

Production, a Bybit DCA bot with `startOrderType: MARKET`:

- The deal's only order is a MARKET `dealStart` row, status CANCELED,
  `executedQty` 0, `updateTime` 4 ms after `transactTime`: the CANCELED status
  was in the venue's placement answer.
- The bot log has `Deal created`, `Send base order`, `Save order …`, then
  nothing for the deal: no `Processing msg … CANCELED` and no `was canceled on
  the exchange …` line. A user-stream restart falls in the same window.
- Every other entry of the same bot cancelled this way in the same window was
  cancelled 15 s later through the queue path, because its report arrived.
- The sentinel invariant `deals.start-stuck-nothing-resting` raised the deal.

Harness (`dca/venueCanceledBaseEntry.harness.spec.ts`, spec 205 block, and
`comboUnbuiltBaseEntry.harness.spec.ts`, spec 205 block) drives the real
`placeBaseOrder` with an answer of `{status: 'CANCELED', executedQty: '0'}`
and no queue event. Before the fix §4.1 fails in both: no check is armed.

## 3. Assumption

The placement answer is a second report of the same venue cancel, and the
check it should arm already exists and is idempotent:
`armCanceledBaseEntryDealCancel` keeps one pending check per deal and a later
report replaces it, and `cancelDealOfCanceledBaseEntry` re-reads the deal,
the own-cancel record and the live entries before it acts. So passing the
answer to `checkUnfilledBaseEntryCancel` is enough; no new decision is needed.

## 4. Required behaviour

- §4.1 A MARKET base order whose placement answer is CANCELED with nothing
  executed, on a `start` deal, not cancelled by the bot, with no other live
  `dealStart` row, arms the cancelled-entry check — DCA and Combo.
- §4.2 The queue's report of the same cancel arriving afterwards leaves one
  pending check, and the deal is cancelled once.
- §4.3 An answer that is resting (NEW), carries a fill (spec 048 owns it), is
  EXPIRED (the queue path does not arm on EXPIRED either), or names a cancel
  the bot recorded, arms nothing.
- §4.4 A LIMIT entry (DCA) is unchanged: its reposition timers are armed as
  before and the placement answer arms no check. Spec 203 §4.4 covers it.
- Not changed: which orders are placed, re-placed or retried.
