# 156 — A cancelled, partly filled KuCoin safety order is completed at market

Bug spec (Definition / Evidence / Assumption). Tests:
`src/bot/cancelledLimitNoTopUp.spec.ts`.

## §1 Definition

A DCA safety order (`typeOrder: dealRegular`) is a resting LIMIT order. When it
is cancelled after part of it filled — by its owner on the exchange, or by the
bot (lowering the deal's order count, `cancelTerminalDealOrder`) — the part that
filled belongs to the deal and the rest does not exist.

**Expected.**

- §1.1 On KuCoin spot, a cancelled safety order that filled part of its size is
  booked with what filled. No remainder order (`GA-BR-…`) is sent for the rest.
  The skip is logged with the quantities that decided it.
- §1.2 Unchanged: a KuCoin MARKET order the venue ended short (the shape
  `buyRemainder` exists for) is still completed with a remainder order.
- §1.3 Unchanged: the base entry (`dealStart`) and take-profit (`dealTP`) top-ups,
  and every other venue's top-up, behave exactly as before.

**Actual.** The rest is sold (short) or bought (long) at market at once, and the
cancelled order's row is rewritten as if it had filled in full at the blended
price.

## §2 Evidence (prod, read-only)

- §2.1 Bot logs for one KuCoin STX-USDT short DCA deal:
  - `2026-10-12T21:21:40Z Processing msg … D-RO-y9yZ…, PARTIALLY_FILLED, 0.4646, base: 59.9214` (of 58778.9383).
  - `2026-10-13T16:55:12Z Api call cancelTerminalDealOrder [… D-RO-y9yZ…]` →
    `Cannot cancel processing order` (the in-app cancel refuses a non-`NEW` order).
  - `16:55:56Z` the deal's take-profit arrives `CANCELED` with no cancel request
    from the bot, then `16:56:03Z Processing msg … D-RO-y9yZ…, FILLED, 0.4646,
    base: 59.9214` — the order was cancelled on the exchange by its owner.
  - `16:56:03Z Order D-RO-y9yZ… not SELL full qty: executed - 59.9214, total -
    58778.9383, diff - 58719.0169`, then `Buy remainder executed - GA-BR-Ttq0…,
    SELL, base - 58719.0169, quote - 25053.41`.
- §2.2 The `orders` row for `D-RO-y9yZ…` now reads `type LIMIT, FILLED,
  executedQty 58778.9383, price 0.4267` (origPrice 0.4646). The deal's `size` is
  29812.2233 = the six earlier fills (29752.3019) + 59.9214, so the 58719 sold at
  market is not in the deal, and the re-placed take-profit
  (`D-TP-tukK…`, 31779.0479) only covers the deal's own size.
- §2.3 The `FILLED` status comes from `@gainium/kucoin-api`
  `convertOrderUpdate`: a spot stream message `type: 'canceled', status: 'done'`
  with a non-zero `filledSize` is reported as `FILLED`. Its REST twin reports the
  same order `CANCELED`. Every other venue reports a cancelled partial LIMIT order
  as `CANCELED`, which `buyRemainder` does not act on.
- §2.4 120 days of KuCoin spot `br` rows on prod, matched to the order they
  completed: 4 completed a LIMIT safety order (2 users, ≈25.4k USDT of market
  orders), against 30 that completed a MARKET safety order, 13 + 8 a base entry
  and 18 a take-profit (all unchanged by this spec).

## §3 Assumption (root cause — hypothesis)

`buyRemainder` treats every `FILLED` row with `executedQty < origQty` as an
order the venue under-filled. On KuCoin spot a resting LIMIT order cannot end
short except by being cancelled, and the stream reports that cancel as `FILLED`
(§2.3), so the engine completes a cancelled safety order at market. The KuCoin
mapping stays as it is: the engine's own cancel path promotes a cancelled order
with fills to `FILLED` too (`setFilledInsteadOfCanceled`), and the deal books
the filled part through that status.

A test driving the real `buyRemainder` with the §2.1 row must see a `GA-BR`
order sent — that is the red.

## §4 Out of scope

- §4.1 The in-app cancel refusing a partly filled order
  (`Cannot cancel processing order`) — what sent the owner to the exchange.
- §4.2 The merged `br` quantity not reaching the deal's `size` (§2.2).
- §4.3 Take-profit LIMIT top-ups: skipping them would leave a `FILLED`
  take-profit closing a deal on a fraction (see `setFilledInsteadOfCanceled`).
