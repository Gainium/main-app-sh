# 235 — A part-filled LIMIT entry buys its remainder twice

Bug spec (Definition / Evidence / Assumption). Code: `src/bot/main.ts`
`buyRemainder` / `remainderMayGoToMarket`, `src/bot/dcaHelper.ts`
`remainderMayGoToMarket` / `restBaseEntryRemainder`.
Tests: `src/bot/dca/limitEntryNoMarketTopUp.harness.spec.ts`.

## §1 Definition

§1.1 Expected. A DCA base entry that ends short has ONE owner for its unfilled
rest, decided by `baseEntryMayEnterAtMarket`:
- a bot that may enter at market (MARKET entry, or LIMIT with the "Enter
  Market Timeout" switch on) buys it at MARKET (`buyRemainder`, specs `057`,
  `103` §4.4);
- a bot that may not rests it as a LIMIT add-funds order (spec `111`), and
  never sends a MARKET order on its own (spec `111` §1.1.6).

§1.2 Actual. `buyRemainder` topped up every `FILLED`-short order at MARKET
without asking whether the bot may enter at market. Only the settle path
(`topUpSettledBaseEntry`) asked. A venue that reports an ended part-fill as
`FILLED` (KuCoin), or a placement answered `FILLED` short, therefore bought
the remainder at market, and `startDeal` then rested the same remainder as a
LIMIT, from a row a venue re-read had already put back to its book quantity.
Both filled: the position held the remainder twice.

§1.3 Not changing. Bots that may enter at market are topped up at market as
before. Safety orders, take profits and combo deals (which rest nothing) keep
the top-up as before.

## §2 Evidence and behaviour

§2.1 On a bot that may not enter at market, a base entry reported `FILLED`
short (user stream, placement answer) sends no remainder order and is not
marked as topped up.

§2.2 The production sequence — settle cancelling, venue report `FILLED` at
173.5435 of 11278.9777, venue re-read `CANCELED`, `startDeal` — buys the
11105.4342 exactly once, as the resting LIMIT.

§2.3 A safety order and a combo base entry on the same bot are still topped
up at market.

§2.4 A bot that may enter at market: LIMIT with the timeout on, or a MARKET
entry, is topped up at market, and nothing is rested afterwards.

§2.5 `restBaseEntryRemainder` rests nothing for an entry whose remainder
`buyRemainder` already sent (`partiallyFilledFilledSet`), whatever the setting
says by then.

## §3 Assumption

The root cause is the missing `baseEntryMayEnterAtMarket` gate on
`buyRemainder`, which spec `111` relied on but only added to the settle path.
The `CANCELED` re-read that overwrote the merged row is a separate defect for
bots that may enter at market; for a bot that may not, no merge exists any
more, so the re-read writes what the venue really did.
