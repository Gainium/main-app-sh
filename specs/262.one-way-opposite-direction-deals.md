# 262 — Opposite-direction deals on a one-way futures account

Bug spec.

## 1. Definition

### 1.1 Expected

On a one-way futures account the venue keeps ONE net position per symbol.
Two bots of opposite direction on the same account and symbol therefore do
not each hold a position: the second one's entry only shrinks, or flips, the
first one's.

- §1.1.1 A DCA or combo bot does not open a deal against the direction the
  account already holds on that symbol.
- §1.1.2 A take-profit the venue refuses as "no position to reduce" because the
  net position sits on the OTHER side is reported as an error. It is not
  booked as a closed, profitable deal.
- §1.1.3 Unchanged: a refused close on a genuinely flat symbol is still booked
  closed without an order (spec 075, and the branch it extends).

### 1.2 Actual

`MainBot.loadData` refuses opposite directions at bot START only ("Cannot start
when existing position not met bot settings"). Nothing checks again when a
deal is opened later. A LONG deal opened while another bot's SHORT deal is open
only reduces the net short. Its reduce-only take-profit is refused by the venue
(Kraken Futures: `wouldNotReducePosition`). `isPositionAlreadyClosedReason`
matches that string, so `closeDealById` books the deal closed as a take-profit
with only its fees as profit. The short deals' records then show more short
exposure than the account holds.

## 2. Evidence

- Production bot log, two accounts, one hour apart: `Close order for deal …
  rejected because the position is already closed (wouldNotReducePosition).
  Closing deal without order`, on LONG deals opened while an opposite-strategy
  deal of another bot of the same user was open on the same symbol. In one
  account the opposite deal was opened after the LONG; in the other, before.
  Both orders reach the same state.
- One of the short deals was still open, and trailing, an hour after the LONG
  was booked closed. So the account's net position was short, not flat, when
  the close was refused.
- Read-only on production, the invariant in §6 finds several open LONG + SHORT
  deal groups on venues that have no hedge mode at all.

## 3. Assumption (root cause)

Two gaps, one mechanism:

1. No per-deal check of the direction held on a one-way account. The start
   check (`loadData`, spec 041 / 078) is the only one.
2. The `isPositionAlreadyClosedReason` branch of `closeDealById` reads every
   "would not reduce" refusal as "flat". On a one-way account that refusal also
   means "held on the other side".

## 4. Change

- §4.1 `refuseNewDealAgainstOpposingPosition`, in the new-deal gate chain of
  `openNewDealBody` (DCA) and `openNewDeal` (combo), after the delivery and
  refused-entry gates and before `checkMaxDeals`. It applies to one-way
  futures (`futures && !hedge`), DCA and combo only. It refuses when:
  - §4.1.1 another bot of the user holds an open deal of the opposite strategy
    on the same symbol and account (`findOpposingDealHolder`, now with an
    optional strategy filter that bot start does not pass); or
  - §4.1.2 failing that, the venue reports a non-zero net position on the
    opposite side.
  - §4.1.3 An unreadable venue answer does not refuse. A failed read must not
    stop trading.
  - §4.1.4 A manual open is answered to the user. An automatic one is reported
    as a bot warning, once per re-arm window (standing-condition latch). An
    ASAP bot has no other trigger to ask again, so it re-arms on a 60 s timer.
- §4.2 `closeDealById`: before the close-without-order branch, on one-way
  futures, read the venue position once (`netPositionHeldAgainst`). When it is
  held on the side opposite to this deal, the refusal goes to
  `handleOrderErrors` with a message that names the net side. The deal is not
  booked closed. Flat, the deal's own side, no answer, or a hedge account:
  unchanged.
- §4.0 Pure helpers in `dca/opposingNetPosition.ts`: `netPositionSide` uses
  `loadData`'s side derivation, and `heldAgainst` works on a
  `VenuePositionProbe`.

## 5. Not changed

- Safety orders of a deal that is already open are not gated. Every new
  opposite open is refused from now on, so an SO meets an opposite net position
  only on state created before this change or a position opened outside the
  bot. That case is filed separately.
- Hedge accounts, spot, grid bots.

## 6. Invariants

`main-app` `specs/invariants/deals.invariants.json`,
`deals.one-way-opposite-direction-open` (observe): there must be no open LONG
and SHORT DCA deals on the same user, account and symbol on a venue with no
hedge mode (Kraken Futures USD-M / COIN-M, Hyperliquid perps). If there are,
the second direction's entries only shrink the first one's position, and its
take-profit can never be placed.

## 7. Tests

`src/bot/dca/opposingNetPosition.harness.spec.ts`: §4.0, §4.1.1–§4.1.4, §4.2
and §1.1.3 (flat, own side, unreadable, throwing read, hedge). It drives the
real `closeDealById`, the real `refuseNewDealAgainstOpposingPosition` and the
real `findOpposingDealHolder`.
