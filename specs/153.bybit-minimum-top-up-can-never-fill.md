# 153 — A Bybit spot "remainder full amount" top-up that can never fill

Bug spec (Definition / Evidence / Assumption). Tests:
`src/bot/unfillableTopUp.spec.ts`.

## §1 Definition

A DCA bot with **Remainder full amount** on tops up a base order that the venue
filled short. When the shortfall is below the venue's minimums, `buyRemainder`
replaces it with `baseAsset.minAmount` and sends that as a MARKET order
(`GA-BR-…`), whatever that minimum is worth.

**Expected.** The engine sends nothing it knows the venue will refuse.

- §1.1 On Bybit spot, a BUY top-up worth less than the pair's
  `quoteAsset.minAmount` (Bybit's own `minOrderAmt`) is not sent. The skip is
  logged with the numbers that decided it.
- §1.2 A Bybit spot market buy whose quote amount rounds to zero or below is not
  sent on any path. It is refused locally with the existing
  `Order qty must be greater than zero` reason, and its write-ahead row is
  removed, not left behind.
- §1.3 Unchanged: a top-up worth at least the minimum is sent exactly as before,
  and the top-up quantity is still `baseAsset.minAmount`. It is **not** raised to
  the venue's minimum order value. No other venue's top-up is skipped.

**Actual.** Both orders are sent and refused:

- `qty 0.00001, price 60.48, side BUY` on LTCUSDT → `round(0.00001 × 60.48, 2)`
  = `0` USDT → `An unknown parameter was sent.`
- Sub-minimum but non-zero amounts → `Order value exceeded lower limit.`

## §2 Evidence (prod `Gainium-DCA-bot` logs, read-only)

- §2.1 `2026-10-08T13:30:44Z 6ac788d8… Order D-BO-… not BUY full qty: executed -
  0.10135, total - 0.10183, diff - 0.00048, remainder is less than allowed on
  exchange and will be increased to exchange minimum 0.00001`, then
  `Reason An unknown parameter was sent. Method limitOrders() Step Send new order
  request GA-BR-…, qty 0.00001, price 64.21, side BUY`. The same shape recurs
  through `2026-10-13T00:07:07Z` (`price 60.48`). `pairs` stores Bybit LTCUSDT
  with `baseAsset.minAmount 0.00001`, `quoteAsset.minAmount 1` and
  `priceAssetPrecision 2`.
- §2.2 Bybit spot top-up refusals in the retained logs: 1,883 lines read
  `Order value exceeded lower limit.` and 7 read `An unknown parameter was sent.`
  The first figure is the 2,000-line search cap, so the real count is higher. The
  refusals come from 589 bots, all `exchange: bybit`, and all are side BUY.
- §2.3 Every Bybit spot `Buy remainder executed` in the same window (15 orders,
  8 bots) was worth between 1.08 and 63.9 USDT, i.e. at or above the 1 USDT
  minimum. None of the filled orders was below it.
- §2.4 Counter-example that bounds the scope: a kucoinLinear ZETAUSDT top-up of
  `1 × 0.05142` (0.05 USDT, against a stored `quoteAsset.minAmount 1`) **filled**.
  So `quoteAsset.minAmount` is not a refusal threshold on every venue. A skip
  that applied to all venues would remove fills that happen today.

## §3 Assumption (root cause — hypothesis)

The engine checks the venue minimums before choosing to top up, then overrides
that check: `fullAmount` sets `diffMore = true` with `diff = baseAsset.minAmount`
and never compares the replacement's value with `quoteAsset.minAmount`. On Bybit
spot a market BUY is sized in quote (`sendOrderToExchange` converts `qty × price`
rounded to `priceAssetPrecision`), so the replacement is either below Bybit's
minimum order value or rounds to `0`. The zero-quantity guard (spec `084` §4.3)
runs on `origQty` before that conversion, so it never sees the zero.

## §4 Out of scope

- Raising the top-up to the venue minimum order value (it would buy more than it
  does today).
- Venues other than Bybit spot for §1.1 (no evidence that their minimum is
  enforced; see §2.4).
- The Bitget `size 0.000 > 0` top-up refusals in the same logs (a separate
  futures sizing path).
