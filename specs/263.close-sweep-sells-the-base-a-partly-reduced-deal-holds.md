# 263 — A spot deal that sold part of its coins leaves the rest behind when it closes

Bug spec (Definition / Evidence / Assumption). Tests:
`src/bot/closeSweepHeldBase.spec.ts`.

## §1 Definition

A spot LONG DCA deal can sell part of its position before it closes, with
reduce-funds sells (`D-TPR-…`). When it then closes, the close order is sized
by `getTPOrder`, and whatever that order leaves on the deal's base ledger is
sold by the close remainder sweep (`closeDeal` → `sellRemainder`, spec `157`).

**Expected.**

- §1.1 After the close and its sweep, the deal holds no base beyond what the
  venue took in base fees and the exchange minimums: every coin the deal
  bought and did not sell is sold.
- §1.2 The sweep deducts from the ledger remainder only the base fees the
  deal's rows actually paid — the venue's own report where it gave one, the
  account-rate estimate for an entry buy where it gave none — which is the same
  reading `closeDeal` books `profit.pureBase` against.
- §1.3 The close order of a spot long with executed reduce-funds is sized from
  the coins the base ledger says were entered, not from `deal.size`.

**Unchanged (§4).** Closes of deals with no executed reduce-funds are sized
exactly as before; partial sells (reduce-funds) are sized as before; spot SHORT
and futures sweeps deduct exactly as before; a sweep the venue refuses behaves
as in spec `157`.

**Actual.**

- The sweep deducted `executedQty × taker` over every entry BUY of the deal,
  whatever the venue had charged. On a venue that charged the fees in quote that
  is base the account holds; and after reduce-funds sells took most of the
  position, the deduction was as large as the whole remainder. The sweep fell
  under the minimum, sent nothing, and the close booked the coins as
  `profit.pureBase` on a deal whose profit currency is quote.
- With the base-order row missing from the order map, `getTPOrder` re-derived
  the base order from `|deal.size| + reduceFunds`. On a spot long `deal.size` is
  the quote ledger divided by the average price, and each reduce-funds sell
  moves the quote ledger by its sale price, not by the coins it sold, so that
  sum under-states the entry and the close under-sizes.

## §2 Evidence (prod, read-only, 2026-11-25)

- §2.1 Kraken XRP-EUR deal, closed 2026-11-09: entries 264,932.20 XRP (all
  reported fees in EUR, LIMIT fills report `0`), three `D-TPR` sells 260,553.38,
  final close `D-TP-BSo…` 4,113.89 = `264,932.20 × (1 − 0.1%) − 260,553.38`.
  Left on the ledger after close: 264.93 XRP — exactly `0.1% × 264,932.20`, the
  sweep's deduction. Booked `profit.pureBase` 251.71 (264.93 less 13.23 of
  estimated base fee on rows with no fee report). No `D-SR` row exists.
- §2.2 Four open Bybit USDC deals of one bot, each with 4–6 `D-TPR` sells:
  DOGE `size` 333.59 against 504.5 on the base ledger and 499.06 on the venue
  (entries 2,176.6, base fees reported 5.44, sold 1,672.1); LTC 0.586 vs 0.729;
  ADA 107.8 vs 153.2; AAVE 0.302 vs 0.379. With the base-order row present the
  close is already sized from the ledger (499.06); with it absent it sized 328.7.

## §3 Assumption (root cause)

- §3.1 `sellRemainder`'s `sellNotByOrder` deduction (the close sweep's only
  caller of it) estimated base fees from the account taker rate over entry BUYs
  regardless of the venue's fee report.
- §3.2 `getTPOrder` → `resolveBaseOrderQty`'s `deal` branch trusted
  `grossEntryVolume(size, reduceFunds)`, which is not a coin count on a spot long
  once reduce-funds sells have executed.

## §4 Fix

- §4.1 `dca/sweepBaseFee.ts` `sweepBaseFeesPaid`: base fees per executed row —
  observed on-pair base leg; else (not a third-asset fee on a `feeByAsset` deal)
  the `estimatedFeeRate` estimate on an entry BUY. Used by the spot-long sweep
  over the deal's FILLED/CANCELED rows less `br`/`rebalance`/`funding`, the same
  set `closeDeal`'s fee loop reads.
- §4.2 `getTPOrder`: on a spot long with executed reduce-funds and a non-zero
  base ledger, `grossEntry` is the ledger's entry (`heldEntry`).

## Invariants

- `dcadeals.closed-spot-long-keeps-base` (`main-app/specs/invariants/deals.invariants.json`):
  a closed spot LONG DCA deal whose profit currency is not base holds a
  `profit.pureBase` worth more than 1 quote unit and more than 1% of its size —
  coins left in the account that no deal manages, counted as profit.
