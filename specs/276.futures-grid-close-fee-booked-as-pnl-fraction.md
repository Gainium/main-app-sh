# 276 — A futures grid close books its fee as a fraction of the PnL

Bug spec.

## §1 Definition

### §1.1 Expected

When a futures grid's position is closed (`profitAfterPositionClosed`: the
stop / TP / SL close, its part-filled leg from spec 273) or liquidated
(`processLiquidationOrder`), the trading fee of the closing fill is taken out
of `profit.total`, `profit.freeTotal`, `profit.totalUsd`,
`profit.freeTotalUsd` and the account statistics (`saveProfitToDb`), so the
bot's profit equals the cash result of its fills.

The fee is the venue's observed fee of the close order (`observedFeeSplit` /
`observedFeeOnSide`, converted to quote at the fill price) when the bot books
observed fees (`BotFlags.feeByAsset`, the gate `createTransaction` uses),
scaled to the closed quantity when the order executed more. Otherwise it is
the stored maker-rate estimate on the close notional,
`price × qty × maker` — the estimate `createTransaction` makes for a sell leg.

### §1.2 Actual

`profitAfterPositionClosed` booked `grossPnl × (1 − maker)`: the fee as a
fraction of the PnL, not of the notional. On a losing close that "fee" even
raised the total. `processLiquidationOrder` booked no fee at all.

### §1.3 Unchanged

The order that is placed and its size; the transaction rows (a close writes
none); spot bots; the observed-fee fallback rule of `orderFee.ts` (an
unresolvable fee falls back to the estimate, never to zero).

## §2 Evidence

Production, Bybit USDⓈ-M, NEUTRAL futures grid with observed fees on
(identifiers withheld, because this file is public). Short 5 entered at
0.06362, closed by the GRID-TP BUY 5 at 0.06374, venue fee 0.00012748 quote;
the bot logged `Stop order filled. Processing stop...` at the fill.
Transaction rows end at 0.00184552; `profit.total` ends at 0.00124576, so
the close booked −0.00059976 = −0.0006 × (1 − 0.0004). Gross close PnL is
−0.0006, the fee should take out 0.00012748; it took out −0.00000024. The
harness reproduces the post-spec-275 total of 0.00111936 exactly.

## §3 Assumption (root cause)

The `(1 − maker)` factor was a stand-in for the fee, applied to the wrong
base. Subtracting the close fill's fee from the gross PnL is the fix; the USD
figure and the account statistics are derived from the same corrected value.

## §4 Fix

`closeLegFeeQuote(result, qty)` returns the close fill's fee (observed, or
estimated) in the unit the close PnL is booked in;
`profitAfterPositionClosed` and `processLiquidationOrder` book
`grossPnl − closeLegFeeQuote(...)`.

Past totals are restated by the parent repo's one-off
`scripts/restateFuturesGridCloseFees.ts` (dry run by default): it subtracts
the observed fee of each closed bot's `typeOrder: 'stop'` orders that
executed before the fix went live. It does not restore the old formula's
second-order term (maker × close PnL): the maker rate in force at the time is
not recorded.

## Invariants

None added. After the close `resetPosition()` zeroes the position price, so
the data cannot tell a close booked as `gross × (1 − maker)` from one booked
as `gross − fee`. An invariant here would have to re-derive the entry price
from the fill history, which is not a cheap indexed query.

## §5 Tests

`src/bot/gridFuturesCloseFeeProfit.spec.ts` — §1.1 totals, USD and the
statistics input, a part close and a liquidation, with and without observed
fees; §1.3 an observed fee wins over the stored rate, and a missing one falls
back to the estimate. The spec 273/274 part-close fixtures and the spec 236
liquidation fixture now expect the notional fee.
