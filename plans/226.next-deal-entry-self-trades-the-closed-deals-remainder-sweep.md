# Plan 226 — The next deal's entry self-trades the closed deal's remainder sweep

Spec: `specs/226.next-deal-entry-self-trades-the-closed-deals-remainder-sweep.md`.

## Build order

1. `src/bot/dcaHelper.ts` (core only, no parent change beyond the pointer bump):
   - `remainderSweepOrder`: the sweep's `Grid` + `OrderAdditionalParams`, moved
     unchanged out of `sellRemainder` so the re-send (§1.1.3) sends the same
     order.
   - `trackRemainderSweep`: at the end of `sellRemainder`'s booking, for a
     close-path sweep (`updateBalances && sellNotByOrder`, spot LONG,
     quote-profit, not combo). It records the cumulative quantity and quote the
     immediate answer booked, plus the booking's deal-write promise.
   - `bookRemainderSweepReport`: called first by `processFilledOrder`,
     `processPartiallyFilledOrder` and `processCanceledOrder` (dealTP). It books
     the unbooked part of the cumulative fill with `$inc` after the booking's
     `$set` has landed, and does the same for the bot's profit.
   - `settleRemainderSweeps`: in `processDealCloseHoldingLock`, at the head of
     the `reopen` branch, before `openNewDeal`/`setRangeOrError`. It polls the
     tracked statuses every 100 ms for up to `remainderSweepWaitMs` (5 s).
   - `resendRemainderSweep`: one re-send of the unsold quantity, held to the
     same minimums as `sellRemainder`, tracked as unbooked.
2. Spec test `src/bot/remainderSweepSelfTrade.spec.ts`.
3. `CHANGELOG.md` + `package.json` patch bump.

## Decisions the spec left open

- **Wait by polling instead of a waiter callback.** Order reports already
  update the tracked status, and a poll needs no hook in `MainBot`'s queue.
  100 ms is well under the stream's own report cadence (≈250 ms in §2).
- **`$inc`, not read-modify-`$set`.** Nothing else writes the closed deal's
  ledger after the close, and `$inc` cannot clobber a concurrent write. Every
  `$inc` waits for the close booking's `$set` first (`ready`).
- **Fee** = sold quote × taker, the same fee `sellRemainder` books for the
  sweep (`srQty * srPrice * taker`).
- **Combo excluded.** `comboHelper` overrides `processFilledOrder` and
  `processCanceledOrder` without calling up, so a tracked combo sweep would
  never be reported and every combo close would wait the full bound.

## JIT extraction tier

Tier 1 (extract in place). `remainderSweepOrder` is pulled out of
`sellRemainder` because a second caller now needs exactly that order. The
booking arithmetic in `sellRemainder` is left as it is: it serves five call
shapes, and only one of them is in scope.

## Blast Radius

1. **Call sites.** `sellRemainder` has three callers in `dcaHelper.ts` and three
   in `comboHelper.ts`. Tracking happens only for `sellNotByOrder &&
   updateBalances` on a spot LONG DCA (non-combo) deal. In `dcaHelper` that is
   the two `closeDeal` calls (the send and its booking). The other callers
   (late entry fill, cancelled deal, `checkBaseOrder`) pass
   `sellNotByOrder = false`.
   `processDealCloseHoldingLock` is shared by DCA, combo and hedge bots. With
   nothing tracked, `settleRemainderSweeps` returns at once (spec §4).
2. **`core/`.** The file lives in the `main-app-sh` submodule, which is deployed
   with every bot worker. The parent's pointer needs a bump.
3. **Contracts.** No GraphQL, REST, socket or queue shape changes. The Mongo
   writes touch only existing `dcadeals` fields (`currentBalances`, `profit.*`,
   `commission`, `lastPrice`, `updateTime`) and the bot's existing `profit`.
   No new field is added.
4. **Other tests on this path:** `limitOnlyCloseRemainder`,
   `lateEntryFillAfterClose`, `reduceOnlyCloseMinimum`, `zeroSizeOrderRefusal`,
   `closeSweepResume`, `closeAfterSweptTpFill`, `closeAfterXStopDeadlock`, and
   the full `npm test` suite.
