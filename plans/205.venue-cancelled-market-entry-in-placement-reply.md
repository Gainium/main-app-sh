## Decisions the spec left open

- Gate on `sentType === MARKET` in DCA, not on the answer's status: the
  predicate already filters status/fill/deal/own-cancel/live entries, and a
  LIMIT entry keeps its spec-203 path untouched (§4.4).
- Call before the timer block. On the edge where a LIMIT-start bot's forced
  market entry is answered CANCELED, the timers the block then arms make the
  deal check defer, and the reposition tick's spec-203 §4.4 net takes over —
  exactly today's handling, so no retry changes.
- JIT tier 0: the decision (`isUnattributedUnfilledBaseEntryCancel`) is
  already pure and tested; this only adds a caller.
