## Blast Radius
- `clearNotEnoughBalanceForSymbol` is new. Its only caller is `restartDeal`.
- The (symbol, side) key is shared by every deal of the bot on that symbol. A
  restart therefore also lets those deals' next orders reach the venue once.
  The venue's answer then re-arms the guard (§1.1.2).
- The retry-branch change only affects attempts the cooldown already refused
  locally. Real venue refusals retry as before. The back-off window itself is
  unchanged (§1.1.3).
- Files are under `core/` (main-app-sh), so the parent pointer needs a bump.
- No GraphQL, socket or DB shape change. `notEnoughBalance` keeps its fields.
- Must stay green: `restartDeal.spec.ts` (fixture gains a symbol),
  `strandedLocalRefusal.spec.ts`, `qtyStepGuard.spec.ts`, and the full core
  suite.
