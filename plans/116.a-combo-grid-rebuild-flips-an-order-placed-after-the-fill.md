# Plan 116 — A combo grid rebuild flips an order placed after the fill

Spec: `specs/116.a-combo-grid-rebuild-flips-an-order-placed-after-the-fill.md`

## Build order

1. `src/bot/combo/minigridWhipsaw.harness.spec.ts` — drives the real
   `updateMinigrid` → `isLastMinigridOrder` → `generateGridsOnPrice` →
   `findDiffCombo` over the production grid (built by `generateBasicGrids`)
   and replays the production burst. It is red on the unfixed tree for the
   reason the spec states: it sends SELL 0.08252 + SELL 0.08273 for one
   unit of base (the second is the refused order), and in the mirror case it
   leaves base with no SELL.
2. `src/bot/combo/reanchorAfterFill.ts` (pure): `reanchorLevelAfterFill` and
   `levelOf`, with unit tests in `reanchorAfterFill.spec.ts`.
3. `comboHelper.updateMinigrid`: the generator call becomes a local
   `rebuildAt(lastPrice)` closure (same arguments). On the rebuild path, ask
   `reanchorLevelAfterFill`. If it names a level: rebuild at that level's
   price, drop the filled order's level from `prev`, record the anchor in
   `lastMinigridOrder` and `schema.lastPrice`, and log one line.

## Contract decisions the spec left open

- Anchor side: the fill's own side, at the level's price on that side.
  `generateGridsOnPrice` then finds the level exactly, whatever the rounding
  of the fill's `origPrice`.
- The close decision (`sells === 0` / `buys === 0`) still runs on the final
  ladder. The re-anchor never moves the empty level to either end, so it can
  never be what closes a minigrid.
- JIT extraction tier: only the pure rule is extracted (tier "pure helper
  beside the call site", like `combo/tpSolve.ts`). `updateMinigrid` gets a
  local closure rather than a new method, so no decorator neighbourhood is
  touched.

## Blast Radius

- Other call sites: `generateGridsOnPrice` is unchanged, and its other
  callers (minigrid create/restore, grid bots) are untouched.
  `isLastMinigridOrder` is unchanged. Only `updateMinigrid` records a
  different anchor, and only when the re-anchor fires.
- `core/` is `main-app-sh`: shared by every combo and hedge-combo bot, on
  cloud and self-hosted. The change is confined to combo deal-grid ladders.
- Contracts: no GraphQL, REST, socket, Redis or Mongo shape changes.
  `combominigrids.lastPrice` keeps its meaning (the price the ladder was last
  rebuilt at). After a re-anchor it is a level price rather than the fill's
  price.
- Money path: the change moves or places exchange orders (a grid order may be
  moved one level, a counter sell placed at another level), so it is in the
  "moves money / executes orders" class and needs an operator decision.
- Other tests on this path: `comboGridCancelLedger`, `comboReloadLadderDuplicate`,
  `lateFillReportUnfills.harness`, `grid*` specs and the full core suite
  (1793) stay green.
