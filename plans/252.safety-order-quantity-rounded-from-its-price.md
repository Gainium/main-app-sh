## Contract decisions
- F = max(100, 10 × gridLevel). A legitimate raise is bounded by about gridLevel × budget. The
  observed corruption is about 23,000×.
- Repair keeps the stored price and takes qty + minigridBudget from the fresh level.

## Blast Radius
- `core/` (main-app-sh): every Combo and DCA bot worker. The DCA path changes only §2.1 (a
  correctness fix) and gets an identity hook. No GraphQL, socket, Redis-key or Mongo schema
  change.
- Normal Combo sizing is unchanged: `createInitialDealOrders` is not edited. The guard only
  removes placements more than 100× (or 10 × gridLevel) away from their own budget.
- Existing corrupted DCA snapshots are not repaired (DCA grids carry no budget). Tracked
  separately.
