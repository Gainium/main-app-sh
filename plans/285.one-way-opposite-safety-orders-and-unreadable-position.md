## Decisions the spec left open

- The retry re-places the held levels the ladder still wants, by spec 247's
  `venueCanceledSafetyLevel`, and does not rebuild the ladder. A level the
  ladder dropped while it was held is not placed.
- The cache stores holds only. An allow is asked again on every pass, so the
  first pass after the opposite deal closes places at once. A hold is
  re-checked after at most 60 s.

## Blast Radius

1. Call sites: `placeOrdersHoldingDealLock` serves every DCA and combo
   placement pass. The gate runs only when the pass carries a position-adding
   `dealRegular` order on a one-way futures DCA/combo bot. Take-profits, grid
   levels, closes and cancels go through unchanged. `netPositionHeldAgainst`
   keeps its one caller (`closeDealById`) and its answers.
2. `core/` (`main-app-sh`): shared, public. Runs in the DCA and combo bot
   workers only. The parent repo needs a pointer bump (done by `psh`).
3. Contracts: no GraphQL, REST, socket, Redis or Mongo schema change. Two
   new reads per gated pass, no writes besides bot warning events.
4. Load: one-way futures DCA/combo bots with no opposite deal and no
   opposite-direction bot cost two indexed `findOne`-style reads per pass with
   a safety order, and no venue read. Bots with an opposite bot add one venue
   position read per pass, or per 60 s while held.
5. Trading behaviour: on one-way futures only, safety orders against an open
   opposite deal or opposite net position are no longer sent. This includes
   pre-existing pairs (spec §4.5). Resting orders are not cancelled.
6. Tests that must stay green: `opposingNetPosition.harness.spec.ts` (262),
   the placement harnesses under `src/bot/dca/` (`spentLevelIdPlacement`,
   `lateLadderAfterClose`, `releasedDealPlacement`, `writtenOffLevelId`,
   `refusedCloseRestore`, `canceledTpRestore`, `restingTpPositionGone`) and
   `comboGridShortfallHold.spec.ts`.
