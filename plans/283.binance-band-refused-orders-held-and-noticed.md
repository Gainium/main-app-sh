# Plan 283 — hold Binance band refusals, take profits included

## Build order (core only; no connector change)

1. `outOfBandRetry.ts`: `bandForUnnamedRefusal` (filter → default 20 %, capped
   by the refused distance); `VenuePriceBand` per-side key; `OutOfBandNotice`
   (Redis once-latch, fail-open).
2. `main.ts`: write-off also notes a `PERCENT_PRICE` refusal (reusing
   `isPriceBandRefusal` from `priceBandReprice.ts`, `refusal.subType` kept);
   `noteOutOfBandRefusal` derives the band for it; `holdsOutOfBand` =
   `replacesVenueCancel` ∪ `holdsTakeProfitOutOfBand`; the known-band
   short-circuit reads the side's band; `holdOutOfBand` raises the notice on a
   first hold; an accepted take profit drops held take profits of its deal.
   The `handleErrors` `PERCENT_PRICE` return stays: under `Order params`
   (errorsBot) it would put the bot in error per retry; the notice is raised
   at the hold instead.
3. `dcaHelper.ts`: opt deal take profits in; release goes through
   `replaceHeldTakeProfit` → `rearmMissingTp`; `noteTpCoverageAfterPlacement`
   skips held take profits; give-up recorded in RetryBackoff `mtg` (day memory).

Decisions the spec left open: the default band (20 %, Binance's tightest
ahead-of-market multipliers on spot); the band is capped by the refused
distance so a release is never at a refused distance (each further refusal
moves the release closer — no per-tick re-send loop). The connector's spot
exchange info has no `PERCENT_PRICE_BY_SIDE`; exposing it is a separate change.

JIT tier: 0/1 — new pure helper in `outOfBandRetry.ts`; legacy methods get
minimal local edits (parked work also edits `main.ts`/`dcaHelper.ts`).

## Blast Radius

1. Call sites: `noteOutOfBandRefusal` (write-off only); the short-circuit; the
   grid bot (`helper.ts`) also gets Binance grid levels held (it opts levels in
   via `replacesVenueCancel`), no take profits. `replaceVenueCanceled` (DCA)
   gains a `dealTP` branch reachable only from the out-of-band flush:
   venue-cancel and market-closed paths gate on `replacesVenueCancel`, which
   still excludes take profits.
2. All under `core/` (main-app-sh): ships with the main-app parent.
3. No GraphQL/socket/schema change: `deal.heldOrders` (spec 264) already
   carries `typeOrder`; a held take profit now appears there.
4. Green: `outOfBandRetry.harness.spec.ts`, `outOfBandHold.spec.ts`,
   `binancePercentPriceReprice.spec.ts`, `dca/mergeAndMissingTp.spec.ts`.
