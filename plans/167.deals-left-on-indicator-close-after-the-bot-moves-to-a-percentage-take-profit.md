# Plan 167 — Deals left on indicator close after the bot moves to a percentage take profit

## Build order
1. `getDealCloseIndicatorSymbols()` (new private method on the DCA helper): per section,
   the symbols of open deals whose own aggregated settings close by indicator.
2. `isIndicator` ORs those into its two close-deal clauses (§4.1).
3. `openIndicators` computes them once and passes them to `connectSettingsIndicator`,
   whose close-deal skip gains `&& !dealCloseSymbols?.<section>.has(symbol)` (§4.2).
   The option is optional, so `syncDcaIndicatorLevels` and the golden harness are unchanged.
4. `checkMinTp` computes the deal's settings first and only short-circuits on the
   bot-level `allowedMethods` flag when the deal itself does not close by signal; its two
   P&L-indicator lookups use the deal's close condition (§4.3).

JIT tier: none — targeted edits inside existing methods; no extraction.

## Blast radius
- Call sites: `isIndicator` ← `startIndicatorInit`; `connectSettingsIndicator` ←
  `openIndicators`, `syncDcaIndicatorLevels` (startDca only, option not passed);
  `checkMinTp` ← `closeAllDeals` only. Combo bots inherit the DCA helper and get the same
  per-deal behaviour.
- File is under `core/` (main-app-sh): shared, deployed platform-wide; pointer bump needed.
- No GraphQL/REST/socket/queue/Mongo shape touched.
- Behaviour for bots whose deals share the bot's close condition is unchanged: the new
  symbol sets are empty, or coincide with the bot-level clause.
- Tests that must stay green: `indicatorConditions.characterization.spec.ts` (golden),
  whole `src/**/*.spec.ts` suite.
