## Decisions the spec left open

- Reset status in place rather than re-send a copy: callers hold the order
  reference and the branch already renames it in place; a copy would change
  what the caller sees.
- Write-ahead in the branch rather than at `count === 1` generally: the other
  `count > 0` re-entries (tick size, qty decimals, position side, balance retry)
  re-send the SAME id, whose row already exists.
