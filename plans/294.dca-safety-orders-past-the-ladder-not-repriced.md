## Blast radius

- **Who:** DCA and combo deals' safety orders (`dealRegular` with `dealId`) on any
  venue whose price-band refusal reaches the spec 248 re-price (Binance
  `PERCENT_PRICE*`, Bitget/OKX "cannot exceed …%"), and the re-priced order's
  NOTIONAL refusal. Grid bots, take profits, add/reduce funds and MARKET orders
  are untouched (`dealLadder` undefined / typeOrder gate).
- **Money:** a held level places nothing while the market is beyond the deal's
  coverage; before, the same level was refused every cycle and placed nothing
  either, so no fill is lost. Inside the ladder, spec 248 behaviour is unchanged
  (test "not changing"). Release is automatic on the next send once the market is
  back (beyondLadder) or no longer through the level (notional).
- **Wrong-ladder risk:** an unknown start or no safety levels → `undefined` →
  today's behaviour. The tolerance floor only narrows "inside" for ladders deeper
  than half their start; it is used by this check alone, not by breakpoints.
- **State:** in-process map only (lost on worker restart, rebuilt on the next
  refusal: one extra venue call per level per restart); notice dedupe in Redis
  (30 d TTL, fail-open = tell again).
- **Errors:** held reasons are registered strings; the existing
  `isHeldOutOfBandReason` silencing covers them, and the existing invariant
  `botmessages.held-order-reported-as-error` would catch a caller reporting one.
- **Parent repo:** `main-app/src/bot/main.ts` overrides `sendOrderToExchange`
  only to delegate to `super`; no parent change. Parent `tsc` clean apart from a
  pre-existing missing `@noble/hashes` module in an untouched file.
