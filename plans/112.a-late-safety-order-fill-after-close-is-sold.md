  change. The `sellRemainder` DB field keeps its meaning and is still written.
- Double-sale risk: `updateDeal` refuses a second pass of one `clientOrderId` (`dealUpdateOrders`, never
  cleared), covered by §3.1.
- Other tests on this path: `zeroSizeOrderRefusal.spec.ts` §4.1, `remainderDoubleCount.spec.ts` (full suite green).
