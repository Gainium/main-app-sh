## Contract decisions the spec left open

- **Where the reconciliation lives.** In the pure module, not in the helper.
  It is a decision about two reports of the same number and has no I/O; it
  joins the three predicates already there and is tested without the 25k-line
  helper. The helper keeps only the snapshot and the spread.
- **The verdict's shape.** `{ executedQty, price, updateTime }` rather than a
  whole row: the caller already holds the row, `Order` stays out of the pure
  module, and the three fields are exactly the ones the cancel response is
  allowed to be wrong about (`clientOrderId`, `origQty` and `origPrice` are
  already excluded from `cancelOrderOnExchange`'s copy loop, and
  `cummulativeQuoteQty` is absent from the Kraken answer so the loop never
  touches it).
- **No new helper.** `terminalEntryHoldsAFill` is reused for BOTH sides of the
  decision — the answer and the row we held — rather than a second "does this
  hold a fill" test being written. The second call asserts `'CANCELED'` because
  `observed`'s own status is the PRE-cancel one; what is being sized and dated
  is the ended order, not the row's former status.
- **The no-answer case is deliberately NOT covered.** `settled` falsy returns
  `null` and keeps the warn. A cancel that got no answer may have left the
  remainder resting on the book, and opening the deal on a fraction of an entry
  that is still filling double-books it. That shape needs a retry or a re-read
  of the order and is separate work.

## Blast Radius

1. **Other call sites.**
   - `settlePartialBaseEntry` — three callers, all in `dcaHelper`:
     `checkBaseOrder`'s reposition branch, `checkBaseOrder`'s enter-market
     branch, and `processCanceledOrder`. All three reach the same method; none
     passes new arguments. Their gate (`shouldSettlePartialBaseEntry`) is
     untouched.
   - `promoteEndedBaseEntry` — one caller, `settlePartialBaseEntry`. The
     `force` change therefore cannot reach any other path.
   - `bookSettledBaseEntry` is new; its body moved out of the branch above it.
   - `terminalEntryHoldsAFill` changes visibility only.
   - `cancelOrderOnExchange` (`main.ts`) is NOT touched. Its copy loop and its
     promotion guard behave exactly as before, for every venue and every
     caller — grid teardown, combo, the re-size opt-out of spec 026. The fix
     is entirely on the DCA base-entry side, where the engine knows what the
     row held before it asked.
2. **`core/` submodule.** Both files are under `core/` (`main-app-sh`) —
   shared, public, and deployed platform-wide. Nothing here is exported past
   the DCA bot engine: `settledBaseEntryFill` is a new export of a module only
   `dcaHelper` imports, and `terminalEntryHoldsAFill` becomes exported without
   changing. The parent's submodule pointer is bumped at ship time.
3. **Documented contracts.** None. No GraphQL/REST shape, no socket event, no
   RabbitMQ queue, no `BaseReturn`, no Mongo schema field. The only persisted
   change is that a `dealStart` row the engine has decided is `FILLED` now
   actually reaches the `orders` collection as `FILLED` — which is the value
   spec `048` already intended to write and the shape every reader of a deal's
   base order already expects.
4. **Tests that must stay green.** `partialBaseEntry.harness.spec.ts` (038),
   `venueCanceledBaseEntry.harness.spec.ts` (048),
   `baseEntryTopUp.harness.spec.ts` (057 + 058),
   `unbuiltBaseEntry.harness.spec.ts` (039), `resizeCancelPromotion.spec.ts`
   (026 — the other reader of the cancel promotion guard).

## JIT extraction tier

**Tier 1.** The decision on the causal path was inline inside a method that
also cancels at the venue, promotes, persists, buys a remainder and opens a
deal. It moves into the pure module that already owns this family of decisions
and gets its own tests; `settlePartialBaseEntry` keeps the I/O. Not Tier 2/3 —
no port is introduced, because the surrounding method's other dependencies are
out of this change's radius.
