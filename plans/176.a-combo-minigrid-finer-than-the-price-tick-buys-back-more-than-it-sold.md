# 176 — plan

## Solution

1. **`combo/subTickLadder.ts` (pure).** `hasCollapsedLevels(levels)` — two
   levels share a rounded buy price. `maxDistinctLevels(low, top, precision)`
   — `ceil(width / tick)`, the upper bound on levels whose rounded prices can
   all differ; it lets the search start near the answer instead of walking
   down one level at a time from a large `gridLevel`.
2. **`comboHelper.createMinigrid`.** Build the requested ladder as before. Only
   when it has collapsed levels, step the level count down (capped by
   `maxDistinctLevels`) and rebuild with the same `generateBasicGrids` until
   the prices are distinct or one level is left; log the reduction. The
   reduced count then feeds the budget split, `generateGridsOnPrice` and the
   persisted `settings.levels`, which `prepareMinigrids` and `updateMinigrid`
   read on every later rebuild.

The brief proposed `floor(width / tick)`. That count reduces some ladders whose
prices are already distinct (4 levels over 0.0265025–0.0269 round onto 5
distinct prices; the floor is 3), which §4.1 forbids. Testing the generated
ladder itself keeps every already-distinct ladder untouched and keeps the most
levels that stay apart.

JIT tier: Tier 2 for the new decision (pure helpers); the call site stays where
it is.

## Blast Radius

1. **Call sites.** `createMinigrid` is called from the base-order and
   safety-order fill paths only. `generateBasicGrids` itself is unchanged and
   still shared with the grid bot, `prepareMinigrids`, `getBaseOrder` and
   `createInitialDealOrders`.
2. **`core/` submodule.** Yes — `main-app-sh`, deployed with main-app's bot
   workers. No other service imports `comboHelper`.
3. **Contracts.** No GraphQL, socket, queue or schema shape changes.
   `comboMinigrids.settings.levels` keeps its meaning (the ladder's level
   count) and simply holds the reduced count for new minigrids.
4. **Tests that must stay green.** Every spec touching `comboHelper`,
   `createMinigrid` or `updateMinigrid` (27 files) and the full core suite.
