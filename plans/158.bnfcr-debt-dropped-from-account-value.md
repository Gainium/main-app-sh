# 158 — plan

Spec: `specs/158.bnfcr-debt-dropped-from-account-value.md`.

## Solution

1. New `src/utils/pegRates.ts` exporting `pegRateEntries` — one rate-table
   entry, `{ pair: 'BNFCRUSD', price: 1, exchange: 'all' }`.
2. `src/utils/user.ts`: append `...pegRateEntries` to the rate table in
   `userSnapshots` and in `priceBalancesUsd`, next to the USDT→USD and fiat
   entries those two functions already append. `findUSDRate` then prices BNFCR
   through its existing `asset/USD` lookup; no lookup logic changes.

## Decisions the spec left open

- Where the price lives. `findUSDRate` itself is also called by the bot engine
  (monitor, sizing, budget), so a special case there would change bot
  behaviour (§1.4). A rate-table entry reaches only the two snapshot tables.
- A positive BNFCR row (profit credits) is valued at 1 USD too — it is part of
  Binance's Wallet Balance just the same.

## Blast Radius

- Affects only accounts holding a BNFCR row (Binance USDⓈ-M, Multi-Assets
  mode). Their snapshot `totalUsd`, `exchangesTotal`, per-exchange snapshot and
  every reader of those (account `balance` in GraphQL `getExchanges`, Portfolio
  history, portfolio alerts, AI portfolio tools, metrics) move by the BNFCR
  balance from the next snapshot on. Past snapshots are not rewritten.
- `getBalances(includeUsdValues)` lists only positive rows; a positive BNFCR
  row now carries a USD value instead of none.
- No GraphQL/REST shape, socket event, or schema changes. Bot engine untouched.
