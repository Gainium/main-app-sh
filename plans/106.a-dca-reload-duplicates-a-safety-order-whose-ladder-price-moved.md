# Plan 106 — DCA reload keeps the resting safety order of a moved level

## Solution

In DCA `getDiffForCheckOrders`, after `findDiff`, take per side the unmatched
rebuilt `dealRegular` levels (`diff.new`) and the unmatched resting
`dealRegular` orders (`diff.cancel`). When the two counts are equal, pair them
by ladder rank and drop those levels from `new`: the resting order is that
level's live order. When the counts differ, leave `new` as it is (§1.1.2).

`diff.cancel` is untouched — `checkOrders` ignores it today and still does.
Combo overrides `getDiffForCheckOrders` (spec 105), so this changes DCA only.

JIT tier: none. The change stays inside the one method that owns the diff.

## Blast Radius

1. `getDiffForCheckOrders` has one caller, the restart branch of
   `checkOrders`. Combo overrides the method.
2. `core/` (main-app-sh): shared by every main-app service; the DCA bot
   workers pick it up. No type, schema, GraphQL, socket or queue shape changes.
3. A resting order kept at a price a tick off the rebuilt ladder later fills at
   its own price. `processFilledOrder` counts levels by filled orders, not by
   price, so the ladder advances as it does for any displaced fill.
4. Other tests on the path: the restart-branch specs under `src/bot/`.
