`open | error | range | monitoring`, and keep `shouldProceed()`. JIT tier 0: a
one-line guard change, no extraction needed.

   schema, GraphQL or socket contract changes.

   cancelled through `closeDealById` (cancel type, `auto` trigger), as on an
   `open` bot. Closed/archived bots are unchanged.

   `dca/venueCanceledBaseEntry`, `comboGridCancelLedger` harnesses.
