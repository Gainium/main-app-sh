# 271 — plan

## Build order

1. `src/bot/marketClosedRetry.ts`: give-up constant, `balanceRefusalExpired`,
   notice texts (pure).
2. `src/bot/main.ts`:
   - Factor the closed-market hold into `holdRefusedForRetry(order, why, hold)`
     and `noticeOncePerSpell`. `noteMarketClosedRefusal` keeps its behaviour.
     The attempts map gains `firstAt`.
   - New `noteBalanceRefusal` and the default `giveUpBalanceRefusedLevel`.
   - In the write-off branch of `sendOrderToExchange`, after the row is
     retired, call `noteBalanceRefusal` for a balance refusal from the venue,
     the local cooldown or the held-funds guard.
   - `flushMarketClosed` also runs for a bot in `error`.
3. `src/bot/dcaHelper.ts`: override `giveUpBalanceRefusedLevel`, which records
   the level on `deal.missedLevels` (DCA, non-indicator) or only tells the user.
4. main-app `specs/invariants/deals.invariants.json`: one observe invariant.

No second retry mechanism: the queue, timer (`scheduleMarketClosedRetry`),
back-off (`marketClosedRetryDelay`), level key and placement hook
(`replaceVenueCanceled`) are the spec 248 / 247 ones.

## JIT tier

Tier 1. The new decisions (expiry, notices) are pure helpers in
`marketClosedRetry.ts`. The orchestration stays in `MainBot` next to the
mechanism it reuses.

## Blast radius

1. Call sites. `noteMarketClosedRefusal` is unchanged for its callers (same
   log, notice and back-off). `flushMarketClosed` now also runs in `error`.
   For a closed market that is a fix too: the same soft error could hold
   its retry. `replaceVenueCanceled` is unchanged.
2. `core/`: yes (main-app-sh), shared with every main-app deployment. No type,
   GraphQL, socket or queue shape changes. `deal.missedLevels` is an existing
   field (spec 233), now also written on give-up.
3. Contracts: none touched. The notices reuse the `Not enough balance` /
   `Market closed` subTypes.
4. Tests that must stay green: `dca/refusedSafetyOrderRetry.harness.spec.ts`
   (closed-market queue), `dca/venueCanceledSafetyOrder.harness.spec.ts`,
   `outOfBandRetry.harness.spec.ts`, and the full core suite.

Money: the change places orders (limit at the level's price), so it is
`stop_class: money`.
