# Plan 212 — a ledger rebuild counts partial take profits the close subtracts again

Spec: `specs/212.ledger-rebuild-counts-partial-take-profits-the-close-subtracts-again.md`.

## Solution
Tier 0 on the touched legacy code: one filter in `updateDealBalances`
(`src/bot/dcaHelper.ts`). On a non-Combo deal that is not closed, a `dealTP`
row whose `clientOrderId` is in `deal.tpHistory` is left out of the rebuilt
ledger. `closeDeal` is not touched: its `qty = orderQty + Σ tpHistory`, the
remainder sweep, the close booking and the lost-ledger replay (`closedTp`,
which already leaves these rows out) are now all measured against one count.
`closeRemainderByLimit`'s `known` netting nets resting take-profits against
`tpHistory`, never against the ledger, so it is unaffected.

## Blast Radius
1. Callers of `updateDealBalances`: add-funds / base-remainder fill, restart
   (non-service), settings update, position merge, Combo TP sizing, add/reduce
   funds settle. Only non-Combo, non-closed deals with a `tpHistory` change,
   and they now read the ledger the fill path keeps. Consumers that read the
   open ledger already assume it excludes partial take profits (spec 128
   `heldEntry = currentBalances.base − add`, where `add` is Σ tpHistory).
2. Under `core/` (main-app-sh): pointer bump needed in main-app.
3. Contracts: none — no schema, GraphQL, socket or queue change. Booked
   `currentBalances`/`profit.pureBase`/`pureQuote` of affected closes change
   (that is the fix); deals without `tpHistory` are byte-identical.
4. Tests: `limitOnlyCloseRemainder.spec.ts`, `evidenceFreeFilledPromotion`,
   `unknownOrderVenueFillSize` and every spec touching `tpHistory` /
   `closeDeal` / `updateDealBalances`.
