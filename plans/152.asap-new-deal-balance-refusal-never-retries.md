# Plan 152 — bounded live re-check after an ASAP balance refusal

## Solution

- `dcaHelper.ts`: export `NEW_DEAL_BALANCE_RETRY_MS = [10 s, 30 s, 60 s, 2 min,
  5 min]`, about 9 minutes in total, and add a per-pair attempt map
  `newDealBalanceRetries`. New method `retryNewDealAfterBalanceRefusal(symbol,
  startCondition, trigger)`. For an ASAP bot and a non-manual trigger it
  arms `openNewDealTimer[symbol]` for the next delay and records the attempt.
  When the delays are used up, or the bot is not ASAP, it forgets the pair.
  It reuses the existing per-pair `openNewDealTimer`, which every fresh attempt
  clears and stop/close teardown already empties.
- `checkBalanceGate`: skip the stored-first read while the pair has a pending
  re-check (§4.2).
- Both refusal branches (`dcaHelper.openNewDeal`, not terminal;
  `comboHelper.openNewDeal`) call the method after `resetPending`. Both pass
  branches drop the pair from the map next to the latch clear (§4.3).
- The latch is not touched (§4.4).

JIT tier 0: the change is a new method plus two one-line calls on the existing
branches. No extraction. The refusal branch is not otherwise touched.

Contract decisions left open by the spec:
- Delays: settlement on the venue takes seconds. The schedule only has to
  outlast the seconds-long cancel/settle burst after a close with margin, and
  stay bounded for a bot that genuinely lacks the funds: at most 5 re-checks,
  10 live reads, per refusal.
- ASAP only. Indicator, webhook and timer bots are re-triggered by their own
  signal. Re-attempting an old signal later would open a deal the signal no
  longer supports.

## Blast Radius

1. Call sites: `checkBalanceGate` is called from the DCA open path, the DCA
   single-position entry path and the Combo open path. The live-read override
   applies only while a re-check is pending for that pair, which only ASAP
   refusals create.
2. `core/` (main-app-sh, public): engine-internal. No GraphQL, REST, socket,
   queue or Mongo shape changes.
3. Exchange load: bounded live reads per refusal on ASAP bots, which previously
   made no further reads at all. Indicator bots, the population the stored-first
   gate (1.83.0) was built for, are unchanged.
4. Tests that must stay green: `dca/storedBalanceGate.harness.spec.ts`,
   `newDealApproval.spec.ts`, `dca/reduceToAvailableOpenDeal.harness.spec.ts`,
   `dca/singlePosition.harness.spec.ts`, and the full `npm test`.
