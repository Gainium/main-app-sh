## Blast radius

- `closeDealById` is shared by DCA and combo. The new path is gated on
  `this.combo`, so DCA closes take the old line byte for byte (§4.4 test).
- Combo closes with no resting closing-side row after the sweep (the
  ordinary case) do not wait and are not capped (§4.4 test).
- Other `prepareTpOrder` callers (split close, re-arm, fee fallback) are
  unchanged. Only the close-after-sweep call site moves.
- `core/` is platform-wide. `psh` does the pointer bump. No GraphQL, socket,
  schema or queue change.
- Worst case added latency: 5 s on a close whose closing exit never settles.
  The trigger and its price are unchanged.
