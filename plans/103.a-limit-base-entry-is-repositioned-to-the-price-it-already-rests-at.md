# Plan 103 — A LIMIT base entry is repositioned to the price it already rests at

## Solution

- **§4.1/§4.2:** `checkBaseOrder`'s order-id branch asks the new
  `keepRestingBaseEntry` before its cancel. On the live tick only (timer
  state exists for the deal), and only for a `NEW` LIMIT row of a
  LIMIT-entry bot whose deal is in `start`, it compares
  `round(getLatestPrice, priceAssetPrecision)` with the resting price. That
  is the price `getBaseOrder` would derive for a LIMIT entry. If the two are
  equal, it re-arms the tick and returns. The pure rule is
  `dca/baseEntryReposition.ts` `repositionKeepsRestingBaseEntry`.
- The tick-arming block in `placeBaseOrder` moves, with its behaviour
  unchanged, into `armBaseRepositionTick`, and its window rule into the pure
  `repositionTickDue`. The keep path re-arms under exactly the rule
  `placeBaseOrder` uses.
- **§4.3:** in the order-id branch, `hasPendingCheck` becomes "an
  enter-market timer is armed" (`dealTimersMap.get(id)?.enterMarketTimer`).
  The restore path (no entry) and a switch-on bot (timer armed) behave as
  before.
- **§4.4:** `shouldTopUpSettledBaseEntry` takes an optional
  `marketEntryAllowed`. `topUpSettledBaseEntry` passes `false` for a
  LIMIT-entry bot with `enterMarketTimeout === 0`.

JIT tier 2: the pure decisions are extracted, and the I/O stays in
`dcaHelper`.

## Decisions the spec left open

- **Settle on the first tick after a partial fill, whatever the price.** The
  alternative was to keep a partial resting while its price is unchanged. On
  an illiquid pair that leaves the position unmanaged for hours, which is the
  complaint.
- **The restore path keeps cancel + re-place.** It holds no timers, and
  `placeBaseOrder` is what arms them, including a switch-on bot's
  enter-market timer.
- **Close-by-limit (`checkTPOrder`) is not touched** (spec 100 §1.3.1).

## Blast Radius

1. Callers: `checkBaseOrder` is reached from `placeBaseOrder`'s timers, from
   restore (`restoreWork`) and from the enter-market timer.
   `topUpSettledBaseEntry` is reached from `bookSettledBaseEntry`, which
   serves every settle path. For a MARKET-entry or switch-on bot, top-up is
   unchanged.
2. `core/`: shared with self-hosted. No types, schema, GraphQL, socket or
   queue contract changes.
3. Behaviour change for switch-off LIMIT bots: fewer cancel/place calls,
   partial fills are settled live, and there is no market top-up. That last
   point also covers a restore-path or venue-cancel settle inside the
   top-up window. Before this change such a settle could buy the remainder
   at market, contrary to spec 100.
4. Tests that must stay green: `partialBaseEntry`, `baseEntryTopUp`,
   `limitTimeouts`, `limitOnlyEntry*`, `venueCanceledBaseEntry`, and the
   full core suite.
