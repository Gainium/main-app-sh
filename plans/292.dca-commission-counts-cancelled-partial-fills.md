# Plan 292 — A DCA close charges the fee of a part-filled, cancelled order

## Build order
1. `src/bot/dcaHelper.ts#getCommDeal`: optional `orders` parameter; default unchanged
   (`FILLED` rows of the deal).
2. `src/bot/dcaHelper.ts#closeDeal`: pass `dealOrders` (the `feePaid` set) for non-combo
   deals; combo passes nothing.
3. Test: `src/bot/closeCommissionCountsCancelledPartialFill.spec.ts` drives the real
   `closeDeal` over a cancelled partial take-profit with a real fee.
4. main-app parent: observe invariant in `specs/invariants/deals.invariants.json`.

## Blast radius
- `getCommDeal` callers: only the take-profit branch of `closeDeal` changes, and only for
  non-combo deals. Combo take-profit solving (`comboHelper` `fullFee`, `combo/tpSolve`), the
  `fullFee` writes, close without a take-profit, external close, cancel with realised profit
  and the multi-TP partial booking all keep the default.
- Orders: none placed, cancelled or resized differently (A1). No venue calls added.
- Money: a DCA deal closed by take-profit after a part-filled cancelled order books that
  order's fee into `commission` and takes it off `profit.total`, `profit.totalUsd` and the
  bot's accumulated profit. Profit moves down by the fee the venue really charged. A
  "stop after X profit" condition can trigger a fee's worth later, which is correct.
- `br`/`rebalance`/`funding` `FILLED` rows' fees leave a DCA close's commission (§4).
- No schema, GraphQL, socket or Redis change. Already closed deals are not restated.
- Type-check: parent `tsc` (core included, `tests/` included) is clean.
