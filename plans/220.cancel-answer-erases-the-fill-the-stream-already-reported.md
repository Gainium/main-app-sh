# Plan 220 — keep the largest known fill when a cancel answer is applied

Spec: `specs/220.cancel-answer-erases-the-fill-the-stream-already-reported.md`.

## Solution

In `MainBot.cancelOrderOnExchange`, success branch:

1. Before the field copy, take the largest executed quantity already known for
   the order (§4.1). The candidates are the row handed in (the pre-cancel
   snapshot) and the map's current row (the stream report, if it landed while
   the cancel was in flight). Keep its `cummulativeQuoteQty` and `price` with
   it.
2. After the copy, the `026` promotion guard and `convertOrderExecutedQty`,
   if that quantity is larger than the answer's, put it back on the row
   (§4.1, §4.3). Both candidates are in map units already, so this step comes
   after the conversion.
3. Delete-on-zero also requires that the reconciled row carries no fill
   (§4.2).

JIT tier 0: an inline guard in the method that owns the copy. There is no
decision logic here worth extracting.

## Blast Radius

- **Call sites.** Every cancel goes through this method: grid rebuilds, DCA/TP
  re-sizes, deal closes and teardown. The only change is in the case where the
  answer states less than the bot already held. In that case the row now ends
  as it would have if the stream report had arrived after the answer: CANCELED
  with its fill, and kept in the map. That state is already produced in prod
  whenever the two reports arrive in the other order. Status, promotion and
  the DB write path are unchanged. `updateOrderOnDb` stays unforced, so a
  terminal Mongo row is still never overwritten.
- **`core/` submodule.** Shared main-app-sh code. The parent pointer needs a
  bump, and parent and core versions are both bumped.
- **Contracts.** None. No GraphQL, REST, socket, queue or schema shape is
  touched. The `bot update` emit carries the same fields.
- **Specs that must stay green.** `026` (`resizeCancelPromotion.spec.ts`),
  `059`/`217` (the settle and reposition specs), `082` (`cancelBatch.spec.ts`),
  `154`/`155` (`cancelNoExchangeOrderId`, `cancelDuringPlacement`), and the
  combo cancel ledger and rested specs. The full suite was run.
