## Solution

1. **`comboHelper.handleOrderErrors` override (§1.1.1, §1.1.5).** Calls the
   inherited reporter unchanged. Then it hands the refusal to
   `settleFlatDealAfterGridRefusal` without awaiting it, because the refusal
   arrives from inside `placeOrders`, which holds the `${botId}${dealId}` lock
   that `closeDealById` also takes. The settle step returns at once unless all
   of these hold: futures, a `dealGrid` order on the closing side,
   `isPositionAlreadyClosedReason(reason)`, the deal `open`, not `closeBySl`,
   and not `isDealClosing`.
2. **Probe (§1.1.1, §1.1.3, §1.1.4).** Reuses spec 005's
   `reconcileDealPosition`: the `positionProbeBackoff` window, the
   `unavailable ≠ empty` verdict, two agreeing readings, the clear-on-seen
   rule, and the user-facing deal event. A per-deal in-memory
   `flatProbeRunning` set keeps a burst of refusals from taking both readings
   at once.
3. **Settle (§1.1.2).** `reconcileDealPosition` gains an optional `settle`
   callback. With none, it calls `closeDeal` as before, so the DCA call sites
   are unchanged. Combo passes the base-minigrid close:
   `closeBySl = true` and `closeDealById(closeByMarket, …, base)`. That close
   cancels the deal's orders. Its refused market close then reaches the
   existing position-already-closed branch, which books the deal closed with
   no order.

JIT tier: Tier 0. Two members of `dcaHelper` widen from `private` to
`protected`, and one optional parameter is added. The rest is a guard and a
reuse at a new call site.

## Blast Radius

- Call sites: every `handleOrderErrors` call on a combo bot. Only refusals
  that pass the §1.1.5 gate do more than they did before: a closing-side grid
  order with a position-already-closed reason. DCA, grid and hedge bots built
  on the DCA helper are untouched. `reconcileDealPosition`'s two DCA callers
  pass no `settle`.
- `core/` (main-app-sh): public and shared. The main-app pointer needs a bump.
  No GraphQL, socket, queue or schema change.
- Venue cost: at most one positions call per deal per probe window
  (5 min → 60 min), and only while that refusal keeps happening.
- Order-moving: a deal closes only after two answered, agreeing readings that
  the venue holds no position on the deal's side for the symbol. On a one-way
  account, any non-zero position vetoes the close. Unchanged: the virtual fills
  of hidden safety levels, the ladder generation, and grid placement while a
  position exists.
- Tests that must stay green: `comboCloseDuringReload.spec.ts`,
  `dca/positionReconcile.spec.ts`,
  `dca/positionFlatDirectionRefusal.harness.spec.ts`, and the rest of
  `npm test`.
