# 113 — plan

Spec: `specs/113.a-bitget-spot-market-buy-is-funded-at-last-trade.md`.

## Solution

1. New pure module `src/bot/bitgetMarketBuyAmount.ts`:
   `bitgetSpotMarketBuyQty(qty, step)` returns the base quantity the quote
   amount is funded for, `qty + step / 2` (spec §3). A missing or non-positive
   step returns `qty` unchanged. Tier 2 (pure) — no imports.
2. `MainBot.sendOrderToExchange`, the Bybit/Coinbase/Bitget MARKET BUY
   conversion: when `this.data.exchange === ExchangeEnum.bitget`, multiply the
   price by `bitgetSpotMarketBuyQty(quantity, ed.baseAsset.step)` instead of
   the raw quantity. Rounding (to nearest, `min(quotePrecision, 6)`) is
   unchanged. Bybit and Coinbase keep `quantity × price`.
3. `buyRemainder`'s `GA-BR` top-up needs no change of its own: it is sent via
   `sendGridToExchange → sendOrderToExchange` and passes through step 2.

The spec left open best-ask vs. buffer; the buffer is chosen because the
exchange chain has no order-book route (spec §3).

JIT tier: 2 for the new decision (a pure function); the surrounding
`sendOrderToExchange` stays as is (Tier 0 — not on the causal path beyond the
one expression).

## Blast Radius

1. Call sites: the conversion is a single expression in `sendOrderToExchange`;
   every MARKET BUY on every bot type reaches the venue through it. Guarded to
   `ExchangeEnum.bitget` (spot) — `bitgetUsdm`/`bitgetCoinm` and every other
   venue do not enter the new branch; limit orders and sells never enter the
   block at all.
2. `core/` submodule (main-app-sh) — shared with self-hosted. Parent pointer
   must be bumped. No type, schema, GraphQL, socket or queue shape changes.
3. Money: a Bitget spot market buy reserves up to half a base step more quote.
   Bitget fills (and spends) only `⌊amount ÷ fill⌋` on the step, so the fill is
   `q` across the §3 band; an ask more than `step/2q` BELOW the sizing price can
   fill `q + step` (today that already happens at `step/q`).
4. Other tests on this path: `zeroSizeOrderRefusal.spec.ts` §4.2 pins the ZEC
   amount at `1.52915` (= `0.001 × 1529.15`). That assertion pinned the scale
   cap, not the pricing; its expected value moves to the half-step-funded
   amount at the same 5-decimal scale. Full core suite must stay green.
