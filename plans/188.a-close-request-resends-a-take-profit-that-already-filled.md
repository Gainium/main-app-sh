## Decisions the spec left open

- **Do not await `handleUnknownOrder` in `cancelAllOrder`.** It would not
  sequence `closeDeal` (fire-and-forget inside `processFilledOrder`), and
  `cancelAllOrder` also runs from `rebuildDealOrders`/`processDealClose`, where
  blocking on `processFilledOrder`'s `${botId}process${symbol}${dealId}` key is
  not provably deadlock-free (the mutex is not re-entrant).
- **Reuse `dealClosedByFilledTp`** (the `placeOrders` predicate) rather than a
  new "did the fill close it" rule; it already excludes multi-TP/SL targets,
  under-filled take-profits and simple terminal deals.
- The FILLED-TP predicate is only consulted when THIS request's sweep found
  the fill, so a deal carrying an older FILLED take-profit row is closed as
  before.
- JIT tier 0: the guard is a small named method beside its only caller; the
  1k-line `closeDealById` is otherwise untouched.
